# Stanford MSEE

# Program Overview
Stanford is one of the CS Big Four, with a top overall ranking, located in California -- proximity is a huge advantage for job hunting.
Being a TA covers tuition and pays $3k/month. Track selection is very flexible -- you can build an entirely software-focused course schedule, such as CS229 or CS 245 Database Systems Principles. When you apply, you'll fill out a questionnaire to choose your track, with options including Machine Learning, Software Systems, Architecture, and more.
Admission-wise, Tsinghua admits 5 or fewer per year for mainland China, and Peking/Fudan/SJTU/Zhejiang each admit at most 2. Applying to Stanford requires a TOEFL score; if your TOEFL is below 109, you'll need to take a language class. IELTS is not accepted. Joint-program (Chinese-foreign cooperative) students can apply for a language requirement waiver. Stanford's course selection and graduation tracks are very flexible, so switching to CS is easy. There are also many other courses available, such as tennis, violin, or 1-credit language courses. CS undergrads coming into MSEE can take almost entirely CS courses -- very flexible.

Joint-program students can waive language requirements. Admission is more accessible than MSCS.

# Admission Preferences

This program is arguably the ceiling for mainland China undergrads applying to MS programs in the US CS space. It's basically a lottery school for every applicant. Many apply each year, very few are admitted. Getting into Stanford comes down to fate.

Here's a summary I posted on Open CS: Female, high GPA (above 90 is fine, not strictly required), AI publications (must have actual top conference output), overseas connections (strong overseas recommendation letters add a lot -- for example, a very strong recommendation from Monash or ETH could get you into Stanford).
For joint-program (Chinese-foreign cooperative) students, there's a significant boost. For example, UESTC's Glasgow College produces multiple Stanford MSEE admits every year -- it's suspected that MSEE does targeted recruitment from the Glasgow College. The MSCS admission threshold is higher than MSEE. For MSEE, US undergrads from top 30 schools with high GPAs have a chance. For MSCS, you need to check whether alumni from your school have been admitted in previous years -- if not, don't force it. Spots are allocated by school.

MSEE admitted around 120 students total; MSCS admitted 150. (MSEE is more friendly to mainland China undergrads. Even CS applicants can choose the AI track. There are data points showing that mid-tier 985 universities can get admitted. MSCS is more friendly to international undergrads, but you need to be from a target school, such as Tsinghua CS, UT, UBC, Georgia Tech, etc.)

## What the course load actually feels like

MSEE can accommodate a heavily CS-oriented schedule, but the degree plan takes some work. The 45 units include depth, breadth and other coursework requirements; an interesting CS course does not automatically fit the plan. For a software / hardware focus, list the courses you want and discuss any petitions with your adviser before relying on them for graduation.

### Scheduling around recruiting

Assessment format matters when applications and interviews are taking up your week. A course with weekly assignments and exams creates a different schedule from one built around a final project. If you already work in NLP / ML, a familiar project topic can reduce the time spent learning new tools and reading background material. Someone moving from EE into programming should allow more time than a classmate with that research experience.

Course experiences from the Fall 2023 cohort put CS224V, CS329T, CS329A and MS&E338 in a relatively lighter group, with CS229S, CS349D, CS349H, CS375 and CS238 requiring somewhat more work. A few details are more useful than an easy-course label: CS349D and CS375 had attendance requirements, CS224V occasionally required attendance, CS349H assignments were increasing, and CS238 quizzes still needed preparation. These observations concern 2023–2025; a different instructor or assessment format can change the workload.

### Leaving time for what you came to do

For research, courses around a common topic can help you build relevant knowledge. For recruiting, avoid putting several major course projects in the same busy interview period. Group projects are especially hard to schedule entirely on your own: your free hours may not match your teammates'. Put project deadlines and interview preparation on the same calendar rather than judging the term only by the number of courses.

The schedule does not have to be equally intense every quarter. Take demanding foundations when you can give them attention, then choose a lighter combination during recruiting. That is a practical use of MSEE's flexibility.

### Internships and CPT: October 2026 update

**An internship is not a universal MSEE graduation requirement, and non-required CPT is currently paused.** The EE290 series offers internship credit, which does not make an internship compulsory for the degree. As of October 11, 2026, Bechtel still states that CPT not required for degree completion is paused. The older approach of finding a summer internship and registering for an internship course should not be treated as an available authorization route.
