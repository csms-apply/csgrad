# UT ECE (SES)

One-sentence summary: A lottery ticket -- there's really no discernible pattern, but highly recommended for job hunting.

## Admission Preferences

If you have a strong GPA or solid research in software engineering, it's worth applying. Annual admission is approximately 5-10 students.

2. UT Austin ECE undergrad, GPA 3.8
3. UT Austin ECE undergrad, GPA 3.98 (top 10%)
4. ACSES track, GPA 3.8, received RA offer
5. ICS track, IIT undergrad, GPA 4.0
6. ACSES track, GPA 3.99, three years work experience

## Internship & Delayed Graduation

> **October 11, 2026 update:** The earlier internship arrangements below are preserved. Internships are not required for the ECE degree, and published CPT rules have tightened; these experiences do not establish current eligibility for two US internships. See the update at the end.

You can extend to 2.5 years, giving you two opportunities for US internships, which is excellent, and it's also very affordable. The school supports semester-long internships (e.g., Snowflake, TikTok, Amazon, Tesla), though not many companies open co-op positions. Free campus rides after 11 PM.

## TA/RA Opportunities
Among the cases around me, everyone who wanted a TA/RA position got one by at least the second semester. Getting one right after enrollment is also quite common (for example, a friend found a TA position in the math department). So the cost-effectiveness is truly maxed out. The ability to extend, do semester internships, and transfer to PhD gives the entire program great flexibility. Tuition is fully waived, and insurance is also covered (plus you can enjoy staff dental and vision insurance benefits for free).

## Tuition
SES tuition + insurance per semester is just over $11,000 (typically for 9 credit hours per semester), which is extremely affordable. There are also plenty of TA and RA positions that can waive your tuition + provide around $2,000/month in salary, essentially making it free to attend. My living expenses will most likely stay under $2,000/month.
Total cost for the entire program is at most around $30k.

## PhD Transfer

Very easy to transfer -- as long as a professor wants you, you're in. You can also select the MS/PhD option when applying.

## Job Outcomes & Data Points

Job prospects are also very favorable, mainly because you can extend graduation, there's co-op available, you can do full-time internships during the semester, and peer pressure is virtually nonexistent. The ECE SES track has fewer than ten students per cohort. In 23 Fall summer, 7 students had nearly a 100% landing rate.

1. SJTU IS undergrad, had ByteDance, Cisco, and Synopsys internships, landed Apple new grad
2. UT Austin ECE undergrad, GPA 3.98 (top 10%), landed AMD new grad
3. UT Austin ECE bachelor’s, two LinkedIn internships before enrollment, Databricks SWE intern while studying SES in 2026.

## Courses

SES is very CS-oriented. ECE has its own systems courses, and students can also take CS courses, although they have lower priority and may need to wait for a seat. There are few restrictions, but the selection of demanding courses in a given semester is limited.

In Spring 2022, EE461S OS used OSTEP, with shell and PintOS labs covering user programs, virtual memory and file systems. EE460N Computer Architecture took more time: five homework assignments, three exams and six labs, building an LC-3b assembler and simulator through to pipelining. Taking both while doing research leaves a semester packed with assignments. Good systems training, substantial workload.

## Assistantships and recruiting

There is a 2022–2023 case of three consecutive semesters with tuition covered through RA/TA appointments, including TA work in CS. The $3,200 monthly pay in that case depended on doctoral status; it was not a standard MS package.

Career fairs are no magic shortcut. The 2022 ECE fair was largely hardware-focused, while the CS fair included Bloomberg and Datadog. An employer attending does not mean an interview will follow. Software recruiting still takes independent applications. SES does give students room to choose lighter courses when recruiting is the priority.

## Life

Austin summers are hot, and Chinese food and entertainment options were fairly limited in 2022–2023. Plenty of tech companies, but less going on than in New York or the Bay Area. A quieter routine centered on classes and recruiting is an easier fit here.

## CPT — updated October 11, 2026

The old idea that registering for an internship course is enough no longer works. Texas Global requires practical training to be necessary for every student's degree completion, while ECE explicitly says internships are not a degree requirement. Taking x97M does not establish current CPT eligibility. No announcement was found making SES internships mandatory or confirming a general resumption; ECE and ISSS must review individual applications.
