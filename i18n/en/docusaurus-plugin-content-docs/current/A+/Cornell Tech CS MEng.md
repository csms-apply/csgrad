# Cornell Tech CS MEng

## Program Overview

The degree is Cornell University's Master of Engineering in Computer Science, taught on the Cornell Tech campus in New York City (Roosevelt Island). It is a separate program from the CS MEng on the main Ithaca campus, with a separate application system.

- Length: 1 year, fall entry only, classes start in mid-to-late August
- Credits: 30
- STEM: yes, so you can use OPT after graduation
- There is also a two-year part-time version with the same curriculum, but it does not offer F-1/J-1 visa sponsorship
- Cost for the 2026-27 academic year: tuition $73,946, health insurance $3,751–4,092, NYC Health Fee $366, student activity fee $110; the site also gives a living-cost estimate of $40,746 per year. The site does not give a total; adding these up comes to roughly $119,000 for the year.

## Courses and Course Selection

Studio depends heavily on the team. Students value the hands-on Machine Learning Engineering work, while Product Studio collaboration can be uneven. The one-year schedule also leaves a tight window for coursework and job searching.

The 2026-27 degree requirements add up to 30 credits:

- Technical courses, 18 credits: 5 CS courses (15 credits), plus a 3-credit technical elective (from CS, ORIE, ECE or INFO)
- Studio, 8 credits: TECH 5900 Product Studio (4 credits); one of TECH 5910 Startup Studio, 5920 BigCo Studio or 5930 PiTech Impact Studio (3 credits); plus a 1-credit Studio elective
- General electives, 4 credits: any course from CS, DESIGN, ECE, ORIE, INFO, LAW, NBAY, TECH or TECHIE

The main Studio courses carry a B-or-higher grade requirement, and at least 28 credits must be taken for a letter grade. Full-time students are advised to take about 15 credits per semester; going above 18 needs the Program Director's approval.

All master's students take Product Studio in their first fall, in teams with business, law, design and other students, building solutions for partner organizations under the mentorship of NYC industry advisors. In the final spring you pick one of three: Startup Studio, where you form your own team, build a startup and pitch it to investors; BigCo Studio, where you build a new product with a large company such as JP Morgan Chase, Google or Samsung; or PiTech, which focuses on the public good. So 8 of the 30 credits are not traditional CS courses, which matters if you only want technical courses.

Technical areas listed on the program site include Applied Machine Learning, Algorithms and Data Structures for Applications, Distributed Systems, Machine Learning Engineering, NLP, Computer Vision, Cryptography, Cybersecurity, Blockchains & Cryptocurrencies and HCI.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The official FAQ says CPT is only for F-1 students while they are pursuing their degree, and since most programs are one year long, CPT is not the appropriate work authorization; the school also generally discourages full-time students from working during the academic year. Neither the program page nor the degree requirements mention any internship or co-op component. In practice, plan on one year of study followed by a full-time job search on OPT.

## Job Search and Location

The campus is on Roosevelt Island in New York City. The program page lists graduate employers such as Google, Amazon, Palantir, Bloomberg, Visa, Microsoft, TikTok and Scale AI, and states a 2025 median salary of $140,000, without saying which class or how many graduates it covers.

## Application Requirements (Fall 2027)

- Deadlines (5:00 PM EST): Priority December 1, 2026; Round 2 January 26, 2027; Rolling April 1, 2027. The FAQ encourages international students to apply no later than Round 2 to leave time for visa processing. Scholarships are limited and need no separate application; if you want to be considered, aim for the Priority deadline
- GRE: not required and not accepted
- English: TOEFL 100 overall or IELTS 7.0 overall band; MyBestScore is not accepted; official scores are not needed when you apply but must be reported after admission; exemptions exist, for example for applicants who will hold a U.S. bachelor's degree
- Recommendation letters: 2, ideally one academic and one professional, with an optional third
- Other materials: a resume of up to two pages, a personal statement of up to 750 words (choose one of two prompts), a 3-minute video essay and transcripts. Degrees from outside the U.S. and Canada need a course-by-course evaluation from WES or a similar evaluator before the application is reviewed (the FAQ makes an exception for institutions with a joint venture with a U.S. institution). The application fee is $95
- You can apply to only one Cornell Tech master's program per cycle, but you may also apply to programs on the Ithaca campus
