# NWU MSAI

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence. It belongs to Northwestern's McCormick School of Engineering, with classes on the Evanston campus. It is full-time with daytime classes; there is no online or part-time option, and no thesis.

The traditional MSAI takes 15 months: 4 quarters of classes with an optional summer internship in between. Admission is for fall only, classes start in September, and each cohort has about 45 students. There is also a 12-month MSAI+X track that only admits people who already hold an MD, PhD or JD; everything below is about the traditional MSAI.

The admissions FAQ states that MSAI is considered a STEM program and qualifies international students for the OPT extension.

Tuition for the 2026-27 academic year is $23,662 per full-time quarter, charged for 4 quarters (fall, winter and spring of the first year, plus fall of the second year). The summer has no tuition, so tuition totals about $94,648. The program estimates the total cost of the degree at about $125,000, with on-campus living costs of roughly $24,000 a year. The program does not directly award scholarships or fellowships.

On the official student body profile, 76% of the 2024 cohort came from engineering or CS, average work experience was 2.4 years, and 24% had no work experience; in the 2022 and 2023 cohorts, over 40% had no work experience.

The relatively fixed schedule lets a cohort move through AI foundations, projects and the capstone together, but leaves less room to build your own course mix. Check the elective restrictions if you want extensive cross-department study.

## Courses and Course Selection

The course plan is mostly fixed:

- First fall: 348 Intro to AI, 349 Machine Learning, 339 Data Science, plus half-unit 431 Frameworks for AI and 495 Topics
- Winter: 371 Representation, Reasoning, and Language, 437 Deep Learning, 330 Human Computer Interaction, with another half unit each of 431 and 495
- Spring: 337 NLP, 490 Practicum (a team project on an open-ended AI problem), and 2 electives
- Summer: optional internship
- Second fall: 490 Industry Capstone (teams of 4–5 MSAI and MBAi students working on a real problem posed by a partner company) and 3 electives; the program highly recommends 448 Law and the Governance of AI

That leaves only 5 electives in the whole program. The official Course Offerings page lists what is available from Fall 2026 to Spring 2027. Besides MSAI's own topics courses such as Computer Vision and Generative AI, it includes COMP_SCI 336 algorithms, 339 databases, ELEC_ENG 432 Advanced Computer Vision, and statistics and optimization courses in IEMS. Non-MSAI courses need permission from the department or instructor. The program FAQ says students generally cannot take courses beyond the requirements, and Northwestern does not allow auditing.

The capstone involves working with teammates from different backgrounds, explaining technical choices and thinking about user needs.

## Internships / CPT

According to the program FAQ, a summer internship is highly recommended but not required, and the program does not guarantee one; you find your own. There is no tuition for an internship summer, but in the rare case a company requires academic credit for the internship, you may owe the internship course fees.

The official pages do not describe the CPT process, so confirm it with the school after admission.

## Jobs and Location

The program FAQ lists alumni roles as AI engineer, SWE, data scientist, data analyst and consultant, with employers including Amazon, Deloitte, Meta, Goldman Sachs, Google, Microsoft, Tesla and TikTok. Most graduates go into industry, and some go on to PhD programs. The program does not publish employment rates or salaries.

The school is in Evanston, and the program home page lists easy access to Chicago's STEM industries as one of its selling points.

## Application Requirements

The application opens on September 15 each year for entry the following fall. The requirements below are from the official pages as checked in October 2026; the pages do not name a year, so read them as the Fall 2027 cycle:

- Deadline: recommended by December 15, final deadline April 15. The application stays open until seats fill, and later applications may not get full consideration if the class is full. Applications in by December 15 get decisions in mid-January
- Application fee: none
- GRE: optional, though the admissions FAQ recommends submitting it
- English: TOEFL iBT 100 or IELTS 7.0, taken within two years of enrollment; a degree from an institution that teaches in English can waive this, subject to approval
- Recommendation letters: 3, from undergraduate faculty or current employers/managers
- Background: a bachelor's in CS or a related field, and you should already know how to program; the admissions page says strong preference goes to applicants with at least two years of related work experience
- Essays: the Personal Statement has two parts. In up to 500 words, cover why MSAI, what you will bring to classmates and your career goals; in up to 300 words, describe a significant problem AI will help overcome in the next five years. You can also upload an optional video of up to 2 minutes and link to GitHub or other work
- Interview: only applicants in final consideration may be contacted; international applicants interview over Zoom

## Admission Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad currently has only 1 DP for this program: Fall 2025, Waitlist.

The applicant was a graduating CS student from a Sino-foreign joint institute in mainland China, with a GPA above 93/100, TOEFL 100+, GRE 320+, research experience in China and abroad, top-venue papers under review, and three strong recommendation letters.

With just 1 DP, this says little.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [NWU MSCS](../B+/NWU%20MSCS.md): the CS master's at the same school. The program FAQ says MSCS covers CS more broadly, while MSAI is more specialized
- Northwestern also has an MS in Machine Learning and Data Science (MLDS). The FAQ says MLDS focuses on predictive, descriptive and prescriptive analytics, while MSAI is about fitting analytical methods into larger AI systems that include symbolic reasoning
- [Duke AIPI](../B+/Duke%20AIPI.md), [CMU MSAII](../S/CMU%20MSAII.md), [CMU MSAIE](../A/CMU%20MSAIE.md), [BU MSAI](../B/BU%20MSAI.md): other AI-focused master's programs to compare on courses, length and tuition
