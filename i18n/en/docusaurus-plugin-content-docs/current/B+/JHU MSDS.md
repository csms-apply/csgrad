# JHU MSDS

## Program Overview

This is the data science master's at Johns Hopkins. The degree is the Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) in Data Science, offered by the Whiting School of Engineering and run by the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics (AMS). The catalogue describes it as a full-time, on-campus program, and the campus is in Baltimore. JHU also has an online Data Science MS through Engineering for Professionals; that is a separate program, so don't mix them up.

- Length: the catalogue says the program is designed for 3 full-time semesters, and the program page says students usually finish in 3 to 4 semesters
- Start terms: both fall and spring
- STEM: the department FAQ says all AMS master's programs are STEM approved; the CIP code for Data Science is 27.05.01
- Cost: WSE full-time graduate tuition for 2026-27 is $68,670 per year ($34,335 per semester), so tuition for 3 semesters comes to about $103,005. The same page's 2026-27 budget for off-campus housing, food, books, transportation, personal expenses and health insurance adds up to $27,597 per year, plus a $500 matriculation fee
- Funding: the department offers only limited partial support to some master's students. After you submit your application you are emailed the WSE master's financial assistance form, and you need to answer "Yes" in the Additional Questions section of the application

## Courses and Course Selection

Students who start in Fall 2025 or later choose one of two options:

- Capstone Option: intro course + 4 core courses + 3 electives + a 6-credit capstone
- Course-Only Option: intro course + 4 core courses + 5 electives

The intro course is Introduction to Data Science (EN.553.636) or Machine Learning (EN.601.675), and it has to be taken in the first semester. For the core you pick one course in each of four areas, Statistics, Machine Learning, Optimization and Computing, for example Applied Statistics & Data Analysis I, Deep Learning, Optimization for Data Science or Intro Algorithms. The elective list is broad: CS courses such as NLP, Computer Vision and Databases are on it, along with more applied courses like Medical Image Analysis and Investment Science.

The capstone (EN.553.806) is a research project. You first find a JHU faculty member as your supervisor and submit a proposal, and you can only register once the supervisor and the department's Internal Oversight Committee approve it. You can take all 6 credits in one semester or split them into 3 credits over each of two semesters, and in your final semester you present a poster and hand in a report.

You also have to pass the academic ethics course (EN.500.603, with automatic enrollment in your first semester), the online Responsible Conduct of Research course, an approved online data ethics course (for example the one on Coursera) and the Communication Skills Practicum.

Each course needs a B- or better to count, with one C/C+ allowed toward the requirements, and the department does not accept courses or credits transferred from other schools.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

- Industry Project Practicum: a course described on the program page in which students work in small teams on projects proposed and supervised by industry staff
- Amazon mentorship: the program page says students are paired with R&D scientists or engineers at Amazon and AWS
- Paid summer internship at APL: Data Science is one of the participating programs in the Hopkins-APL Master's Scholars program, but the summer 2027 round requires U.S. citizenship and the ability to obtain a security clearance, so international students cannot apply
- The degree has no required internship, and neither the program page nor the catalogue mentions co-op. Whether a summer internship can be done on CPT depends on JHU OIS rules at the time you enroll

## Jobs and Location

The program page lists alumni employers including Amazon, Meta, Accenture, American Express, Boeing, Johnson & Johnson and Nasdaq, and names software, management consulting, financial services, national security and sustainable energy among the fields graduates go into. It does not publish an employment rate.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines: January 15, 2027 for Fall 2027 and September 15, 2026 for Spring 2027
- GRE: not required for Spring 2027 or Fall 2027
- Application fee: the program page says there is no application fee, while the department FAQ only says master's applicants are not charged for Spring 2026 and Fall 2026, so go by what the application system shows
- English: the WSE admissions page gives preferred minimums of TOEFL 100 (5.0 on the new 1–6 scale), IELTS 7 and Duolingo 135, and says they are recommended but not required; the AMS Data Science section does not list its own scores. Scores are valid for two years
- Materials: statement of purpose, supplementary information, transcripts and 3 recommendation letters
- Background: ideally multivariable calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability (preferably with a statistics course), programming (C++ or Python) and at least two proof-writing courses. The department says it puts the most weight on math coursework, GPA and recommendation letters
- The department prefers that applicants apply to only one AMS program; applying to programs in other JHU departments at the same time is fine
- Official numbers: for Fall 2026 the DS MSE received 1,162 applications and admitted 659, an admit rate of about 57%

- No admits or waitlists on record

The applicant studied CS in a Sino-foreign joint program at a mainland Chinese university, with a GPA of about 87/100 (around the top 15%), TOEFL 99 and GRE 326 (Q170), one research experience and one algorithm internship in China, and no publications. The notes say the program seems to prefer math and statistics backgrounds, which matches the department's emphasis on math coursework.

One data point says nothing about where the admission bar is.
