# WUSTL MSCS

## Program Overview

This is the Master of Science in Computer Science at Washington University in St. Louis (WashU), offered by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering in the McKelvey School of Engineering, in St. Louis, Missouri. The department also offers MS degrees in Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity Engineering and AI Engineering, plus an MEng in CSE.

- Units: 30. The program does not state a length; the department handbook defines full-time as at least 9 units per semester and does not recommend more than 12, so at 9–12 units per semester it takes 3 to 4 semesters
- Start terms: both fall and spring
- Cost: McKelvey's 2026-27 full-time rate (9–21 units) is $34,595 per semester, or $69,190 per year. The school's estimated annual cost of attendance is $99,120 (tuition, fees, housing, food, books, transportation and personal expenses), with the student health plan at $2,923 on top
- Funding: the tuition page says master's students are expected to be self-supporting. There are two limited forms of support that need no application and depend on faculty funding: a one-semester Master's Fellowship (15% tuition plus a living stipend) and a two-year MS Research Assistantship (full tuition plus a living stipend)
- STEM: neither the program page nor the bulletin lists a CIP code. OISS says STEM OPT eligibility depends on the CIP code in the Major field on page 1 of your I-20, and OISS cannot change that code, so if it matters, ask the department before enrolling

You can complete the degree through coursework alone, a project or a thesis. If you want research on your résumé, explore faculty interests and capstone requirements early, and budget for self-funding because research funding is limited.

## Courses and Course Selection

The prerequisites are CSE 1301 (Intro to CS) and CSE 2407 (Data Structures and Algorithms) or equivalents. If your transcript doesn't show them, you can take a placement test or make them up after enrolling; those courses don't count toward the degree.

Requirements from the 2026-27 bulletin:

- At least 18 of the 30 units must be CSE courses at the 5000 level or above, including 3 breadth courses: one Theory (T), one Software Systems (S), and one of either Machines (M) or Applications (A)
- The remaining 12 units are electives at the 4000 or 5000 level
- Out-of-department courses are normally capped at 6 units and count only as electives; independent study does not count
- Each course needs a C- or better, and the cumulative GPA must be at least 2.70

If you want research, you can take CSE 7998 Master's Capstone as a 3-unit project, a 6-unit project or a thesis; a successful thesis defense waives the breadth requirement. These units don't count toward the 30 until you pass the defense.

Graduate courses in the 2026-27 catalog include CSE 5107 Machine Learning, 5610 Large Language Models, 5202 Advanced Operating Systems, 5300 Database Management Systems, 5401 Advanced Algorithms and 5701 Network Security. Which ones count as T/S/M/A is set by the list on the department's Resources for Current Master's Students page.

Two more rules from the handbook: international students must be full-time every semester, though those with fewer than 9 units left can apply to OISS for a reduced course load in their final semester; and once you finish all degree requirements you must graduate that semester, with no extending by taking extra courses or a co-op.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

McKelvey runs an Engineering Co-op: full-time paid work for at least 16 weeks aligned with a fall or spring semester, open after two semesters at WashU; summer-only positions don't qualify. The co-op semester counts as full-time enrollment but earns no credit, and it may delay graduation.

The Co-op page currently carries a notice: SEVP issued CPT guidance on August 12 and August 24 requiring CPT to be an "integral and required" part of the curriculum. McKelvey does not currently require practical training across its degree programs, so under current guidance international PhD students may be eligible for co-op and domestic students at all degree levels remain eligible. International master's students are not among the groups the page lists.

OISS's CPT page likewise says CPT is only for work that fulfills a graduation requirement. Under McKelvey it lists only PhD programs, and it says a program not listed may not have a curricular practical training requirement, in which case you are not eligible for CPT. The MSCS degree requirements include no internship, so for work authorization for a summer internship, ask OISS directly once you enroll.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in St. Louis. The 2026-27 bulletin, describing the department's master's programs, says many graduates take positions at companies like Boeing, Google and Microsoft; the official pages give no MSCS employment statistics.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines: for full-time master's programs, the fall deadline is March 1 with decisions by March 31; early decision closes December 31 with decisions by January 31, and requires a complete application, payment and official scores by the deadline. The spring deadline is October 1. The page shows no year; for Fall 2027 that works out to December 31, 2026 (ED) and March 1, 2027
- Application fee: $75, waived if you attend an eligible admissions info session
- GRE: optional for full-time master's programs; the page says submitting GRE scores will neither help nor hurt your chances
- English: TOEFL iBT requires 90 or above on the old scale; the new-scale figure is ambiguous (the cell reads "5.0 overall (new scale) OR 4.5 ≥ 90 overall (old scale)"), so ask admissions if unsure. IELTS 6.5, Duolingo 125. Scores must be valid through the deadline and arrive by it
- Recommendations: 3 letters, accepted only through the online application system
- Documents: a 1–2 page statement of purpose, a CV and transcripts; current students can submit transcripts showing work completed so far

## Admissions Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has 6 data points for WUSTL MSCS:

- Fall 2023: 2 admits, 2 rejects
- Fall 2024: 1 admit
- Fall 2025: 1 admit
- No Fall 2026 data yet

None of the 6 were marked as funded. Admits were notified between mid-January and March 19, and the two rejects came around March 20.

Of the 4 admits, 3 had bachelor's degrees from mainland China (one each from a "Huawu" top-five, a 985 and a 211 university) and 1 from Canada; two majored in CS and two in AI. Two had GPAs of 3.31–3.48 on a 4-point scale and two had 3.70–3.75 on a 4.3-point scale. The 3 who reported the GRE scored 322–335, with TOEFL 101–105. All 3 mainland admits had research and internship experience, 2 had research abroad and 2 had publications; the Canadian admit had one internship abroad and reported no test scores.

Both rejects were for Fall 2023, both from mainland universities (a "Huawu" top-five and a 985), majoring in AI and math/statistics. Both reported a GPA rank around 40%, GRE 322–324, and English scores of TOEFL 105 and IELTS 8 respectively; one had three research experiences and an exchange abroad.

All 4 applicants from 2023 submitted the GRE, but it is now optional for full-time master's programs. The sample is very small, so treat it as a rough picture only.
