# Duke Kunshan ECE

## Program Overview

The degree is Duke University's Master of Engineering in Electrical and Computer Engineering (MEng), offered by Duke together with Duke Kunshan University (DKU). The program site calls it the Duke @ DKU MEng Program, and Duke's 2026–27 Pratt Professional Programs Bulletin lists it as K-EGR-ECE. You spend the first year on the DKU campus in Kunshan, Jiangsu, and the second year at Duke's Pratt School of Engineering in Durham, North Carolina. Graduates receive a Duke MEng.

The program takes two years: 4 semesters, 10 courses, 30 credits, with a required internship in the summer between the two years. Students start in the fall.

STEM: notes from Duke Visa Services' Spring 2022 OPT information session for MEM/MEng students say that all Duke MEng programs have STEM-related CIP codes and are eligible for the 24-month STEM OPT extension. The DKU site says students are eligible for OPT during their second year at Duke. The CIP code on your I-20 is what counts.

Costs, from the program's AY 2026–27 estimates:

- Year 1 at DKU: tuition and fees (including health insurance) are ¥210,800 for PRC students and $73,433 for international students; with books and living expenses, the full-year estimates are ¥280,737 and $85,968
- Year 2 at Duke: tuition and fees (including health insurance) are $77,294 and the full-year estimate is $103,260, the same for PRC and international students

A footnote on the cost page says DKU tuition for the 2027 intake will be RMB 220,000.

Accepting an offer requires a non-refundable deposit of 45,000 CNY (6,200 USD), applied to tuition or housing. There is no separate scholarship application: every admitted student is considered automatically, awards appear in the admission letter, and the site does not publish amounts. Students from mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan can also submit family financial information after admission to apply for need-based aid, and there is a research scholarship that funds new students to do summer research with ECE faculty before the program starts.

The first year is in Kunshan and the second in Durham. Consider where you want to build connections and arrange your summer internship, alongside the degree and total cost.

Studying in both Kunshan and Durham affects coursework, housing and internship planning. Review the arrangements at both locations alongside your own goals.

## Courses and Course Selection

There are two tracks: Software Engineering and Machine Learning & AI. The 10 courses are 8 technical courses plus 2 industry preparation courses, 3 credits each.

In year 1 at DKU you take 4 track core courses:

- Software Engineering: Programming, Data Structures, and Algorithms in C++; Fundamentals of Computer Systems and Engineering; Systems Programming and Engineering; Software Engineering
- ML & AI: Programming and Data Structure for Machine Learning; Mathematics for Machine Learning; Practical Machine Learning; Deep Learning

In year 2 at Duke you choose 4 technical electives. The site lists:

- Software Engineering: Compiler Construction, Probability for Electrical and Computer Engineers, Human-Centered Computing, Mobile Application Development, Edge Computing
- ML & AI: Signal Detection & Extraction Theory, Image and Video Processing, Advanced Computer Network, Performance Optimization & Parallelism, Theory & Practice of Algorithms

The two industry preparation courses are the same for both tracks: Management of High-Tech Industries and Business Fundamentals for Engineers. There are also 0-credit language and professional training courses; students take the Duolingo English Test at the start of year 1, a Conversation score of 115+ waives the Speaking course and a Literacy score of 140+ waives the Writing course, and failing any DKU English course ends your enrollment in the program. Students entering in Fall 2024 or later must attend the weekly ECE Master Success Seminar (0 credits, no tuition). Before graduating, your advisor and the ECE DGS must approve your course list through the Program of Study form, and the site notes that courses are subject to change.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The summer after year 1 is a required internship worth 0 credits. The curriculum page says it is in China; the careers page says in China or your home country. Year 2 includes a summer internship evaluation. Internship employers listed on the site include Alibaba, Bosch, ByteDance, Ctrip, DellEMC, Intel, Lenovo, Microsoft, RedHat and SAP.

During year 2 at Duke, students can do a co-op and work at a US company during an academic term. The site does not describe specific CPT rules.

## Jobs and Location

Year 1 is in Kunshan and year 2 is in Durham; after graduation you can apply for OPT in the US. The site lists Amazon, Apple, Google, Meta, Microsoft, NVIDIA and VMware among the destinations of Duke ECE master's graduates, and says some go on to PhD programs.

The csgrad DP database has no job-outcome records for this program yet.

## Application Requirements

These are the requirements for the Fall 2027 cycle (official site, October 2026). The application portal is still marked "will be opened soon."

- Deadlines: Early is December 15, 2026, with priority consideration for admission and scholarships. Early applications are reviewed within a month of being complete, applicants not admitted roll over to regular decision, and early applicants must also email the admissions office with their name and application number. Priority is January 15, 2027: applications completed by then are guaranteed review, and later ones are considered only if space remains. Admission is rolling throughout.
- There is also an early admission pathway for current juniors, open March 1 to July 15, 2026. It has closed, and students admitted through it also start in Fall 2027.
- Background: a bachelor's degree in engineering or science. There is no minimum GPA and no work experience requirement.
- GRE: optional for 2027 admission.
- English: TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo, with no minimum score. The language page (written for the 2025–2026 application cycle) lists average scores above TOEFL 90, IELTS 7.0 and Duolingo 125; the application page gives a Class of 2026 average GPA of 3.7 and average TOEFL of 102. DKU undergraduates and some other applicants are exempt.
- Materials: 3 recommendation letters (current students need at least one academic letter), a statement of purpose, a resume, transcripts, and a video introduction answering one question shown in the application system. You apply through Duke's graduate application system, and there is no application fee.

Undergrad schools: 3 from 211 universities, 3 from Sino-foreign joint institutes in mainland universities, 1 from the "Huawu" group and 1 from a non-985/211 university. Majors: 4 CS, 2 ECE, and 1 each in software engineering and information security. GPAs were 87–93.5 on a 100-point scale (4 people) and 3.7–3.83 on a 4.0 scale (2 people), plus one on a 5.0 scale and one on another scale; the 7 who reported class rank were between the top 1% and the top 20%. TOEFL 98–110 (7 people), plus one with IELTS 7.0. The 6 who reported a GRE scored 322–335, and the 3 who reported section scores all had 170 Quant. 4 of the 8 are marked as receiving a scholarship; the ones that list an amount describe partial awards ranging from a few thousand to a few tens of thousands of RMB.

The sample is small and has only admits, so it says nothing about who gets rejected.
