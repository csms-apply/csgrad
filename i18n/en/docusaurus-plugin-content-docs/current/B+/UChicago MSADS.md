# UChicago MSADS

## Program Overview

The degree is the Master of Science in Applied Data Science, offered by the University of Chicago's Data Science Institute (DSI).

- Formats: in-person, full time or part time; an online version aimed at working professionals with 2+ years of full-time experience; and an MBA/MS joint degree with Booth, which goes through Booth's admissions. Only the full-time in-person program (1-year and 2-year) is eligible for student visa sponsorship; the online version is not.
- 1-year program: 12 courses. Full-time students usually finish in 4–5 quarters, part-time students usually in 6, and everyone must graduate within 4 years at most.
- 2-year thesis track: 18 courses over 21 months and 6 quarters, full-time in-person only. It was first offered in the 2026-27 academic year, and the FAQ projects about 50–55 spots. Students admitted to the 1-year program cannot later move into the 2-year program.
- Start term: admission is for Autumn entry only, once a year.
- Classes: in-person classes are held at NBC Tower and the Gleacher Center in downtown Chicago. Most courses meet Monday through Thursday, 6–9 pm, with some on Fridays and Saturdays.
- STEM: the full-time in-person program is on the DHS STEM-designated degree list, so students can apply for the STEM OPT extension; USCIS makes the final decision.
- Cost: the tuition page currently shows 2025-26 rates (Summer 2025 through Spring 2026): $6,384 per course, $76,608+ for 12 courses and $114,912+ for 18 courses. The $1,500 enrollment deposit is credited toward the first quarter's tuition. The program expects tuition to rise 3–7% per fiscal year. Partial-tuition merit scholarships are available; there is no separate application, and the program recommends applying early.

## Courses

Full-time students take 3 courses per quarter and part-time students take 2. Each quarter is 10 weeks, and there is a 5-week pre-quarter before the program starts.

The 12 courses in the 1-year program are 6 core courses, 4 electives and 2 capstone courses. The core courses are Statistical Models for Data Science, Leadership and Consulting for Data Science, Cloud-Native Data Engineering, Machine Learning I, Machine Learning II, and Time Series Analysis and Forecasting.

Past electives include Next-Gen NLP: LLM and AgenticAI in Practice, Advanced Computer Vision with Deep Learning, Machine Learning Operations and Inference Engineering, Scalable Data Systems & AI Pipelines, Deep Reinforcement Learning, Causal Models for Data Science, and Quantitative Finance: Methods and Applications; offerings change from year to year.

The capstone runs for two quarters: students work in teams on a real industry or research problem, covering research design, implementation and writing. Full-time students usually start in their third quarter and part-time students usually in their fifth.

There are also 4 noncredit, no-cost foundational courses, all taken online: statistical concepts, R, Python, and linear algebra for machine learning. Incoming students take an online foundational skills assessment first, or can skip it and take all 4 courses. The Career Seminar is required: Pass/Fail, 0 units, registered for every quarter. Students with 3+ years of relevant full-time work experience can petition for a waiver.

The 2-year track adds 4 more electives (8 in total) plus two thesis courses, taken in the winter and spring of the second year and ending with a written thesis.

### Alumni experience

Course projects and real cases help students apply data science methods to work. The program leans applied, so students seeking theoretical depth should look closely at the mathematical content.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The CPT page of UChicago's Office of International Affairs (OIA) states that MS in Applied Data Science students must have been enrolled full time for the three quarters directly before their employment starts, so students are typically eligible from their fourth quarter, and they must register for ADSP 40100 (Applied Data Science Practicum) in the quarter they work. CPT is approved one quarter at a time, and the program's Academic Adviser signs the recommendation form. Work must be under 20 hours per week while classes are in session and can be full time during official breaks. Students who use 12 months of full-time CPT lose eligibility for OPT.

The program page says the evening schedule lets students hold an internship or job while studying, and its career services include internship placement support.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Chicago. The program's Career Outcomes page draws on 2024-2025 First Destination survey data and names JPMorganChase, Google and Amazon as graduate employers; it does not publish an employment rate or salaries. The page also lists 41 companies where students interned in 2026, including Citadel, Jane Street, McKinsey, Meta and BCG.

The employer and internship lists show examples of destinations, not how many students each company hired. The page does not publish an employment rate or salary figures.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines (Autumn 2027, in-person): six rounds, on November 9 and December 7, 2026, and January 26, March 4, May 6 and June 23, 2027. January 26 is the deadline for international applicants who need visa sponsorship from UChicago. Decisions come out about 1–2 months after each deadline, and the portal may close early if the cohort fills. For the 2-year thesis track, the FAQ only says future application timelines will be announced.
- Application fee: $90.
- Materials: unofficial transcripts, a résumé (GPA not needed), a candidate statement of no more than 700 words, 2 recommendation letters (the program strongly recommends that at least one come from a direct manager or internship supervisor; letters from family, friends or peers are not accepted), a programming supplement of no more than 2 pages showing your R or Python skills, and video responses (one required, one optional, about 1.5 minutes each).
- GRE/GMAT: not required; scores can be submitted voluntarily.
- English: TOEFL 102 (prior requirement) or 5 (current requirement), IELTS 7, with no subscore requirements. The university does not accept Duolingo, and scores are valid for two years. You can be exempt if, within the last ten years, you completed at least one full academic year as a full-time degree-seeking student at an English-medium institution in a listed country such as the US or UK.
- Background: a bachelor's degree is required, and the admissions committee values programming experience. The program page says the most competitive applicants usually have at least 1 year of full-time work experience and one or more relevant, sustained internships.

## Admissions Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has no records labeled MSADS. There is only 1 self-reported 2025 Fall record listed as "UChicago MSDS": waitlisted in mid-March, rejected at the end of April. The DSI also runs a separate, research-oriented MS in Data Science (MSDS), so it is unclear which program this record was for; treat it as a rough reference only.

The applicant was a CS major at a Sino-foreign joint institute in mainland China, with a GPA of 86.9 on a 100-point scale, TOEFL 99 (below the 102 that MSADS now requires), and GRE 326 with 170 on the quantitative section. They had domestic research and internship experience and applied several years after finishing their bachelor's.
