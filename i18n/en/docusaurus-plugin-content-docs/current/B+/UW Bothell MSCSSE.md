# UW Bothell MSCSSE

## Program Overview

This is the CS master's in the School of STEM at the University of Washington Bothell. The full degree name is Master of Science in Computer Science & Software Engineering (MSCSSE).

- Credits: 48-49, depending on whether you can waive one writing course
- Length: full-time students take 10 credits per quarter, and the tuition page says 1.5-2 years; part-time students take 5 credits per quarter and need 3-4 years. The curriculum page says most students finish in just over two years
- Schedule: evening classes two or three times a week, built for working professionals; you can enroll full-time or part-time
- Entry: admission once a year, for autumn quarter only, and it can't be deferred to another quarter
- Campus: Bothell. The university's 2025-26 fact sheet puts it about 12 miles from the Seattle campus and about 16 miles from downtown Seattle, 30-45 minutes by car or bus, between Bellevue and Everett
- STEM: neither the program pages nor UWB International Student Services (ISS) list this degree's CIP code. ISS says STEM OPT depends on the CIP code on page 1 of your I-20, so check it yourself once admitted
- Cost: a fee-based program, with the same rate for Washington residents, nonresidents and international students. For 2026-27 tuition is $999 per credit ($979 in 2025-26), so a full-time year of 30 credits is $29,970. Student fees come on top; the tuition page estimates about $500 per quarter, plus a Professional & Continuing Education (PCE) registration fee with no amount listed. At $999 per credit, tuition alone for 48-49 credits comes to about $48,000-49,000
- Funding: RA/TA positions are few and depend on faculty funding; they are announced only to current students and to new students who have paid the enrollment deposit

## Courses and Course Selection

- Core, 16 credits: CSS 599 Faculty Research Seminar (1 credit), plus one 5-credit course from each of three groups: Development, Design and Foundations. Development includes CSS 533 Distributed Programming, 534 Parallel Programming in Grid & Cloud and 535 High Performance Computing; Design includes 553 Software Architecture, 555 Evaluating Software Design and 577 Secure Software Development; Foundations includes 565 Research Methods in Software Development, 566 Software Management and several cybersecurity courses. The curriculum page says faculty may require one or two additional systems core courses depending on your background
- Electives, at least 20 credits: CSS 500-level courses, CSS 600 or approved 400-level courses, with at most 6 credits of CSS 600 independent study and at most 10 credits at the 400 level. Examples on the site include CSS 581 Machine Learning, 586 Deep Learning and Artificial Intelligence, 549 Algorithm Design and Analysis, 545 Mobile Computing and 532 Internet of Things; actual offerings depend on each year's schedule
- Writing, 2-3 credits: CSSSKL 594 (2 credits) is required; CSSSKL 511 Technical Writing (1 credit) can be waived if your writing meets the standard. These rules apply to students entering in Autumn 2025 or later
- Thesis (CSS 700) or capstone project (CSS 595), 10 credits, usually over 2-3 quarters. You shape a proposal with your chair and committee and present your work before graduating. The thesis is research-based, and the site says it suits students considering a PhD or research careers; the project produces something like a software tool, system or prototype

There is no coursework-only option. Each course needs a grade of at least 2.7 to count, you need a cumulative GPA of 3.0 to graduate, and at most 5 credits can transfer in from another institution.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The degree requirements include no internship or co-op component.

UWB ISS rules for CPT: you must first complete 3 quarters without interruption, CPT has to be tied to a course, and the training must be required of all students in the program. The ISS page lists only 11 courses approved for CPT, with the prefixes BNURS, BHS, BEDUC and BES, and no CSS course is among them. Whether you can intern on CPT during the program is something to ask the program advisor and ISS after you are admitted.

ISS also covers pre-completion OPT: you can use it only after one academic year of continuous full-time enrollment, it is capped at 20 hours a week while classes are in session, time used is deducted from post-completion OPT (part-time at half rate), and USCIS approval takes about 90 days.

## Jobs and Location

The careers page's Fast facts say 90.3% of graduates are employed upon graduation and 88% have jobs directly related to their degree, without saying which graduating class the numbers cover. The same set of figures says 52% of students enroll to prepare for new employment, and a good share of students are already working, so this is not the job placement rate for international students fresh out of school.

Alumni employers listed on the careers page include Amazon/AWS, Microsoft, Google, Apple, Adobe, Boeing, Expedia, T-Mobile and Oracle. STEM graduate students are automatically enrolled in a Canvas career resources course.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines (Autumn 2027 entry): the application opens in October; priority deadline February 1, 2027, international applicants March 1, 2027, domestic applicants May 3, 2027. Review is rolling from early February, and decisions usually come about 8 weeks after review starts
- Background: a bachelor's in CS or a closely related field that covers object-oriented programming, data structures and algorithms, and software engineering; a 3.0 GPA over the last two years. Applicants from other fields can first complete UWB's Graduate Certificate in Software Design & Development (GCSDD); students admitted that way may need 5-10 extra credits of systems coursework, and certificate courses don't count toward the master's
- GRE: optional, no minimum score
- English: TOEFL iBT 4.5 (92 for tests taken before January 21, 2026), MyBest scores not accepted; IELTS 7.0, single-skill retakes not accepted; Duolingo 130
- 2-3 recommendation letters; professional references can replace academic ones if academic recommenders aren't available
- Essays are all short answers: 3 personal statement questions of 100-300 words each, where the first (why UW Bothell) is required and you pick 2 of 5 for the other two; a competencies statement, up to 50 words for each item, saying where on your transcript or in your work experience OOP, data structures and algorithms, and software engineering show up, with grade and quarter (operating systems is recommended too); and 4 research questions plus 1 optional one, one of which asks which UWB faculty you want to work with. You also submit a resume and transcripts
