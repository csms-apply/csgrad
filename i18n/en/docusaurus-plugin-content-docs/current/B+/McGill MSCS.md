# McGill MSCS

## Program Overview

The degree is the M.Sc. Computer Science (Thesis), offered by the School of Computer Science at McGill University in Montreal. It is a research master's designed to take 18–24 months: you first register for three full-time terms (usually Fall/Winter/Fall), often followed by one Winter additional session. You need at least 3 full-time terms, and the maximum is 3 years.

Admission is for Fall only. Winter admission is possible in exceptional cases, with an outstanding record and strong support from a professor in the School.

Costs, from McGill Student Accounts' 2026-27 fee table for international students (a Fall 2027 start will be billed at later rates): tuition for a full-time thesis master's is CAD 10,674.30 per term; with insurance and other fees, the Fall total is 13,150.59 and the Winter total 11,515.72. After the three full-time terms you pay additional session fees instead, which total 1,419.18 for a Winter term. The School estimates that a single student needs about CAD 22,000 a year to live in Montreal, not counting tuition.

Funding: the School says most master's students are funded (scholarships, RA or TA). The amount varies by student and is stated in the admission letter; funded master's students receive about CAD 15,000 a year on average. Only applications completed by December 15 are automatically considered for funding. New students can usually only apply for a TA position in their second term.

Canada has no equivalent of the US STEM designation. IRCC says master's graduates can apply for a 3-year PGWP (the program must be at least 8 months long, or 900 hours for Quebec programs). Because the school is in Quebec, international students also need a CAQ in addition to the study permit.

This is a research degree: 29 of 45 credits are thesis research. Look at potential supervisors’ work before applying, and consider whether the funding will cover your living expenses.

## Courses and Course Selection

The 45 credits break down as follows:

- 29 credits of thesis research (COMP 691/696/697/698/699)
- The School's seminar in the first year: COMP 602 (Fall) and COMP 603 (Winter), 1 credit each
- At least 14 credits of COMP courses at the 500 level or above, usually 4 courses, covering at least two of the three areas: Theory, Systems and Applications

You take at least 2 courses in your first term, and the School recommends finishing all 4 by the end of the second term; only one may be taken outside the School. Applications courses include COMP 551 Applied Machine Learning, COMP 579 Reinforcement Learning and COMP 550 NLP; Systems courses include COMP 512 Distributed Systems and COMP 520 Compiler Design.

The thesis has to meet your supervisor's publication requirements. If you want a PhD, you can apply to fast-track into the PhD program after 2 to 4 full-time terms.

The School also runs an MSc non-thesis program. It typically takes 16 months, is aimed at industry, and offers an optional 4-month industrial research internship. One online application can cover up to two programs for a single application fee. Thesis students can switch to non-thesis after their second term with their supervisor's approval.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The thesis program's degree requirements include no internship or co-op; the 4-month internship is an option in the non-thesis program.

Study permit rules let you work off campus up to 24 hours a week during academic sessions, with no limit during scheduled breaks.

## Jobs and Location

The School says the thesis program prepares students for research careers in academia and industry. It publishes no employment data, and csgrad DP has no job outcome records for it either. To stay and work in Canada, you would use the PGWP mentioned above.

## Application Requirements

The following is from the School of Computer Science website as checked in October 2026; the deadlines page does not name a year:

- Deadline: December 15 for international students, which for Fall 2027 means December 15, 2026
- Background: a CGPA of at least 3.2/4, with some room below that for a superior record in CS and math. You need at least the equivalent of a strong CS minor: courses equivalent to at least 4 of McGill's 5 core courses COMP 206/250/251/273/302, plus at least 4 advanced courses at the 300 level or above, and math covering multivariable calculus, linear algebra and discrete structures. Work experience cannot replace courses
- GRE: optional for applicants whose undergraduate degree is from outside Canada
- English: TOEFL iBT 100 with at least 20 in each section; on the new 6-point scale, 5.0 overall with 4.5 in Reading, Listening and Writing and 4.0 in Speaking; or IELTS 6.5 overall. You are exempt if your degree is from a Canadian or US institution or a school that teaches in English
- Recommendation letters: 2; thesis applicants should preferably get academic references from professors
- Statement: thesis applicants are strongly encouraged to submit a personal statement of no more than one page covering their research interests, experience and the professors they want to work with; separately, you must list at least 3 proposed supervisors, or the School will not process the application
- Supervisor: you do not need one to apply, but the School says most students have a supervisor by the time they are admitted; if you start without one, you have one year to find one
- Review: applications for September admission are reviewed between January and March

If you work on machine learning, note that the School's FAQ says many ML professors have limited capacity. Mila opens its supervision request every year from October 15 to December 1; for Fall 2027 the deadline is December 1, 2026. Some Mila professors only take students selected through this process, and if you are selected you still have to submit a separate application to McGill.

## Admission Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has only 4 data points for McGill MSc CS. The catalog entry does not separate thesis from non-thesis; 1 note says it was the thesis program.

| Intake | Result |
|---|---|
| Fall 2022 | 1 reject |
| Fall 2023 | 1 admit, 1 reject |
| Fall 2025 | 1 reject |

All four majored in CS as undergraduates: 1 from the "Huawu" top-five group, 1 from UCAS/ShanghaiTech/SUSTech, 1 from a non-985/211 school and 1 from a Canadian university. Two reported GPAs on a 4-point scale (3.68 and 4.0) and two on a 100-point scale (88.42 and 92). The two who reported TOEFL scored 106 and 109, and the two who reported GRE scored 321 and 326. All four already had published papers when applying, some not as first author.

The only admit studied in Canada, had a 4.0 GPA, several first-author papers and three research recommendation letters; the note says "deferred offer". The three rejects also include an applicant with a first-author paper at a top venue and several research and internship experiences. The Fall 2022 note says the applicant did not contact professors beforehand and only learned after applying that their preferred supervisor recruited only through Mila.

The sample is far too small to say much. Combined with the official statement that most students already have a supervisor when admitted, contacting professors early is worth doing.
