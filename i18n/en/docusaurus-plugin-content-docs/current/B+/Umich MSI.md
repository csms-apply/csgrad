# Umich MSI

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Master of Science in Information, offered by the University of Michigan School of Information (UMSI). It is an iSchool information master's, not the CS master's in the CSE department.

- Credits: 48 graduate credits, at least 39 of them from School of Information (SI) courses
- Length: most students finish in two years; part-time study over three to four years is also possible
- Entry: the website lists Fall 2027 as the next term
- Campus: North Campus in Ann Arbor, in person
- STEM: the FAQ says the program is STEM-designated
- Tuition: for 2026-27, full-time students (9 or more credits per term) pay $31,555 per term at the out-of-state rate, plus fees; over four terms, tuition comes to $126,220. Tuition is capped per term, so extra credits cost nothing more
- Funding: the school offers scholarships from partial to full tuition; every applicant is considered automatically, with no separate application, and offers go out on a rolling basis from March, separately from admission decisions. Almost all of the school's assistantships are teaching (GSI) positions

## Courses and Course Selection

In the first semester everyone takes SI 500 Problem-Solving with People, Information and Technology and SI 506 Programming I. The website says no coding experience is required, and many students arrive having written little or no code.

You then pick one of five pathways, and you can switch during the first semester: Information and Data Analytics; Libraries, Archives and Knowledge Environments in Society; Product Management; User-Centered Application Development; and User Experience Research and Design. Each pathway has a set of prerequisite courses and a few selective courses, and ends with the SI 699 mastery course, a project course. There are also 9 credits of free electives, which can be graduate courses in other U-M schools or internship credit.

For the two more technical pathways, the website lists these courses:

- User-Centered Application Development: prerequisites SI 504 Servers, The Shell, and Git, SI 539 Web Design, SI 579 Building Interactive Applications, SI 582 Interaction Design, SI 631 Agile Software Development and SI 679 back-end development; pick two selectives from SI 526, SI 612, SI 659 AR/VR and SI 669 mobile development
- Information and Data Analytics: prerequisites SI 504, SI 507 Intermediate Programming, SI 544 statistics, SI 564 SQL and Databases, SI 568 and SI 618 Data Manipulation and Analysis; pick three selectives, with options such as SI 630 NLP, SI 650 Information Retrieval, SI 670 Applied Machine Learning and SI 671 Data Mining

The website notes that requirements can differ by cohort, so check the advising page for your own entering class.

## Internships / CPT

An internship is a degree requirement: at least 6 credits and up to 9, which you can start earning after two semesters. Most students do it in the summer between the first and second year, but fall and winter terms also work. The usual courses are SI 681 (mostly for summer internships) and SI 690 (mostly for fall or winter).

International students in good academic standing can use CPT for off-campus internships after two semesters. The FAQ also lists on-campus internships, pre-completion OPT and summer internships in your home country.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Ann Arbor, Michigan. UMSI's 2026 MSI Employment Report covers 2025 graduates: 358 graduated, and 52% answered the survey or had a known outcome. Among respondents the employment rate was 88%, the average salary was $91K overall, $95K in the private sector and $103K in technology. The average job search took 3–4 months, and 20% accepted return offers from their internship employers.

By region, 55% stayed in the Midwest; by state, 45% are in Michigan and 11% each in California and New York. Technology is the top industry at 31%, followed by universities (14%). Top hiring companies in the report include U-M, Michigan Medicine, Accenture, Microsoft, Amazon, Epic, JPMorgan Chase and Adobe. The job list includes UX Designer, Product Designer, Data Engineer, Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer, plus many library, archive and museum roles.

## Application Requirements

The Fall 2027 application is open. The website says:

- Deadline: January 15 for international applicants (January 15, 2027 this cycle), or March 1 for international students currently at U-M; for domestic applicants, January 15 is the scholarship priority deadline and March 1 is the final deadline
- GRE: not required
- GPA: 3.0/4.0 or higher is recommended; applicants below that can still apply, and review is holistic
- Background: any undergraduate major, no prerequisite courses
- English: TOEFL is strongly preferred, with a recommended 5+ overall and 5+ in each section on the new scale (102+ overall and 25+ in each section on the old scale); the recommended IELTS score is 7.5+ overall. Scores must be from the past two years. You are exempt if your whole degree was taught in English
- Materials: three letters of recommendation, a short essay of up to 250 words, a 1–2 page personal statement, a resume and transcripts; the application fee is $90 for international applicants
- Decisions: released on a rolling basis, with the first ones usually in mid- to late February
