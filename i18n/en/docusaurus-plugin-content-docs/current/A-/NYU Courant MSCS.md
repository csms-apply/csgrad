# NYU Courant MSCS

## Program Overview

The degree is the Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS), and you apply through NYU's Graduate School of Arts and Science (GSAS). The CS department is part of the Courant Institute, with its office in Warren Weaver Hall at 251 Mercer Street in Manhattan. NYU Tandon also has an MSCS; the two are different degrees with separate applications, so don't mix them up.

The department FAQ describes the MSCS as rigorous technical preparation, recommended for people planning careers outside academia.

- Credits: the 2026-27 Bulletin lists two options, the standard 30 credits (10 courses) or 36 credits with a concentration (12 courses, AI or Systems and Security)
- Length: the department FAQ says full-time students usually take 3 courses (9 credits) a semester and most finish in two years; part-time study is allowed, and GSAS gives a 5-year limit
- Start terms: fall and spring
- STEM: the Bulletin lists CIP 11.0101, which is on the DHS STEM list
- Cost: for 2026-27, GSAS charges $2,426 per credit, plus a registration and services fee each semester ($503 plus $81 per credit). At that rate, tuition comes to $72,780 for 30 credits or $87,336 for 36 credits, not counting the registration and services fee or New York living costs

## Courses

Both options require three foundational courses: CSCI-GA 1170 Fundamental Algorithms, CSCI-GA 2110 Programming Languages and CSCI-GA 2250 Operating Systems. You need a B- (2.667) average across the three, full-time students must finish them in the first year, and failing a foundational course twice means dismissal.

30-credit option: besides the foundational courses, take one course in one of four application areas (Intelligent Systems, Computing for Science and Society, Graphics, Databases) and one capstone course with a B or better. The capstone can be replaced by a 6-credit master's thesis or a 3-credit advanced lab. The thesis requires a 3.75 GPA after six courses, B+ or better in each foundational course, and DGS approval.

36-credit option: the AI concentration requires CSCI-GA 2560 Artificial Intelligence or CSCI-GA 2565 Machine Learning, two AI electives and an AI capstone. Systems and Security requires three electives and a capstone. Both capstones need a B or better, and a thesis can't replace them. You declare the concentration after your first semester, by April 1 for fall admits or November 1 for spring admits.

At least 21 credits must be standard CS department courses, and another 6 can be related CS, math or data science courses. Courses in other schools such as Tandon need DGS approval. The department FAQ says most master's courses meet in the evening, 5:10–7:00 or 7:10–9:00.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

Internships can earn credit through the 3-credit course CSCI-GA 3870; F-1 students use CPT:

- Full-time students must complete two full-time semesters before the internship starts (you can apply during the second semester), with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 and no probation; students who transfer in nine credits are not eligible
- Get department approval first, then submit the CPT request
- Fall and spring internships are capped at 20 hours a week; summer internships can go over. Internships must last at least 8 weeks at 10 or more hours a week
- The internship course can be taken at most twice; per the degree requirements page, at most 3 internship credits count toward the 30-credit option and at most 6 toward the 36-credit option

The department FAQ also mentions that students can apply for Pre-Completion OPT.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Manhattan, so part-time internships in the city during the semester are possible, but fall and spring internships are capped at 20 hours a week, so full-time internships basically have to happen in the summer. The CIP code is on the STEM list, so eligible graduates can apply for the 24-month STEM OPT extension.

Neither the department nor GSAS publishes employment data for this program.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines: the GSAS program page (updated October 2026) lists February 12 for fall admission and November 1 for spring admission, submitted online by 5 PM Eastern; a deadline on a weekend or federal holiday moves to the next business day. Going by that version of the page, the Fall 2027 deadline is February 12, 2027
- GRE: GSAS lists the MSCS as GRE optional, and the department FAQ says scores are recommended but not required
- English: the GSAS test score page says it sets no minimum scores and recommends TOEFL iBT 100 (5 on the new scoring used since January 2026), IELTS 7 and Duolingo 130, and applicants with lower scores may still apply; the department FAQ also lists TOEFL iBT 100 and IELTS 7.0. For 2027 admission, TOEFL must be taken on or after October 15, 2024
- Recommendations: GSAS requires three letters
- Essays: a Statement of Academic Purpose of no more than two double-spaced pages, plus a résumé and transcripts
- Prerequisites: the department FAQ asks for a deep working knowledge of C, experience with an object-oriented language such as Java or C++, working knowledge of pointers, lists, stacks, queues, trees and recursion, plus induction, growth orders, probability and combinatorics, and familiarity with both Windows and Unix
- Alternate programs: under the same application fee you can also be considered for the department's MSIS and MS-CEI, as well as the Data Science, Mathematics and Scientific Computing master's programs

## Admission Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad currently has 12 NYU Courant MSCS data points, 8 admits and 4 rejects, all for fall entry:

| Entry year | Admit | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 0 | 2 |
| 2023 | 4 | 2 |
| 2024 | 2 | 0 |
| 2025 | 2 | 0 |

There is no data yet for 2026 or 2027, and the recent sample is small.

The 8 admits: 7 from mainland China (2 from China's "top five" universities, 3 from 985, 1 from 211, 1 from a non-985/211 school) and 1 from a Canadian university. Most majored in CS, SE or AI, with one in a CS-related interdisciplinary major and one non-CS major. GPAs ranged from 87 to 91.1 on a 100-point scale and 3.71 to 3.92 on a 4.0 scale. The 6 who reported GRE scored 320 to 329; the other 2 didn't report one. TOEFL ranged from 101 to 109 and IELTS from 7.5 to 8. Seven of the eight listed research experience, including North American summer research, and more than half had one or two internships.

The 4 rejects: 3 from "top five" universities and 1 from a 985 school, with GPAs of 89.3 to 90.1 on a 100-point scale, plus 3.55 on a 4.3 scale and 4.41 on a 5.0 scale. The two who reported GRE scored 326 to 330, and TOEFL ranged from 100 to 106. The notes mention one applicant who applied after two years of work and one who submitted a low GRE. The sample is too small to show a clear admission bar.
