# UW MSDS

## Program Overview

The University of Washington (Seattle) Master of Science in Data Science, which the program describes as a professional master's degree. Faculty come from six departments and schools: Applied Mathematics, Biostatistics, the Allen School (CSE), HCDE, the Information School and Statistics. Courses use the DATA prefix.

- Credits: 45, made up of eight core courses (40 credits) plus a two-quarter capstone (5 credits)
- Length: 1.5 years full-time with two courses per quarter, graduating at the end of winter quarter in the second year; part-time usually takes 2.5 years
- Classes: in the evenings, in person on the UW campus in Seattle, with no online courses. Full-time students attend two evenings a week, and the program estimates 40 hours of work per week on average
- Entry: autumn only, once a year, with a target of 60–65 students per cohort
- STEM: yes, CIP code 11.0401, so graduates may apply for the STEM OPT extension
- Cost: the same rate for Washington residents and nonresidents. For 2026-27 it is $1,244 per credit, or $55,980 for 45 credits; the program tells Autumn 2027 entrants to budget for an increase of about 5%. Quarterly fees are extra; in 2024-25 full-time students paid about $423 per quarter
- Funding: no TA positions for first-year students; most campus TA/RA positions go to PhD students, and the few MSDS students who get one are mostly in their second year. Admitted students are automatically considered for a small number of partial scholarships
- F-1 students must enroll full-time and take at least 10 credits each quarter

## Courses and Course Selection

The cohort structure helps students build connections and learn about job searching from classmates with professional experience. Evening classes can be long, and the curriculum is fairly fixed.

The eight core courses:

- Statistics: DATA 556 Introduction to Statistics and Probability, DATA 557 Applied Statistics and Experimental Design, DATA 558 Statistical Machine Learning for Data Scientists
- Data systems: DATA 514 Data Management for Data Science, DATA 516 Scalable Data Systems and Algorithms (including cloud computing basics)
- Software: DATA 515 Software Design for Data Science (version control, testing, automated builds)
- Other: DATA 511 Data Visualization for Data Scientists, DATA 512 Human-Centered Data Science (ethics, privacy, algorithmic bias)

Full-time students take 511, 514, 515, 556, 557 and 558 in the first year, plus the 1-credit DATA 598 special topics course (reading data science papers). In the autumn and winter quarters of the second year they take 512, 516 and the capstone (DATA 590 and 591). The capstone has small groups work on a project for a company or nonprofit; past sponsors listed on the program site include Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Adobe, Boeing, Zillow and Seattle Children's Hospital.

The FAQ says an elective or two is offered most quarters, but there are no specializations and no real way to go deep into one subfield. Courses lean on Python and R. The program states that it prepares students for industry, not for PhD programs.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

According to the FAQ, internships are recommended but not required; the program encourages part-time internships during the academic year and full-time internships in the summer. The program site does not mention co-op.

International students should pay attention to CPT. On September 8, 2026, UW International Student Services (ISS) announced that, following SEVP guidance from August 2026, it is continuing to pause approval of CPT that is not required for all students in a program. The ISS CPT page also says that an internship a program only "strongly recommends" cannot be authorized for CPT. Under that reading, MSDS internships cannot get CPT at the moment. ISS suggests that students who are not eligible consider pre-completion OPT. The policy is still changing, so check again with the program and ISS before enrolling.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Seattle. Full-time employers the program lists for 2023 alumni include Expedia, Zillow, Intel, IBM, JPMorgan Chase, Roblox, Costco IT and the CDC; summer internship employers include Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Apple, Nike and Walmart, with no year given. The program runs a data science career fair every February, and MSDS students get early access to recruiters. The program does not publish an employment rate.

## Application Requirements

For Autumn 2027 entry:

- Deadlines: Deadline A is January 19, 2027 (11:59 pm Pacific), with decisions in mid-March; Deadline B is April 8, with decisions in mid-June. Applicants who need an F-1 visa can only use Deadline A. Recommendation letters and English scores may come in up to two weeks after the deadline; other late materials are not accepted, and there is no rolling admission
- GPA: 3.0/4.0 over the last two years (about 60 semester credits); applicants below 3.0 can explain in the additional information section
- GRE/GMAT: not accepted
- English: TOEFL iBT 106 overall and 25 per section on the old scale; for the new scale (tests taken on or after January 21, 2026), 5.0 overall and in every section; IELTS 7.5; Duolingo 125. Applicants whose undergraduate instruction was entirely in English and who can document it are exempt
- Prerequisites: calculus (including functions of several variables), linear algebra (matrix operations, linear systems, eigenvalues) and programming with data structures. Show them either through graded, for-credit courses with at least a C+ (2.3), finished by the deadline, or through a technical endorsement from someone who knows your technical work
- Materials: two recommendation letters; two short essays, one of 600 words on what you like about data science and what draws you to UW, and one of 500 words analyzing a visualization from a past capstone; a resume (one page recommended); an optional 350-word additional statement. The application fee is $90, and applicants on F or J visas cannot get a waiver
