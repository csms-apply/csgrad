# UChicago Pre-Doctoral MSCS

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Pre-Doctoral Master of Science in Computer Science, and it sits under UChicago's MPCS (Masters Program in Computer Science). The official page is blunt: the program is only for people preparing for a PhD and committed to academic research, not for those aiming at industry jobs. Each student works with a CS faculty member as a research advisor.

- 12 courses, full-time, Autumn quarter start only, finished in 15 months
- Campus is Hyde Park, Chicago; the page marks it as eligible for the STEM OPT extension
- Cost: the program page estimates tuition at about $89K based on current-year rates; the MPCS tuition page puts the 12-course total for 2026-27 at $89,016. On top of that there is a $520 per-quarter Graduate Student Services Fee and student health insurance ($1,715 per quarter, listed as the 2025-26 rate), plus living costs. Neither the program page nor the tuition page mentions scholarships
- The page only says it is highly selective and admits a small cohort each year, with no number given

How it relates to [UChicago MPCS](../A-/uchicago%20mpcs.md): you apply through the same system and also pick the 12-Course track, but Pre-Doc admission is separate from all other MPCS tracks and transfers in are not accepted. The Pre-Doc faculty on the official list only advise students admitted to this program.

## Courses and Course Selection

The 12 courses fall into three groups:

- Core, 4 courses: 1 theory course (MPCS or PhD level), 2 systems courses (MPCS or an approved 300-level PhD course), 1 machine learning course (MPCS 53111 or an approved 300-level PhD course)
- Research, 4 courses: 2 Practicum courses with your advisor and 2 Reading & Research courses
- Electives, 4 courses: CMSC 300-level PhD courses; MPCS courses need the Pre-Doc faculty advisor's permission

The sample schedule on the official page has 3 courses per quarter: first Autumn, Reading & Research, theory and systems; Winter, Practicum, systems and an elective; Spring, Practicum, Reading & Research and machine learning; Summer, research with your advisor; second Autumn, 3 electives. Research is concentrated in the first year, leaving the final quarter for putting together a PhD application.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The "Internship requirements" section has a single line: students are expected to work with their MS research advisor in the summer after the first academic year. The page says nothing about outside internships or CPT.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Hyde Park, Chicago. The program page publishes no employment or PhD placement data. The program describes its goal as helping students build a competitive PhD application; it is not a job-search program. If you want to go straight into industry after graduating, the MPCS 9-course and 12-course options are a better fit.

## Application Requirements

- Deadline: the Pre-Doc page lists March 1, 2027 for Autumn 2027 entry. But the application steps on the same page tell you to select Round 1 in the portal, and the MPCS deadlines page lists the Autumn 2027 full-time Round 1 deadline as November 2, 2026. The two do not match, so to be safe go by the earlier date, or ask first through the Contact Us form on the official site
- How to apply: in the MPCS portal, choose Masters Program in Computer Science, Round 1 and 12-Course; after submitting the initial application, set the schedule to Full-Time and the Field of Interest to Pre-Doctoral MS in CS on the status page
- Faculty list: the page names 11 faculty willing to review Pre-Doc applications for 2027-28, with their areas, covering graphics and vision, security and privacy, ML systems and networking, trustworthy ML and optimization, HCI, theory, quantum computing and PL/formal verification, NLP and more. Applications that name faculty or areas not on the list will not be reviewed
- Background: faculty put strong weight on prior research experience and want students who can contribute to ongoing research from the start

The Pre-Doc page does not list its own materials, so these are the general requirements from the MPCS admissions FAQ:

- GRE not required; you can choose to submit it
- Applicants who do not qualify for a waiver need TOEFL 5.0 or higher, or IELTS Academic 7.0 or higher. TOEFL moved to a 1–6 scale on January 21, 2026; ETS's comparison table maps a 5 to 95–106 on the old scale
- 2 recommendation letters and a $90 application fee

## Admissions Data (csgrad DP)

csgrad currently has only 3 data points for the Pre-Doctoral MSCS, all for Fall entry:

- 2025: 1 admit, 1 reject
- 2023: 1 reject
- No data for 2024, 2026 or 2027

Backgrounds: 2 US undergrads and 1 mainland China 211 undergrad, all in CS or closely related engineering majors; GPAs of 3.8–3.85 (4.0 scale); the 2 who reported the GRE scored 320–328, both with 170 in Quant; only 1 reported TOEFL, 103 on the old scale. All three had research experience, and each had recommenders who were research advisors or established scholars in their field.

From the notes: the admitted applicant emailed faculty after submitting, had an informal conversation in early March and got a verbal offer; this entry is marked as funded and as the applicant's final choice (the official pages do not mention scholarships, so go by the actual offer). The 2023 reject had no interview and received no email notification. Both rejected applicants also had several research experiences and strong letters. The sample is too small to read an admissions bar from it.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [UChicago MPCS](../A-/uchicago%20mpcs.md): the professional master's on the same application system. The 9-course option can be finished in as little as 9 months full-time and is not eligible for CPT; the 12-course option requires choosing a specialization, full-time students must complete an internship or an approved alternative, and it is aimed at industry. The Pre-Doc admits separately and is built around PhD courses and research
- [Yale MSCS 2 year](../SS/Yale%20MSCS%202year.md): also centered on research and a thesis; the official page mentions a tuition fellowship and a small stipend, but you serve as a 20-hour-per-week Teaching Fellow for all four terms. The Pre-Doc's official pages say nothing about funding
- [Wisc MSCS](../A+/Wisc%20MSCS.md): UW–Madison's traditional research-oriented CS master's, 30 credits, where a project or master's thesis can count for credit; funding comes as fellowships, TA or RA positions
