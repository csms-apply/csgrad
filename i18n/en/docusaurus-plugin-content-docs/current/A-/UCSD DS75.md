# UCSD DS75

## Program Overview

DS75 is one of the Data Science codes in UC San Diego's graduate major code table (it maps to DSC-MS-001) and refers to the on-campus Master of Science in Data Science (MSDS) offered by the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute (HDSI). In July 2024 the UC Regents approved the Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing, and HDSI students are also students of that school.

- Units: twelve 4-unit courses, 48 units in total, at least 40 of which must be graduate-level.
- Length: the catalog says full-time students can finish in 1 to 2 years, with a minimum of 3 registered quarters.
- Start term: the admissions page lists Fall 2027 entry, with the quarter beginning on September 20, 2027.
- Format: an in-person, on-campus program. It is separate from the online Master of Data Science and from the MAS in Data Science and Engineering run jointly with the engineering school. In the online application, choose "Master of Science, Data Science".
- STEM: HDSI's admissions page states that its in-person graduate degrees are STEM designated.
- Cost: the admissions page's tuition link points to UCSD's standard graduate fee table, and the program is not on UC's 2026-27 list of programs charging PDST (Professional Degree Supplemental Tuition). For the 2026-27 academic year, that is $38,087.17 for California nonresidents and $22,985.17 for California residents, both including mandatory health insurance of $2,220 per quarter. Fall 2027 entrants should check the 2027-28 table.

## Courses and Course Selection

Courses cover a range of ML and data science topics. Depending on course choices, there can be time for research and job searching. Popular AI labs attract students from several departments, and relevant experience can help.

Courses fall into three groups, and everything except research units must be taken for a letter grade:

- Group A (foundational): DSC 200 programming, 202 data management, 210 numerical linear algebra, 211 optimization, 212 probability and statistics. At most 4 of these 5 count toward the degree.
- Group B (core): DSC 240 Machine Learning, 241 Statistical Models and 260 data ethics are required, plus at least 3 from 203, 204A Scalable Data Systems, 204B Spark, 206 Algorithms for Data Science, 215, 242, 243, 244, 245 causal inference, 250 data mining and 261. With approval, 241 can be replaced by MATH 282B, and 204A by CSE 202 or DSC 206.
- Group C (electives): DSC 205, 213, 214, 231, 234, 251, 252, 253, 254 and DSC 291 topics courses. With HDSI faculty advisor approval, you can also take graduate or upper-division undergraduate courses in other departments. Up to 8 of the 48 units can be in a domain outside data science.

Statistics and machine learning make up most of the core courses; the systems and algorithms side is mainly 204A, 204B and 206.

There are two ways to finish: Plan I is a thesis, with 8–12 units of DSC 299 and a committee of at least three members; Plan II is a course-based comprehensive exam, where you pass one designated course (B- or better) in each of machine learning/computing, math/statistics and systems/algorithms, with up to 5 attempts, and no thesis.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

HDSI's MS pages don't mention a co-op, and the course requirements include no internship-for-credit course.

The CPT page of ISEO, UCSD's international student office, states that, under two new ICE guidance memos from August 2026, CPT may only be authorized when an internship is a graduation requirement for all students in the program. HDSI's MS is not on the page's list of approved programs; for programs not listed, the academic department has to submit an eligibility request to ISEO. The same page still describes the Summer Cooperative Education program for graduate students, but doesn't say whether the new guidance affects it. Whether Fall 2027 entrants can use CPT for internships will depend on ISEO's and HDSI's rules at that time.

HDSI has its own career services. Career advising is open to graduate students, and job postings and events are on the 12Twenty platform.

## Jobs and Location

The program is in San Diego. HDSI does not publish employment statistics for the MS, and csgrad doesn't have any DS75 job outcome data points yet. Contributions are welcome.

## Application Requirements

The following applies to the Fall 2027 cycle:

- Deadline: December 2, 2026 (marked tentative on the official site). Applicants hear back by April.
- Application fee: $155 for international applicants.
- GRE: not required; you may submit scores voluntarily (institution code 4836).
- English: HDSI does not set its own minimums and uses the Graduate Division's standards: 4.5 on the new TOEFL iBT (85 on the previous iBT), IELTS 7, PTE 65, Duolingo 120. Applicants with a bachelor's or higher degree, with grades of B / 3.0 or better, from a U.S. or foreign institution where English is the sole language of instruction (as listed in the WHED database) may be exempt.
- Recommendation letters: 3.
- Other materials: statement of purpose (no word limit, cannot be edited after submission), CV, unofficial transcripts.
- Background: the catalog asks for an undergraduate GPA of at least 3.0/4.0 and a quantitative degree, or coursework or experience in programming, calculus, probability and statistics. The admissions page says the committee looks for linear algebra, programming and statistics, welcomes applicants from varied majors, and generally admits students with high GPAs.

## Admission Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad currently has 9 DS75 data points, all for fall entry:

| Entry term | Admit | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2023 | 2 | 0 |
| Fall 2024 | 3 | 3 |
| Fall 2025 | 0 | 1 |

5 admits: undergraduates from 985 universities (2, one of them in a non-CS-related major), the "Huawu" top-five group, one of UCAS/ShanghaiTech/SUSTech, and a Canadian university (1 each); majors include CS, DS, information systems and a CS-related interdisciplinary major. GPA was about 86–87 on a 100-point scale (3 people) or 3.87/4 (2 people), TOEFL 103–109 (4 reported), GRE 322–327.

4 rejects: two from 985 universities, one from a 211 university and one from a Sino-foreign joint institute; majors were CS, SE and AI. GPA was 3.21–3.82 on a 4-point scale (2 people), plus 4.33/5 and 86.9/100 (1 each), TOEFL 96–106, and 3 submitted GRE scores of 322–326.

The test score ranges of admits and rejects overlap heavily, and with only 9 data points there is no clear bar. 8 of the 9 applicants listed research or internship experience, and none listed a top-venue first-author paper.
