# Waterloo MMath CS

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Master of Mathematics (MMath) in Computer Science. It is offered by the David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science in the University of Waterloo's Faculty of Mathematics and is taught in person on the main Waterloo campus. It is a research master's: 24 months full-time, with Fall, Winter and Spring start terms. You can finish with a thesis or a Master's Research Paper (MRP), but admission is only to the thesis option; MRP is only available by transferring after you start.

The school states that all full-time MMath and PhD students are funded, usually through a mix of TA pay and graduate research studentships (GRS). For MMath students admitted in Fall 2026 or later, year one is CAD 51,990 for international students and CAD 34,908 for domestic students; the international figure includes the International Masters Award of Excellence. Year two is slightly lower. Part-time students are not funded.

Tuition, per the Finance office's Fall 2026 international fee schedule: a full-time research master's pays CAD 7,696 in tuition plus CAD 1,014.11 in incidental fees per term. Later terms follow that term's fee schedule.

As a Canadian program, STEM designation does not apply. IRCC says graduates of a master's program of at least 8 months can apply for a 3-year PGWP if they meet the other requirements, and most applicants now also need to submit language test results.

## Courses and Course Selection

Under the Fall 2026 Graduate Studies Academic Calendar, the thesis option requires only 4 graduate courses: at least 1 at the 800 level, at most 1 at the 600 level, and no more than 2 from any one area. Courses normally come from the school's areas table, which covers software engineering, programming languages, systems, algorithms, scientific computing, computational statistics, quantum information, AI, databases, graphics and user interfaces, bioinformatics and health informatics.

There are few courses; the thesis is the main work. Before graduating you present your research topic in a public seminar and complete the university's academic integrity module, Graduate AIM. If you transfer to the MRP option, you take 7 courses instead (at least 2 at the 800 level) and finish with a research paper.

Students who do well and whose research is under way can apply after their second term to transfer straight into the PhD, skipping the master's thesis; this needs strong support from the proposed PhD supervisor. The school also has a separate MMath in the Quantum Information specialization.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

Canada has no CPT. To do internships you need the co-op version (MMath in Computer Science - Co-operative Program), but you cannot apply to it directly. You can only apply to transfer in during your first or second term. The school looks at your progress, your supervisor and the Director of Graduate Studies must approve, and the transfer has to be approved at least two weeks before term 2 or term 3 starts.

Once in co-op, you take WIL 601 Career Foundations in your first term and complete at least 1 work term, with a second one possible if the school approves, usually in term 3 (or terms 3 and 4). Within 30 days of returning to study you submit a work report. Jobs are found mainly through WaterlooWorks, and the university states that co-op is competitive and placement is not guaranteed. Finance lists the 2026/2027 co-op fee as CAD 836, charged once in the first study term and again in each work term.

## Jobs and Location

The university is in the city of Waterloo, Ontario. The school's graduate admissions page says MMath co-op students graduate with up to two terms of relevant, paid work experience. The official sites give no employment rate or salary data for this program.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines: for September entry the deadline is December 1 of the previous year, so Fall 2027 means December 1, 2026. January entry closes on June 1 of the previous year, and May entry on October 1 of the previous year. Reference letters can arrive up to 14 days after the deadline, and most decisions come 8–16 weeks after it.
- Background: an honours bachelor's in CS or engineering (or equivalent) with at least 78%. The school expects coursework in programming languages, data structures, operating systems, algorithms and computer organization; gaps may mean remedial courses.
- Supervisor: you do not need a supervisor to apply, but you need one to be admitted, and the school suggests contacting potential supervisors in advance.
- Materials: résumé, a statement of interest of up to 500 words, and transcripts.
- Recommendation letters: 3, at least 2 academic.
- GRE: the school states no CS graduate program requires it.
- English: the Fall 2026 Graduate Studies Academic Calendar sets these minimums for CS programs: TOEFL 5.0 on the new 1–6 scale (writing 4.5, speaking 4), 93 on the old TOEFL used before Winter 2026 (writing 22, speaking 22), and IELTS 6.5 (writing 6.0, speaking 6.5). Scores must be met in a single test, TOEFL MyBest and IELTS One Skill Retake are not accepted, and scores are valid for two years. Three or more years of university study in English in Canada or in a country on the university's exemption list (which includes the US, the UK and Australia) exempts you; a letter from your university confirming English-medium instruction does not count.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- Waterloo MDSAI: also in Waterloo's Faculty of Mathematics; the co-op version is a 16-month coursework master's with 1 work term, while MMath CS is a two-year research degree.
- UBC MSCS: another two-year research CS master's, with two years of guaranteed funding for thesis students.
- [McGill MSCS](../B+/McGill%20MSCS.md): a thesis master's designed to take 18–24 months.
- [UofT MScAC](../A/UofT%20MScAC.md): also in Ontario; a 16-month professional master's whose last 8 months are an applied research internship at a company.
