# UCSD EC93

## Program Overview

EC93 is the M.S. in the Machine Learning and Data Science (MLDS) area of UC San Diego's ECE department, which is part of the Jacobs School of Engineering. UCSD's graduate major code list gives the degree name as Electrical Engineering (Machine Learning and Data Science) (M.S.).

- Units: 48 units under Plan II (exam) or 52 units under Plan I (thesis).
- Length: everything must be finished within 6 quarters. The department FAQ says finishing early is possible, but because many courses aren't offered every quarter, almost all students use all 6 quarters.
- Start term: fall only.
- Campus: the main La Jolla campus.
- STEM: UCSD's public major code list doesn't include CIP codes. Whether you can apply for the STEM OPT extension depends on the CIP code printed on your I-20 after admission.
- Cost: the program is not on UC's 2026-27 list of programs charging PDST (Professional Degree Supplemental Tuition) and is not on UCSD's list of self-supporting major codes, so it pays standard graduate fees. For the 2026-27 academic year, that is $38,087.17 for California nonresidents and $22,985.17 for California residents, both including mandatory health insurance of $2,220 per quarter. New international students also pay a one-time $200 visa administration fee. Students entering in Fall 2027 will pay the 2027-28 rates.

## Courses and Course Selection

The curriculum has a substantial math component. CSE graduate courses can fill gaps or deepen an existing interest, and some course projects can be developed further.

The 2026-27 degree planner has three blocks of 16 units each:

- Core (all 4 required): ECE 143 Programming for Data Analysis, 225A Probability and Statistics for Data Science, 269 Linear Algebra & Application, 271A Statistical Learning I.
- Additional units (16 units): 271B Statistical Learning II, 271C Deep Learning and Applications, 273 Convex Optimization, 250 Random Processes, 225B, 226 optimization and acceleration of deep learning on different hardware, 227 Big Network Data, 228, 229, 275A-B Parameter Estimation, 276A-B-C robotics, 277 GPU Programming, 284 and 285 special topics (285 can be repeated if the topics differ), and others.
- Technical electives (16 units): any 4-unit 200-level or higher course from ECE, CSE, MAE, BENG, DSC, MATH, PHYS, COGS and several other departments. The planner recommends MATH 245, 282 and 289C and COGS 260. At most 12 units of undergraduate ECE courses can count, or 2 undergraduate ECE courses plus 1 undergraduate CSE course. Plan II students can count at most 4 units of research.

Under Plan II, at least 16 units must be ECE courses numbered 201 or above, and every course counted toward the degree (research aside) must be taken for a letter grade.

ECE 143 carries an undergraduate course number. The department FAQ still advises against pairing it with two other core courses in the same quarter, and especially against taking 2–3 core courses in your first quarter.

The FAQ says almost all students choose Plan II: pass course-hosted exams in three courses on the eligible list, where the exam is that course's final exam or overall grade, with at most 5 attempts in total. The 2026-27 list includes the three core courses 225A, 269 and 271A, all currently judged on the final exam (the department says the assessment type may be updated each quarter); 250 and 276A are on the list too.

CSE graduate courses give first priority to CSE graduate students. Non-CSE majors can enroll only if space permits, which may not happen until week 1–2 of the quarter.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

Right now the department and UCSD's International Services and Engagement Office (ISEO) say different things:

- The ECE department's CPT page says that CPT during the academic year requires enrolling in ECE 298 (S/U, does not count toward the degree, with a 2–3 page report due by week 10), and that in summer you can sign a Summer Cooperative Education agreement, with no academic credit needed and no summer tuition.
- ISEO's CPT page states that, under two ICE guidance memos from August 2026, CPT can only be authorized when an internship is a graduation requirement for all students in the program. ECE's MS is not on the page's list of approved programs; for a program that isn't listed, the department has to ask ISEO to approve it.

Whether Fall 2027 entrants can use CPT for internships will depend on ISEO's rules at that time.

## Application Requirements

The following applies to the Fall 2027 cycle:

- Deadline: January 6, 2027, 11:59 pm California time. The application opened on September 2, 2026. Review starts after the deadline; decisions go out on a rolling basis from the end of January, with the goal of releasing most of them by early April.
- Area: MLDS is an impacted major in ECE. You have to choose EC93 when you apply; if you're admitted to a different area, you can't switch into it later.
- Background: a bachelor's or master's degree in engineering, physical sciences or mathematics, with a GPA of at least 3.0/4.0.
- GRE: not required for Fall 2027; the admissions page flags this as new.
- English: TOEFL iBT 85 (4.5 on the new 1–6 scale), IELTS 7, Duolingo 120, PTE 65. A degree from a school that teaches solely in English (with grades of B or better) can waive this, but the school must be listed in the WHED database with English as its only language of instruction.
- Recommendation letters: 3.
- Other materials: statement of purpose (PDF, no larger than 2 MB), unofficial transcripts from every college or university attended; a CV is recommended.
- Application fee: $155 for international applicants, with no waiver available.

## Admission Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad currently has 5 EC93 data points, all for fall entry:

| Entry term | Admit | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2023 | 1 | 0 |
| Fall 2024 | 2 | 1 |
| Fall 2025 | 1 | 0 |

One Fall 2024 entry recorded as Admit has a note from the applicant saying it was actually a rejection. Counting it that way, Fall 2024 was 1 admit and 2 rejects.

3 confirmed admits: one each from the "Huawu" top-five group, a 211 university and a Sino-foreign joint institute, with majors in CS, a CS-related interdisciplinary field and another science/engineering field. GPA was about 87 on a 100-point scale (2 people) or 3.75/4 (1 person), TOEFL 99–109, GRE 326–327.

The entry whose note says it was a rejection: undergraduate at Tsinghua/Peking, AI major, GPA 3.69/4, TOEFL 113, GRE 320, with one top-venue first-author paper under submission.

The clear reject: undergraduate at a 985 university, already holding a master's degree from China when applying, undergraduate major listed as "other disciplines", GPA about 86 on a 100-point scale, TOEFL 103, GRE 322.

All 4 who reported their experience had 1–2 domestic research projects and 1–2 domestic internships. None reported overseas experience, and none had a published top-venue paper. Both confirmed admits who described their recommendation letters had at least two marked as strong, while the clear reject marked all three as ordinary letters. All 5 submitted GRE scores, which are no longer required from Fall 2027. With only 5 data points, treat this as a rough reference.
