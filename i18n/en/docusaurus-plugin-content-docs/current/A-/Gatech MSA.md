# Gatech MSA

## Program Overview

This is Georgia Tech's analytics master's. The degree is the Master of Science in Analytics (MSA), offered jointly by Georgia Tech's Colleges of Computing, Business and Engineering. This page covers the in-person Atlanta program. There is also an online version, OMSA; the program says the two have the same academic requirements, courses and faculty, with fewer electives online. The Atlanta program costs more but comes with one-on-one help from the MSA career services team.

- Credits: 36
- Length: designed for one year (fall, spring and summer); it can also be done over four semesters
- Entry: the on-campus cohort generally starts only in Fall; Spring entry is occasionally allowed for current GT students with the right background
- Campus: Atlanta
- STEM: the program FAQ says MSA is a STEM program (CIP 52.1399) and F-1 students qualify for OPT
- Cost: estimated at 2026-27 rates (including a one-time $2,000 program fee), total tuition for international students is about $61,774 for one year (three semesters) and about $74,034 for four semesters. The program notes rates may change
- Funding: dozens of TA positions each year, open to international students; the program estimates their value at more than $17,750 per semester for non-Georgia residents. All applicants are considered automatically, with no separate application

The program's 2026 student body profile: 85 students, 32% international; the most common academic backgrounds (students can count in more than one) are CS/software/IT (27%), data science and AI (20%), and business and math/statistics (16% each).

## Courses and Course Selection

Under the 2026-27 catalog, the 36 credits break down into three parts:

- Core, 15 credits: CSE 6040 Computing for Data Analysis, ISYE 6501 Intro to Analytics Modeling, MGT 8803 Introduction to Business for Analytics (or MGT 6201 or MGT 6753), plus the advanced core CSE 6242 Data and Visual Analytics and MGT 6203 Data Analytics in Business. With enough background, the first three can be replaced by electives
- Electives, 15 credits: at least 2 statistics/machine learning courses and at least 1 operations research course; the rest are open
- Applied Analytics Practicum, 6 credits (CSE/ISYE/MGT 6748)

Electives must satisfy one of three tracks: Analytical Tools leans toward methods such as statistics, machine learning and optimization; Business Analytics requires at least 3 business analytics courses (including MGT 6203); Computational Data Analytics requires at least 3 computing courses (including CSE 6242) and at least one machine learning course.

On the program's course list, statistics options include CS 7641 Machine Learning, CSE/ISYE 6740 and ISYE 6414 Regression; operations research options include ISYE 6644 Simulation and ISYE 6669 Deterministic Optimization; computing options include CS 7643 Deep Learning, CS 6400 Database Systems and CS 6220 Big Data Systems. The program notes that the semesters courses are offered may change.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The practicum can be done two ways: a company-sponsored team project (3–4 students per team, 10 weeks in summer or 15 weeks in fall/spring, about 20 hours a week, remote and unpaid), or an internship counted as the practicum. Only US citizens and permanent residents can count a full-time job. Before taking the practicum you need at least 7 (preferably 10) MSA courses, including CSE 6242 and MGT 6203.

International students counting an internship register for the practicum course and record the internship with the Career Center in CareerBuzz. They are then automatically enrolled in INTN, a 12-audit-credit course, and the Career Center sends their information to the Office of International Education to start CPT approval. The program notes that if you finish 10 courses in fall and spring and take a summer internship, that internship must count as your practicum and you graduate in August; international students who extend to a December graduation need to keep at least one required MSA course for their second fall.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Atlanta. Each student has a dedicated career advisor, there is an MSA-only Analytics Career Fair every February, and Meet the Firms runs in October. Past practicum sponsors include Delta, The Home Depot, NCR, Wells Fargo, Accenture and Emory Healthcare. The program's statistics page currently shows only the 2026 student body profile, with no employment data by class.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines: for the Fall 2027 Atlanta cohort, the priority deadline is January 1 and the late deadline is March 1, the same for international applicants; decisions usually come out around the end of February. The program says it receives more than 1,000 applications a year and admits a class of about 100 each Fall
- GRE/GMAT: required, no waivers, expired scores accepted
- English: TOEFL total 5 with each section at least 4, or IELTS 7.5 with at least 6.5 in Reading, Listening and Speaking and 5.5 in Writing. The program page does not state the TOEFL scale; the Graduate School says TOEFL tests taken on or after January 21, 2026 are scored on a 1–6 scale, and earlier 0–120 scores stay valid for two years
- Prerequisites: calculus, probability/statistics, linear algebra and a programming language (Python preferred); applicants missing these can learn the material on their own before arriving or take preparatory courses on campus
- Three recommendation letters, plus a Statement of Purpose of up to 500 words and a resume
- Application fee: $105 for international applicants
