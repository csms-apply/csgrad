# UCI MDS

## Program Overview

UCI's MDS is a compact professional master's: it takes 15 months full time, splits coursework between statistics and computing, and ends with an industry capstone. If you want more time to explore research or a more flexible internship schedule, compare that timeline with your other options.

- Length: 15 months full time, 5 quarters including summer; the part-time pathway takes about two years and is open only to domestic applicants.
- Units: 52.
- Start term: Fall only.
- Format: in person on the Irvine campus; there is no hybrid or online version.
- STEM: the admissions page says the program is STEM certified and eligible for 36-month OPT.
- Cost: MDS is self-supporting, and the Registrar's fee schedule has no nonresident supplemental tuition. For the full-time cohort entering Fall 2026, the program fee is $63,200 in total, $12,640 per quarter; the four quarters of 2026-27, with campus fees and health insurance, come to $60,525.13. Fees for the Fall 2027 cohort are not published yet.
- Funding: a limited number of need-based fellowships, considered automatically with the application; TA and RA positions are not available to professional students. Admitted students pay a $500 deposit to hold their spot.

## Courses

Ten required courses:

- Computer science core: DATA 220P Databases and Data Management, DATA 260P Fundamentals of Algorithms in Data Science, COMPSCI 273P Machine Learning and Data Mining.
- Statistics core: DATA 200AP / 200BP Intermediate Probability and Statistical Theory, DATA 210P / 211P Statistical Methods.
- Data core: DATA 280P Data Science Career Seminar (0 units), plus the two capstone courses taken together in the final quarter, DATA 296P and DATA 297P.

You also take one of DATA 294P (Hypothesis and Project Development), DATA 298P (Curricular Practical Training) or DATA 299P (Individual Study), and at least 3 electives. The electives include CS courses in security, data management, distributed and big data, data structures, AI and deep learning; statistics courses such as Bayesian analysis, multivariate methods, time series and stochastic processes; and DATA 275P on generative models and the DATA 295P special topics course.

The FAQ says you cannot take courses outside the program curriculum. You need to keep a GPA of at least 3.0 throughout, with no single grade below B. You can petition to substitute at most 2 relevant graduate courses taken earlier.

The capstone is a small-team project on a real problem with an industry partner. At the end you hand in code and documentation and explain the findings to a non-technical audience in a report or presentation. Most of the 2022 and 2023 sample projects on the site are about health or social issues, such as NLP on clinical notes to predict NICU readmissions and deploying an open-source LLM for a municipality.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The curriculum includes DATA 298P Curricular Practical Training (2 units, an internship at an outside organization), but it is only one of three options, so you can graduate without it. The UCI International Center's current CPT page says CPT is approved only for internships required for the degree. Among the programs it lists as having an internship requirement, the only one in the ICS school is MSWE; MDS is not listed. CPT also requires one full academic year of enrollment first, and you can work at most 20 hours a week during the quarter, full time only during breaks.

The five full-time quarters include summer, and you register and pay the program fee for the summer quarter too. If you want an internship while enrolled, check with the program and the International Center early after you arrive.

## Jobs and Location

The campus is in Irvine, in Orange County, Southern California. ICS has a career development team dedicated to its professional master's students, offering one-on-one coaching, resume reviews, career fairs and alumni events. The site lists graduate job titles such as AI app engineer, AI/ML engineer, algorithm engineer, data scientist and data engineer, but publishes no employment rate or salary data.

## Application Requirements

- Deadline (Fall 2027): the application opens in October 2026; the deadline is January 15, 2027 for international applicants and March 15, 2027 for domestic applicants. Decisions come within two months of the deadline.
- Background: you need the equivalent of a four-year US bachelor's degree. Any major can apply, with preference for STEM degrees (data science, CS, statistics or biostatistics, computer or electrical engineering). The program homepage says applicants should be proficient in at least two programming languages.
- Prerequisites: 1 programming course (preferably C++ or Python), 3 calculus courses, 1 linear algebra course and 1 introductory probability and statistics course. You also submit a supplemental list of your CS, math and statistics courses with course number, title and grade. Online platforms such as Coursera or edX do not count as prerequisite courses.
- GRE: not required.
- English: TOEFL iBT 80; IELTS 7 with no module below 6; scores are valid for two years. You are exempt if your undergraduate institution is listed in WHED with English as the sole language of instruction.
- Materials: transcripts, 3 letters of recommendation (academic or professional), Personal History Statement, Statement of Purpose and resume; GitHub, Kaggle or a portfolio is encouraged. The application fee for international applicants is $155, and fee waivers are only for domestic applicants.
