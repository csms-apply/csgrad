# UF MSCS

## Program Overview

The degree is the Master of Science in Computer Science, offered by the CISE department in the Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering at the University of Florida. The department FAQ says both the CS and CE master's programs are on-campus programs and cannot be done fully online. The main campus is in Gainesville, Florida, and you can start in Fall or Spring.

There is also a separate MSCS in Jacksonville with AI and cybersecurity concentrations, also 30 credits. The site says it is designed for working professionals as well as students with a CS background who want a graduate degree. For the Fall 2027 cohort, its priority deadline is April 15, 2027 and the final deadline is June 1, 2027. This guide covers the Gainesville program.

You need 30 credits to graduate. The cap is 9 credits per semester; only in your final semester can you request a one-time exception up to 12 credits, and approval is not guaranteed. Under this cap, 30 credits take at least 4 semesters, or 3 if you get the 12-credit exception in your final semester. All requirements must be finished within 7 years, and full-time students are expected to finish within 3.

Cost: UF Student Financial Aid's projected 2026-27 tuition and fees for an out-of-state graduate student living off campus are $34,780; with living costs, books, transportation and the rest, the total budget is $58,470. These are projected figures for graduate students entering UF for the first time.

Master's students do not get TA or RA positions; those go to PhD students only. New master's students who are not Florida residents (international students included) are automatically considered for the Academic Achievement Award, with no separate application: students admitted Fall 2026 or later receive $3,300 in total, $1,100 per semester for three semesters; the earlier Spring 2026 cohort (including Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 admits who deferred) receives $4,500. You must take at least 9 credits and keep a 3.0 GPA in each award semester; it is not paid in summer and cannot be combined with other awards such as assistantships. The department also hires graders as needed: you need an A in the course, an overall GPA of at least 3.7 and one completed semester; the work is up to 10 hours a week and does not reduce tuition.

STEM: the CISE pages do not give this degree's CIP code. The UF International Center's STEM OPT page requires the major to be on the DHS STEM list; what counts is the CIP code on your I-20.

## Courses

The core is 4 courses chosen from a list of 12, 12 credits in total: human-computer interaction, bioinformatics, database management systems, computer architecture, software engineering, cryptology, computer networks, advanced data structures, programming language principles, distributed operating systems, analysis of algorithms, and mathematics for intelligent systems.

- Non-thesis: 12 core credits plus 18 credits of other graduate courses, at least 12 of them from CISE. Individual Study counts for at most 3 credits and the graduate seminar for at most 1. After finishing coursework you pass a brief written exam on general computer science knowledge.
- Thesis: 12 core credits plus 6 credits of thesis research and 12 credits of other courses (at least 6 from CISE). You form a supervisory committee by the end of your second semester and finish with an oral defense.

Under either option, Special Topics count for at most 9 credits, courses outside the department for at most 6 (pre-approved courses only), and graduate students cannot take undergraduate courses numbered 4999 or below. Up to 9 credits of graduate coursework from another school can be transferred, and the request must be made in your first semester. To graduate you need a GPA of at least 3.0 overall and in CISE courses. International students can take at most one online course within their 9 credits each semester.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The degree has no required internship or co-op. CISE lets you count the EGN 5949 internship course toward the degree, up to 3 credits, and a fall or spring internship must last 12 weeks. The department's new-student page lists these conditions: international students need one year of study at UF or in the US, you need a GPA of at least 3.0, and the internship credits count against the 6-credit limit on courses outside the department.

The UF International Center's current CPT page is stricter: you first need one full academic year (fall and spring) of full-time enrollment, and the training must be a required part of the curriculum for all students; CPT is not approved for optional or elective training. Under that rule it is unclear whether an elective MSCS internship qualifies for CPT. The alternative the International Center gives is pre-completion OPT, which it says takes USCIS about 90 days on average. This reflects the official pages as of October 2026; after you enroll, confirm with a CISE graduate advisor and the International Center.

## Jobs and Location

CISE does not publish employment data for master's students. The department's new-student page says students have to find internships themselves, and UF's Career Connections Center can help with resumes and interview practice. The department's CISE Graduate Blog reposts some internship openings.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines: for Fall entry, the priority deadline is January 5 and the international deadline is February 1; for Spring entry, the international deadline is August 1. The admissions page does not give a year, so check the application system for Fall 2027.
- Background: the department says most admitted students have a bachelor's in CS, CE or EE with an undergraduate GPA of at least 3.3/4.0. The department FAQ says the Graduate School enforces a 3.0 GPA minimum and applications below it cannot be considered. Prerequisites include calculus, statistics, discrete structures, computer organization, data structures and algorithms, and operating systems.
- GRE: optional; if you submit it, a Verbal score of at least 140 is expected.
- English: CISE requires TOEFL iBT 90 with at least 19 on each section, or IELTS 7.5 overall (6.5 in Reading, Listening and Speaking, 5.5 in Writing), which is higher than the Graduate School minimum (TOEFL 80 / IELTS 6.5).
- Materials: the statement of purpose and 3 letters of recommendation go through the online application, and unofficial transcripts are fine for review. The application fee is $30 and cannot be waived.

## Admissions Data (csgrad DPs)

csgrad has 3 self-reported UF MSCS records, all for Fall entry and all admits:

- 2023 Fall: 2 admits
- 2024 Fall: 1 admit

All three did their undergrad in mainland China (one each from the "Huawu" top-five group, a 985 school and a 211 school), majoring in CS, software engineering and information systems. GPAs: 3.69–3.87 on a 4.0 scale (2 records), and one just above 85 on a 100-point scale. TOEFL 105–109, GRE 321–327 with Quant 167–170. All three had research experience.

All three records are marked as funded; two notes describe small scholarships ($4,500, or $1,500 per semester), matching the older Academic Achievement Award amount on the official site. None recorded a final destination. The sample is very small, and there are no Fall 2025 or Fall 2026 records.
