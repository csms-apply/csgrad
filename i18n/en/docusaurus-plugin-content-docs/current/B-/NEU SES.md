# NEU SES

## Program Overview
The full name is Software Engineering Systems. It is very much a traditional CS program. The College of Engineering currently waives the MS application fee (Spring 2027 and Fall 2027 entry), and one application lets you check up to three programs — so when you check off NEU MSCS and CS Align, you can also check NEU SES. SES is offered at the Boston, Arlington, Toronto and other campuses; check the official website for the current list.

## Admission Preferences & Representative Data Points
Easier to get into than IS. Applicants with a GPA of 80+ (on a 100-point scale) all have a chance.

## Job Outcomes & Data Points
Overall mediocre, but there are cases of Indian students landing offers at Google DeepMind.

- information science undergraduate in India, full-time software engineering at a small company before the master’s, then SWE full-time at a small company after graduating in 2025.
