# USC ADS

## Program Overview

USC's Applied Data Science master's (ADS) is offered by the Viterbi School of Engineering. It is coursework-based, with at most two courses per semester, so plan for at least two academic years. When budgeting, include Los Angeles housing and health insurance alongside tuition.

- Units: 32, all coursework. There is no thesis option; Directed Research can be requested after the first semester.
- Pace: the program website says students may take only 2 courses per semester, so the fastest path is 4 semesters (two academic years).
- Start terms: spring and fall.
- Delivery: on campus in Los Angeles, or online through DEN@Viterbi, with the same degree and the same tuition.
- STEM: eligible for the STEM OPT extension.
- Cost: graduate engineering tuition for 2026-27 is $2,742 per unit. Viterbi's official example for 32-unit master's programs assumes a Fall 2026 start and four semesters of 8 units each: tuition plus fees come to $91,487, and with two years of estimated housing ($1,300 per month) the total is $122,687. Health insurance adds about $7,044. Rates for 2027-28 have not been published yet.

## Courses

Three required courses, 12 units in total: DSCI 551 (Foundations of Data Management), DSCI 552 (Machine Learning for Data Science) and DSCI 553 (Foundations and Applications of Data Mining).

The other 20 units are five electives, chosen from additional 500-level DSCI courses and an approved CSCI list. The CSCI list has 11 courses (544, 550, 551, 561, 566, 567, 570, 571, 572, 585, 587), covering NLP, foundations of AI, deep learning, machine learning, algorithms, web technologies, information retrieval, databases and more, but at most 2 CSCI courses count. Directed Research, thesis courses and the ENGR 595/596 internship courses do not count as electives.

If your CS background is limited, the program recommends taking DSCI 510, DSCI 549 and DSCI 550 first; waivers can be requested. DSCI 550 should come before or alongside DSCI 551, and before DSCI 552 and DSCI 553.

The Viterbi admission page presents the professional practicum as a feature of the program, but it is not one of the three required courses.

The program page notes that the USC Catalogue is the official source for degree requirements, and current students follow the Catalogue for the year they started.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The program page does not describe a co-op arrangement. Internships go through Viterbi's ENGR 595 internship course to get CPT. International students must keep full-time F-1 status for one academic year (fall and spring) before applying, and the GPA requirement is 3.0 for fall/spring and 2.75 for summer. In fall and spring you can only work part time (10–20 hours per week) unless it is your final semester, in which case you can work full time if you are enrolled in no more than 4 units. Summer internships can be full time.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Los Angeles. The admission page publishes 2025 First Destinations data from a voluntary survey, which it says should not be read as a full picture of the class: the average reported salary is $108,961, the top employers listed are Amazon, Meta, TWG Global Holdings and USC, and international students completed 78 CPT internships in 2025.

## Application Requirements

- Deadline (Fall 2027): December 15 for scholarship consideration and January 15 as the final deadline. The spring deadline is October 15, so Spring 2027 has already closed.
- Background: a bachelor's in engineering or a related field; statistics, mathematics, CS, information systems, economics, IT, physics and similar majors are also accepted, and the admission page asks for a demonstrated aptitude for math and science. Applicants from fields that are not directly related may still be considered, with holistic review.
- Materials: transcripts, résumé, personal statement, and 2 recommendation letters (listed as highly recommended). The application fee for most USC graduate programs is $120.
- GRE: not required for 2027 applications.
- English: USC has no university-wide minimum score for admission. The scores that exempt master's students from the on-arrival English exam (ISE) are TOEFL 90 with 20 in each section (tests taken before January 21, 2026) or 4.5 with 4 in each section on the new scale, and IELTS 6.5 with 6 in each band; all subscores must come from the same test sitting. Scores must be dated within 24 months of submitting the application. A U.S. bachelor's degree qualifies for a waiver.

## Admissions Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has only 2 self-reported USC ADS records, both for fall entry:

- 2025 Fall: 1 record; the note says the applicant never heard back, so it is counted as a silent rejection
- 2026 Fall: 1 admit

Both applicants were CS majors from mainland Chinese universities: one from a non-985/211 school and one from a Sino-foreign joint institute. GPA was 86.9–91.8 on a 100-point scale, both within the top 15% of their major; TOEFL 99–103; GRE 326–332, with 170 on the quantitative section in both cases. Each had one domestic research experience and one domestic internship. The applicant who never heard back applied several years after finishing their bachelor's.

The sample is too small to say much, and there are no records for spring entry yet.
