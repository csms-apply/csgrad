# UBC ECE MEng

## Program Overview

The official degree is the Master of Engineering in Electrical and Computer Engineering (MEng), offered by the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering in the Faculty of Applied Science at UBC Vancouver. It is course-based, so you do not need a supervisor to apply; the department says applicants who may want a PhD later should apply to the MASc instead.

You need 30 credits to graduate. Most students finish in 12–16 months, and you have up to 5 years. Intake is September only; there is no January intake. Full-time students must spend at least one winter session at UBC. The degree is simply Electrical and Computer Engineering, with no sub-classification on the certificate. According to the graduate school, the 224 students who graduated between 2021 and 2024 took 1.7 years on average.

Canada has no equivalent of the US STEM designation; after graduation you go through the PGWP. UBC's international student guide says that since February 15, 2024, graduates of a master's program of 8 months or longer are eligible for a 3-year PGWP, and master's graduates do not need to meet the field-of-study requirement. Conditions include full-time study in every academic session, completing at least half of the program in person in Canada, and English at CLB 7.

Tuition here is the first-year, full-time rate listed on the graduate school program page in October 2026: 3 installments a year, with international students paying CAD $9,139.78 per installment, or $27,419.34 a year, and domestic students paying $2,324.98 per installment. The page notes that tuition usually rises 2%–5% a year. Student fees are about $1,203.20 per year. Admitted students pay a non-refundable $1,000 deposit, which is credited toward tuition. The graduate school page says financial aid is generally not available to MEng students, though they can apply for TA positions in ECE.

## Courses and Course Selection

There are three ways to make up the 30 credits. The common rules: at least 15 credits must be ECE courses (EECE/ELEC/CPEN), at most 6 credits can be 300/400-level undergraduate courses, and directed studies count for at most 3 credits.

- Option 1: 12 credits of 500-level ECE + 3 credits of 300–500-level ECE + 12 credits of 500-level courses in any subject + 3 credits of 300–500-level courses in any subject
- Option 2: 15 credits of 500-level ECE + 9 credits of 500-level courses in any subject + 6 credits of 300–500-level courses in any subject
- Option 3: includes the 6-credit EECE 597 project. You find your own supervisor and topic and submit a final report. The department's example is 4 courses in each of the first two terms plus EECE 597 in the summer, which finishes the degree in one calendar year.

The department lists recommended course groupings. The software and computer systems ones are:

- Software Engineering: CPEN 522 Software Verification and Testing, CPEN 542 Topics in Computer Security, CPEN 541 Human Interface Technologies, EECE 571B Big Data Systems
- Computer Systems: CPEN 511 Advanced Computer Architecture, CPEN 513 CAD Algorithms for Integrated Circuits, CPEN 502 Architecture for Learning Systems

There are also groupings for communications, signal processing, energy, and micro/nano electronics. The department notes that these courses are not guaranteed to be offered every year.

The grade rules are strict: 68% is a pass, 60–67% is a marginal pass that can count for at most 6 credits, and you can fail at most 2 courses in the whole program. A third failure means automatic withdrawal, and a repeated course needs 74%.

## Internships / Co-op

Eligibility for co-op does not guarantee a placement. Learn about the application requirements and job-search process early when planning your studies.

Co-op is optional and does not count toward MEng credits. It makes the program longer; the department notes that co-op work may turn into a project for EECE 597.

According to UBC Applied Science's co-op office, full-time MEng students starting in September must apply in the same year and term their program starts, and applications are taken once a year (the 2026/27 round ran from August 5 to September 13, 2026). Work terms usually start in May and last 4 or 8 months, with at least 420 hours in a single work term. There is a one-time $288.25 administration fee, plus an $889 co-op course fee for each 4-month work term once you secure a job. A job is not guaranteed.

During academic sessions, full-time international students with a valid study permit can work unlimited hours on campus and up to 24 hours a week off campus.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Vancouver. The two main routes to work are co-op during the program and a full-time job in Canada on the PGWP after graduation.

The csgrad DP database has no job-outcome records for this program yet.

## Application Requirements

These are the requirements for the Fall 2027 cycle, as shown on the official site in October 2026.

- Deadline: December 12, 2026, for both international and Canadian applicants; the application opens October 1, 2026. If you submit by the deadline, references, other documents, the video response and test scores can follow until January 6, 2027. Decisions come out in the spring.
- Background: a four-year bachelor's degree recognized by UBC in EE, CE or a closely related field; industry experience is preferred. US and Canadian degrees need at least a B+ (3.3/4.0). The department says an application is unlikely to succeed unless the CGPA is in the first-class range, and that meeting the minimums does not guarantee admission: admitted applicants usually exceed them, and many hold degrees from highly ranked universities.
- GRE: not required; you may submit one voluntarily.
- English: new-format TOEFL iBT overall 5, with section minimums of Reading 4.5, Listening 5, Writing 4.5, Speaking 4 (previously 100 overall and 22/22/21/21); IELTS Academic 7.0 with no band below 6.0; Duolingo 130. Applications below the minimums are not considered. Applicants with a degree from the US, Canada, the UK, Australia and some other listed countries within the past 5 years are exempt.
- Materials: 3 reference letters, at least one from an academic referee, and referees must use an institutional email address; a statement of purpose of up to two pages; a CV; and a recorded video response to one interview question, which is not shared in advance. The international application fee is CAD $168.25.

## Admission Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has 15 DPs for UBC ECE MEng, all for fall intake, and none yet from 2025 onward:

| Intake | Admit | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2022 | 1 | 0 |
| Fall 2023 | 3 | 7 |
| Fall 2024 | 1 | 3 |

5 admits: 3 from Chinese 985 universities and 2 from US or other overseas undergrad programs, all in CS or software engineering. GPAs on a 4.0 scale were 3.92–3.99, plus one above 90 on a 100-point scale; the 3 admits who reported class rank were all in the top 3%. TOEFL 100–110; the 2 who submitted the GRE had 324 and 327.

10 rejects: 8 from mainland Chinese universities (Tsinghua/Peking, the "Huawu" group, 985 and 211 schools are all represented), plus 1 from Hong Kong and 1 from Canada. Most were CS majors, with some in software engineering, EE and automation. GPAs were 3.3–3.74 on a 4.0 scale and 86.5–90.9 on a 100-point scale, plus some on 4.3 and 5.0 scales; class rank ranged from the top 3% to below the top 50%. TOEFL 101–109, IELTS 7.0–7.5, and the 6 who submitted the GRE had 323–334.

The sample is small and the score ranges of admits and rejects overlap; some rejected mainland applicants ranked in the top 3%. One 2023 rejection note mentioned that the decision came very late.
