# USC CS37: Scientists and Engineers student perspectives

USC's [Computer Science for Scientists and Engineers](https://www.cs.usc.edu/academic-programs/masters/scientists-and-engineers/) serves science and engineering graduates with limited CS preparation. This page retains the historical CS37 label used by the sources and distinguishes it from general CS master's accounts.

## Use the coursework to close specific gaps

A Spring 2021 entrant valued some foundational classes but described limited elective choices and career support. A Fall 2022 entrant's retrospective notes that database concepts became more valuable after starting work.

Map your gaps in programming, algorithms, systems and databases before selecting courses. Aim to explain the design, implementation and debugging of your projects, not merely list their course titles.

## Extra time has a cost

The 2025 account describes another internship search opportunity and campus research when an industry internship did not materialize. These are individual paths, not guaranteed internship eligibility or placement rates. Similarly, the earlier author's honors and teaching experience does not establish an automatic GPA-to-job pathway.

Check prerequisite sequencing, your earliest eligible internship date and the cost of the entire degree. Budget without assistantship or internship income until it is secured.
