# USC CS37: Scientists and Engineers student perspectives

## Start with the sequence of foundational courses

CS37, formally Computer Science for Scientists and Engineers, serves students with science or engineering backgrounds and limited CS preparation. Its 37 units include CSCI 455x, a four-unit programming course required in the first semester that sits outside the other 33 degree units. The foundation also includes CSCI 402 Operating Systems, EE 450 networks or EE 457 computer organization, and CSCI 570 algorithms. These courses occupy a substantial part of a career changer's schedule, leaving less elective space than the full CS catalog might suggest.

Complete 455x before planning 402. Practicing programming before arrival means one less thing to learn from scratch during the semester. Passing classes alone will not prepare you to explain implementation choices in interviews; data structures and additional practice still need attention.

## Courses worth taking seriously while recruiting

Experiences from the 2021–2022 entering cohorts suggest that the workload could leave room for recruiting, although the value of the coursework depended on course choices. CSCI 571 Web Technologies is an option for students lacking application projects. CSCI 585 Database Systems is also worth connecting to actual development: completing a database assignment and explaining queries, data organization and design tradeoffs are different levels of understanding.

For a career changer, completing a coherent project in one area is a useful starting point. For SDE roles, be ready to discuss the code, tests and debugging. If systems interest you, continue with networks and operating systems. Sampling everything can leave a long course list but little you can discuss in depth.

## More time in school also means more expense

A longer program lets you spread foundational study, projects and applications across semesters. Campus labs are another possibility when an industry internship does not materialize; programming skills may be useful to a research project. Read departmental vacancy emails and approach faculty early with concrete examples of your work and the time you can commit.

TA and RA funding requires an actual appointment, so it should not be deducted from the budget in advance. Honors and teaching experiences from around 2023 do not establish a present-day formula in which a particular GPA guarantees a job. CS37 already entails additional coursework, and housing and living expenses continue throughout the degree.

LA offers plenty of food and weekend activities, but Koreatown, the Chinese communities and the campus area have different travel patterns. Compare Downtown LA, campus-area housing and more distant options together with the commute. During a busy job search, convenient housing and a routine for meals and rest may matter more than the number of weekend destinations.

## Plan CPT under the current rules

As of October 11, 2026, USC OIS requires practical training to be mandatory for all students in the program; an ordinary elective internship does not qualify. CS37's published requirements do not list an internship required of everyone, and ENGR 597/598 do not count toward the degree, so a longer program should no longer be presented as guaranteeing two CPT internships.
