# Online MSCS Programs

Gatech OMSCS — very affordable tuition, very high acceptance rate (roughly 85%+), good for career changers to CS

UIUC OMSCS

UT Austin OMSCS

Stanford OMSCS (reportedly has the same admission threshold as the on-campus MSCS; recommended for local applicants; the diploma is identical)

Purdue OMSCS

JHU OMSECS

Columbia CVN (course selection is top-tier, and you can access the on-campus library — virtually no difference from on-campus students)

Boston OMSCS

UPenn MAS-CS Online (formerly MCIT Online) — favors career changers with high GPAs; you cannot take more than two CS courses. For Fall 2027 the priority deadline is March 1, 2027 and the final deadline is May 31
