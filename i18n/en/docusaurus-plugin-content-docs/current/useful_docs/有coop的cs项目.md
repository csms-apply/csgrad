# CS Programs With Co-op

Gatech MSCS

Gatech MS CSE — no need to transfer into the MSCS program; CSE is co-managed by CoC and inherently comes with co-op

Duke ECE MEng — starting from Fall 2024, the program announced it offers co-op

JHU MSECS (not many people know about this one, but a friend emailed JHU and confirmed that co-op is available)

UCSD ECE / CS75 (ECE can do co-op; CS75 has also offered co-op since 2025)

NYU Tandon MSCS (can co-op in the spring)

UT Austin ECE (SES) — can co-op and can extend graduation

UIUC MCS (main campus, Chicago, and even spring enrollment are the same; non-US undergrads can apply for co-op after 1 year)

UIUC ECE

UW EE PMP (can do full-time co-op in the fall without extending graduation)

TAMU MCS

NEU MSCS

NEU CS Align

NEU IS
