# CMU MSAIE

## Program Overview

MSAIE stands for Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence Engineering, a family of degrees from CMU's College of Engineering. It is not the same thing as [CMU MSAII](../S/CMU%20MSAII.md): MSAII is a four-semester program run by LTI in the School of Computer Science (SCS), while MSAIE sits inside individual College of Engineering departments, and you apply to a specific department's version.

The College of Engineering currently lists 10 versions: BME, ChemE, CEE, CMU-Africa, ECE, EST&P, ETIM, INI Information Security, MSE and MechE. Every version shares four core courses: systems and tool chains for AI, machine learning for engineers, deep learning, and trustworthy AI. This page covers only the two most relevant to CS applicants, the ECE version and INI's MSAIE-IS; they are also the only two versions in csgrad's data.

- ECE version: MS in Artificial Intelligence Engineering–Electrical and Computer Engineering, three semesters, 97 units, fall entry only
- MSAIE-IS: run by INI, launched in 2022, all students start in fall. Standard is three semesters and 123 units, Advanced is four semesters and 159 units; from Fall 2027 INI adds Applied (three semesters, 126 units) and Applied Advanced (four semesters, 162 units)

Both versions are in Pittsburgh. For 2026-27, College of Engineering master's tuition is $30,755 per semester ($61,510 per academic year), and INI's tuition page gives the same figure. The university estimates housing, food, insurance, books and other non-tuition costs at $37,374 a year. INI notes that tuition for future years will likely go up.

## Courses

ECE version (requirements for students entering Fall 2024 and later):

- AI core, 48 units, one course from each of four areas: 18-763 Systems and Tool Chains for AI Engineering, 18-661 Intro to ML for Engineers, 18-786 or 18-794 (deep learning), and 18-813 Fundamentals of Responsible AI. The official sequence is ML and Systems in the first semester, deep learning in the second, Responsible AI in the third
- AI domain electives, 36 units, with at least 12 units from application courses such as signal processing, image and video processing, and speech recognition; the rest can include 18-613 Foundations of Computer Systems, 18-660 Optimization, 18-667 large-scale distributed ML, and others
- General electives, 12 units: 600-level or higher courses from ECE, SCS departments, Statistics, Tepper and other approved departments
- 18-989 Introduction to Graduate Studies, 1 unit; up to 27 units of research projects can count

Truly free electives are only 12 units; most of the schedule is tied to the AI course lists.

MSAIE-IS (2026-27 entry, MS38): the core totals 105 units: AI core 48 (14-763, deep learning 18-786 or 24-788, 24-784 Trustworthy and Ethical AI Engineering, and one of three ML courses), security core 36 (14-741, 14-795 AI Applications in Information Security, plus 12 units of security courses), 14-789 AI in Business Modeling 6, a networking course 12 (14-740 or 14-760), and 14-601 3. That leaves 18 units of electives; MSIN, also an INI Standard program, is 75 core + 48 electives.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

ECE version: the 3-unit 18-994 internship course can count toward the general electives. ECE's FAQ for incoming MS students says CPT internships can only be done in the summer; an internship during fall or spring requires pre-completion OPT and keeping at least 36 units. A three-semester program gets a three-semester I-20, which cannot be extended. ECE's MS programs do not currently allow co-ops.

MSAIE-IS: in Standard and Advanced, an internship is an optional 3-unit elective, charged at the per-unit rate in summer ($855 per unit for 2026-27). INI's course page says international students must be eligible for pre-completion OPT to do this; international students who start in fall may be eligible in their first summer. From Fall 2027, students in Applied or Applied Advanced must complete a summer internship after their first academic year to graduate. You find the internship yourself; INI does not guarantee one.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Pittsburgh, far from both the Bay Area and Seattle.

From INI's outcomes page: the 2025 MSAIE-IS median first full-time salary was $130,000 (only five graduates reported salaries), with employers listed as Archer, Auditoria, ByteDance and Confluent, and locations listed as San Francisco, Phoenix, Austin and Chicago. The 2026 summer internship median hourly pay was $45, with Wipro and BNY listed as employers. The ECE MS AIE pages do not give separate outcome data.

## Application Requirements

ECE version (Fall 2027):

- Deadlines: ECE's deadline page lists the fall MS early review deadline as December 15 and the final deadline as January 15, 11:59 p.m. ET
- GRE: optional for Fall 2027
- English: TOEFL total 86, with reading, listening and writing at least 22 each and speaking at least 20; IELTS 7 (reading and listening 6.5, speaking and writing 6); DET 115; TOEFL MyBest scores are not accepted
- 3 recommendation letters, SOP and resume, $75 application fee
- Background: Python, probability and statistics, linear algebra
- One ECE application can rank up to three programs, so you can apply to MS ECE in the same application

MSAIE-IS (Fall 2027):

- Deadlines: priority December 1, 2026; final January 29, 2027; 11:59 p.m. ET
- GRE: not required, and not reviewed if sent
- English: no published minimum, and TOEFL Best Score reports are accepted. The FAQ says the most successful applicants usually have TOEFL section scores of 25 or higher, IELTS 7.5 or higher, or DET above 120
- 3 recommendation letters, with INI preferring one professional and two academic; 3 required essays plus 1 optional, 300 words each at most; a 3-minute video answering a random prompt; a resume of up to two pages; $75 application fee
- The INI application lets you list a first and second choice; INI and ECE are separate applications with separate fees
