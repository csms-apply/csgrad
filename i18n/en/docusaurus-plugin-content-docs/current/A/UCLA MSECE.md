# UCLA MSECE

## Program Overview

This is the Master of Science (M.S.) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the ECE department in UCLA's Samueli School of Engineering. Its Graduate Division major code is 0333. It is a separate program from [UCLA MEng](../A+/UCLA%20MENG.md) in the same school, with its own application.

- Length: at most 2 academic years (6 quarters); the department says students finish in 5 quarters on average. Graduate Division statistics put the average time to a master's degree at 1.71 years for degrees awarded from 2018-19 through 2022-23
- Entry: fall only, on the Westwood campus in Los Angeles
- Cost: on the Registrar's 2026-27 fee schedule, an Academic Master student who is not a California resident pays about $38,330 for one academic year (3 quarters), including $13,722 tuition, $15,102 nonresident supplemental tuition and about $7,517 for the UCSHIP student health plan
- Funding: the department does not fund MS students, and TA or GSR positions are not guaranteed; you can look for them in other departments on your own. International admits must show more than $55,000 in funding for the first year
- STEM: neither the department nor the Dashew Center for international students lists this degree's CIP code. The STEM OPT extension requires a DHS-designated STEM degree, so check the CIP code on your I-20 against the DHS list

## Courses and Course Selection

New students start in the Capstone – Courses plan by default. Switching to either of the other two plans needs an ECE faculty member who agrees to supervise you:

- Capstone – Courses: 9 four-unit, letter-graded courses, at least 5 of them formal ECE graduate courses (200-level lectures) and the other 4 electives, plus the ECE 297 seminar
- Capstone – Project: 8 courses (again at least 5 from ECE), plus ECE 299 in your final quarter for a project with a written report and a presentation
- Thesis: 7 courses (at least 4 from ECE) plus at least 8 units of ECE 598, with a committee of at least 3 members. The department says this plan suits students headed for research or a PhD

Electives can come from 10 approved departments, including CS, Math, Statistics, Physics and MAE. A course from any other department needs a petition, and at most one such course counts. You can also use one upper-division undergraduate course in place of one elective. Full-time enrollment is 12 units per quarter, and your GPA must stay at 3.0 or above. You cannot take MS Online courses in fall, winter or spring; in summer you can ask the MS Online program for permission.

The department lists 13 suggested tracks, which are advisory rather than required. For example, Computer Systems Architecture & Design asks for 2 core courses out of ECE 201A, ECE M216A and CS 251A; the core courses for Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Machine Learning are ECE 241A Stochastic Processes and ECE 246 Foundations of Statistical Machine Learning.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

Dashew Center rules: CPT requires the internship to be a required or integral part of your curriculum, and you must stay enrolled in the matching internship course while you work. In fall, winter and spring, CPT can only be part time (up to 20 hours a week); full-time CPT is only allowed in summer.

The ECE MS requirements do not include an internship course, so ask the department first whether CPT is possible. Another route is pre-completion OPT, but any time you use is deducted from the 12 months of OPT after graduation, with part-time use deducted at a 50% rate.

## Jobs and Location

UCLA is in Westwood, Los Angeles. Neither the department nor the Graduate Division publishes placement data for this master's, so no numbers are given here. Graduate Division statistics for Fall 2019 through Fall 2023 show that, across MS and PhD students in this major, 72% were international and 52% were pursuing a master's.

## Application Requirements

- Deadline: December 15, 2026 at 11:59 pm Pacific Time for Fall 2027. Letters are due the same day; late letters are accepted until January 1, 2027 at 5:00 pm, and anything arriving after that is not guaranteed a review. Decisions are emailed in mid-to-late April
- GRE: not required for the 2027-28 cycle, and submitted scores get no extra weight
- English: TOEFL 87 or IELTS 7.0, no Duolingo, and you must take the test by December 15; a degree from the US or from a country where English is the sole language of instruction exempts you
- GPA: at least 3.0/4.0; the department says the most competitive applicants usually have 3.5 or higher
- At least 3 recommendation letters; MS applicants are advised to ask professors who taught them in junior and senior years, and those who graduated two or more years ago can use professional references
- Statements: a Statement of Purpose of up to 2 pages, an optional Personal Statement, and a required resume
- You cannot apply to the MS if you already hold or are enrolled in a master's in ECE or a similar field; each applicant may apply to only one program and one degree objective
- PhD applicants who are not admitted are automatically reconsidered for the MS
- Graduate Division statistics for Fall 2019 through Fall 2023 show an average of 1,718 applicants and 484 admits a year, a 28% admit rate, for MS and PhD combined

## Admissions Data (csgrad DP)

csgrad has only 1 data point for the UCLA ECE master's so far: an admit for Fall 2024.

The applicant had a bachelor's from a mainland China university in the "Huawu" top-five group, in a CS-related interdisciplinary major, a GPA of 3.96/4, GRE 330 and TOEFL 107, with research experience in China and abroad plus one internship in China. One data point is not enough to show an admissions bar.
