# UofT MScAC

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Master of Science in Applied Computing, offered by the Department of Computer Science at the University of Toronto. It is full-time and in person, with no part-time option and a fall (September) intake only. The program says almost 500 students have graduated so far.

It runs 16 months over 4 terms. The calendar lists the registration sequence as fall, winter, summer, fall: courses in the first fall and winter terms (September to April), then an eight-month applied research internship in industry from May to December.

You apply to a concentration. There are currently 7: Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare, Applied Mathematics, Data Science for Biology and Quantum Computing.

This is a Canadian program, so US STEM designation does not apply. To work in Canada after graduating you go through the PGWP; the official FAQ says every MScAC student who has applied for a PGWP so far has received a three-year permit.

Cost: the program's estimate is for the cohort starting September 2026. Tuition and fees for the whole 16 months come to about CAD 90,050 for international students and about CAD 33,325 for domestic students, including a CAD 2,200 mandatory fee for pre-program activities (May–August before you start). The university posts tuition in mid-July each year, and it may change.

## Courses

Under the 2026-27 graduate calendar, coursework totals 3.0 FCE (one H course is 0.5 FCE):

- Two required professional courses: CSC2701H Communication for Computer Scientists and CSC2702H Technical Entrepreneurship, 1.0 FCE in total
- The remaining 2.0 FCE, i.e. four graduate courses, picked from the MSc/PhD course schedule according to your concentration's rules

Some examples: Computer Science requires 1.5 FCE from two different course groups, with at most one course from group 2, and the remaining 0.5 FCE cannot come from group 2 either; Artificial Intelligence requires 1.5 FCE of AI courses, including 1.0 FCE of core AI courses covering at least two research areas; Data Science is 1.0 FCE of STA courses plus 1.0 FCE of CSC courses; Quantum Computing requires 1.0 FCE of graduate physics courses. Course choices are made in consultation with the relevant concentration lead or program lead.

## Internship / CPT / Co-op

Students apply for internship positions themselves, with a list of postings provided by the program. If a particular area matters to you, ask about the interview and matching process.

The internship is CSC2703H: eight months, coordinated by the department, graded pass/fail, and required for the degree. The FAQ says interns work 35–40 hours a week, depending on the company.

The program does not place students. Partner organizations provide pre-approved applied research projects, and students interview with the companies themselves. The program also runs an Internship Expo and offers individual search support. The FAQ recommends starting the search as soon as you arrive, and says most internship-finding activity happens in the winter term (January–April). During the internship you meet your academic supervisor at least six times, and at the end you present at the ARIA (Applied Research in Action) Showcase and submit a final research report.

The FAQ states that, to date, 100% of graduated students have secured an internship, with an average total salary of $65,000 per eight-month internship in 2026, while also saying remuneration cannot be guaranteed.

On immigration: the FAQ says that since April 1, 2026, a mandatory placement like this no longer needs a separate co-op work permit, and that graduate study permit applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026 no longer need a PAL.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Toronto. The program says more than 300 companies are represented among its partners. Besides Adobe, AMD, Google, Meta, Microsoft Turing, NVIDIA, Shopify and Tesla, the list includes banks and investment organizations (Bank of Canada, CIBC, Scotiabank, CPP Investments), hospitals (SickKids, UHN) and AI companies (Cohere, Vector Institute, Waabi).

You graduate with an eight-month full-time research role already on your resume. To stay in Canada, you use the PGWP mentioned above.

## Application Requirements

For the Fall 2027 cycle:

- Deadline: December 1, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET. The application fee is paid to SGS and the program cannot waive it
- Grades: at least B+ in the final year of undergrad (two terms), equivalent to UofT 77–79%; work experience helps but cannot replace grades
- GRE: optional, but strongly recommended for applicants without a Canadian degree, and the site says serious applicants from outside Canada are assumed to have taken it; averages are Verbal 158+ (80th percentile) and Quant 168+ (95th percentile); GMAT is not accepted
- English: the standard SGS minimums, with no higher program-specific bar. TOEFL iBT 93 overall with 22 in writing and 22 in speaking (on the 1–6 scale used since January 21, 2026: 4.5 overall, 4.5 writing, 4.0 speaking); IELTS Academic 7.0 with at least 6.5 in each band; tests must be taken within 24 months before you submit
- Letters: 3, from faculty and/or employers, ideally at least 2 academic
- Documents: a 1–2 page resume; no traditional SOP, replaced by the program's own short-answer questions
- Concentrations: rank your top three; the admissions committee makes the final call
- Interviews: rolling, December to April, technical, non-technical or both; not every applicant is interviewed, and no one is admitted without one. Offers usually go out between mid-December and April

The official FAQ says 5–10% of applicants receive an offer each year.

csgrad has 13 data points for UofT MScAC:

| Intake | Admit | Reject | No response |

| Fall 2024 | 1 | 5 | 1 |
| Fall 2023 | 2 | 2 | 0 |
| Fall 2022 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Total | 5 | 7 | 1 |

- All 5 admits did their undergrad in mainland China: 2 from C5 schools, 2 from 985 schools and 1 from a Sino-foreign joint institute, majoring in CS or AI. GPAs were 86.5–93.5 out of 100, plus one 3.92/4.0. All submitted TOEFL (107–110) and GRE (321–335; those who reported Quant had 169–170), and each listed at least 3 research experiences.
- Of the 8 rejections and no-responses, 6 were mainland China undergrads (3 from 985 schools, 1 from Tsinghua/Peking, 1 from a 211 school, 1 from UCAS/ShanghaiTech/SUSTech), plus 1 Canadian undergrad and 1 Hong Kong undergrad. GPAs were 3.21–3.92/4.0, 3.7/4.3 and 92/100, and 5 of them ranked in the top 3%–10%. Five did not report a GRE score; the 3 who did all had Quant 170.
- Notes on a few admits mention interview invitations in December and January, one interview running over an hour; the no-response case was interviewed and never heard back.

With only 13 data points this is a rough picture: a high GPA does not guarantee admission, and the admits were all mainland China applicants with GRE and TOEFL scores and a fair amount of research.

## Similar Programs

- [UofT ECE MEng](../B/UofT%20ECE%20MEng.md): a coursework master's at the same school. From Fall 2027 there is also a two-year co-op version, applied to separately with limited spots, that includes an eight-month paid work term; at MScAC everyone does the eight-month research internship.
- [Waterloo MDSAI](../B/Waterloo%20MDSAI.md): the co-op version is also 16 months with a fall-only intake, with one four-month work term in the third term.
- [Waterloo MMath CS](../A-/Waterloo%20MMath%20CS.md): a research (thesis) master's where full-time students typically get TA and GRS funding, worth comparing if you lean toward research.
- [UW EE PMP](../A/uw%20ee%20pmp.md): a US evening program; its site says international students can fit in internships quarter by quarter, unlike MScAC's single required internship.
