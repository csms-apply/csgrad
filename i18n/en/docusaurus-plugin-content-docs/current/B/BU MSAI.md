# BU MSAI

## Program Overview

Compare the AI-focused curriculum with the department's MSCS if you also want a broad grounding in systems and theory. A degree switch is possible only after meeting the applicable requirements; it is not automatic.

The full degree name is MS in Artificial Intelligence. It is offered by Boston University's Department of Computer Science and sits under the Graduate School of Arts & Sciences (GRS), with classes on campus in Boston. The coursework centers on machine learning, computer vision and natural language processing.

The department also runs a 100% online MS in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence with different deadlines. That is a separate program; this page covers the on-campus MS in AI.

The degree takes 8 courses, or 32 credits. The program page gives sample plans for both 2 and 3 semesters. The FAQ says you can take 4 courses a semester without overloading, and the department's master's students take 3 semesters on average. Admission is fall only, and the FAQ says spring admission is no longer offered. International students on a visa must be full-time in every semester except the last. The FAQ also says that once enrolled, you can switch between the department's four MS degrees as long as you complete the requirements.

On BU ISSO's STEM majors list (Fall 2026 edition), the Artificial Intelligence MS has CIP code 11.0102, which is STEM. The CIP code on your I-20 is what counts.

For 2026-27, full-time GRS master's tuition (12–18 credits a semester) is $36,512 per semester. With the student services and health & wellness fees it comes to $37,036 per semester, or $74,072 for the academic year. The student health insurance plan (SHIP) is extra; its standard 2026-27 rate is $4,054 a year. Every admitted student is automatically considered for merit scholarships. The FAQ says awards are generally $5,000–$30,000 per semester of full-time study, and recipients must stay full-time, keep a GPA of 3.0 or higher and earn B- or higher in their courses.

## Courses and Course Selection

The 8 courses are 4 core courses plus 4 electives.

- All 4 core courses are required: CS 505 Introduction to Natural Language Processing, CS 542 Principles of Machine Learning, CS 585 Image and Video Computing and CS 640 Artificial Intelligence. If you have already taken an equivalent course, you can substitute another elective with approval from your advisor and the Director of Graduate Studies.
- At least 1 elective must come from List A, which leans toward data and databases: CS 504 Data Mechanics, CS 506 Data Science Tools and Applications, CS 562 Advanced Database Applications, CS 565 Algorithmic Data Mining and CS 660 Graduate Introduction to Database Systems.
- The remaining electives are chosen with your advisor from List B (CAS CS courses numbered 500 and above) or List C (AI-related courses in other departments; the program page names Mathematics & Statistics and ECE, and these need approval from the Director of Graduate Studies).

The program page's 3-semester sample plan: CS 640, CS 660 and a Fairness in AI topics course in the first fall; CS 585, 505 and 542 in the spring; then Deep Learning and an AI Directed Study in the second fall.

A thesis is optional. You can do a faculty-supervised master's project and expand it into a thesis, which needs two faculty readers.

## Internships / CPT

The degree has no required internship.

In August 2026, SEVP narrowed how CPT can be approved. BU ISSO's CPT page now says it is pausing CPT processing for internships that are not required of all students in a program; those internships need pre-completion OPT instead. The MS in AI has no required internship, so under this rule its students cannot get CPT for now. That is the status as of October 2026, so check ISSO's latest notices before you enroll. Under the usual rules, CPT also requires one full academic year of full-time study first.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Boston. The official pages publish no employment data for this program, and the csgrad DP database has no job-search records for it yet.

The CS department's Careers page lists an Industry Connections information session open only to CS master's students and undergraduate seniors (the latest was on October 23, 2026). Other job-search resources go through the university's Center for Career Development and Handshake.

## Application Requirements

These are the Fall 2027 requirements as listed on BU's website in October 2026:

- Deadlines: priority January 15, 2027; regular March 15, 2027. To be in the first round of review, submit a complete application by January 15. That round gets priority for admission and merit scholarships, and the site strongly recommends international applicants apply by January 15. The department's admissions page says MS in AI applications are still reviewed on a space-available basis after the deadline
- GRE: not required; you may submit scores. Admitted students who submitted the GRE averaged the 80th percentile in Quant and the 60th in Verbal
- English: GRS recommends TOEFL iBT 100 (5.0 for the new TOEFL score reports from January 2026), IELTS 7.0 or Duolingo 130. These are listed as recommended scores, not hard minimums. MyBest scores are not accepted
- Materials: 2 recommendation letters, a personal statement, a resume and unofficial transcripts (official ones only after admission); the application fee is $95
- Background: the program is aimed at CS undergraduates, but it also takes applicants with equivalent training or experience, or with gaps in their computing background and strong academic records. Applicants missing one or two of algorithms, computer systems, programming languages and theory may be admitted with prerequisite courses. An undergraduate GPA of 3.0 or higher (4.0 scale) is recommended but not required

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [BU MSCS](../B/BU%20MSCS.md): the same department's general CS master's, with the same tuition, deadlines and application materials. The general MSCS track asks for one additional applications course, and any course that counts for the MS in AI automatically satisfies it; once enrolled you can switch degrees if you meet the requirements.
- [NEU MSCS](../B/NEU%20MSCS.md): also in Boston, and NEU has co-op. The BU MS in AI has no required internship and CPT got tighter in 2026, so compare the two if internship options matter to you.
- [Waterloo MDSAI](../B/Waterloo%20MDSAI.md): another coursework master's in data science and AI, in Canada, with one co-op term.
