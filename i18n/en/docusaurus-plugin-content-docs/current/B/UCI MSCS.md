# UCI MSCS

## Program Overview

The degree is the M.S. in Computer Science, offered by the Department of Computer Science in UC Irvine's Donald Bren School of Information and Computer Sciences (ICS). The department describes it as a research-oriented master's, separate from its professional master's, the MCS; the 2026-27 catalogue says the two master's programs together have 200+ students.

- Start term: ICS admits for Fall only
- Length: quarter system, main Irvine campus, at least 47 units. The ICS FAQ says most M.S. students finish in about 2 years; international students cannot study part-time
- Cost: on the Registrar's 2026-27 fee page, self-supporting master's programs such as MCS and MSWE have their own fee schedules; CS MS is not among them and is charged the standard fees for state-supported graduate programs. For 2026-27, a nonresident pays $38,784.44 for the three quarters, including $13,722 tuition, $15,102 nonresident supplemental tuition and $7,771.86 student health insurance; new students also pay an $87 document fee
- Funding: the ICS FAQ says M.S. students can apply for TA and GSR (graduate student researcher) positions, while the goal of full funding applies only to PhD admits. TA positions require a separate application every quarter
- STEM: the ICS FAQ answers "Yes" to whether ICS programs qualify for STEM CPT/OPT

## Courses

The 2026-27 UCI catalogue says the M.S. course requirements are the same as the PhD's; only the completion plans differ. The 47 units break down into three parts:

- Three quarters of the COMPSCI 200S research seminar, 1 unit each, graded Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory only
- Four core courses: pick four of seven areas and take at least one course in each. Data Structures and Algorithms (260, 261, 263), Architecture/Embedded Systems (250A, 244), System Software (205 security, 241 compilers, 230 distributed systems), AI (271, 273A machine learning), Networks/Multimedia (232, 203), Database Systems (222, 223), Scientific and Visual Computing (206, 211A)
- Seven electives: any course from an ICS graduate program, except COMPSCI 290/298/299, courses with an S/W/P suffix, and non-COMPSCI 29x courses. At most 2 COMPSCI 295 special-topics courses and at most 2 approved upper-division undergraduate courses (operating systems, compilers, networks, machine learning and so on), and at most 2 from those two groups combined; at most 2 graduate courses from outside ICS, with written consent of the Vice Chair for Graduate Studies

Every course counted toward the degree needs a B or better. P-suffix courses such as 201P and 220P are restricted in the catalogue to MCS students (some also to MDS); M.S. students cannot take them and they do not count.

You finish under one of two plans:

- Plan I (thesis): only for students in good academic standing. Take at least two quarters of COMPSCI 298 (Thesis Supervision) in place of two courses; a three-member committee (the advisor plus two readers, with the advisor and at least one reader from the department) approves the thesis, and you give an oral presentation to the committee
- Plan II (comprehensive exam): after the coursework, take a comprehensive exam given by CS faculty that covers the core requirements

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The degree requirements include no internship or co-op. The UCI International Center's CPT page says CPT is only authorized for internships that are a required part of the degree; optional internships do not qualify. Among the graduate programs the page lists as having a required internship, the only ICS program is MSWE, not CS MS.

The other route is only for thesis-track master's students: after advancing to candidacy, the advisor writes a letter stating that the work is data collection the thesis requires, with no alternative method. So under Plan II CPT basically does not fit, and under Plan I you still need your advisor's backing. Other conditions: at least one academic year of enrollment first; at most 20 hours a week while classes are in session, full-time allowed during breaks; and only employers in the U.S. qualify.

## Jobs and Location

The campus is in Irvine, in Orange County in Southern California. The CS department says M.S. graduates go on to PhD programs at UCI or elsewhere, or to research and development work in industry, startups, non-profits and government; the pages give no employment statistics for the M.S.

## Application Requirements

- Deadline (Fall 2027): the Graduate Division's program list gives December 15, 2026; the application opens in early October. Test scores, letters and other materials should all arrive by that date
- Background: a bachelor's degree in a related field by the time you enroll. The university GPA requirement is 3.0/4.0, but you can apply below that, and applications are reviewed holistically
- GRE: the Graduate Division's program list says CS does not require it
- English: TOEFL iBT 80 (4 on the new scale from January 2026), or IELTS 7 overall with no band below 6; scores two years old or older are not accepted. You are exempt if your bachelor's or master's is from a school WHED lists as English-only instruction. Being a TA has its own English requirement
- Materials: a required Statement of Purpose about your academic and research background and interests; a Personal History Statement, optional but highly recommended; transcripts from every institution you attended, uploaded; at least 3 letters, submitted online by your recommenders; a $155 application fee for international applicants
- You cannot apply to both the M.S. and the PhD in the same program; you can apply to several ICS programs, each with its own application, fee and materials

## Admissions Results (csgrad DPs)

csgrad has 5 self-reported UCI MSCS records, all for Fall entry:

- 2023 Fall: 1 admit
- 2025 Fall: 3 rejects
- 2026 Fall: 1 reject
- No 2024 Fall records yet

That is 1 admit and 4 rejects in total. Four applicants came from mainland Chinese universities (one 985, two 211, one Sino-foreign joint program) and one from a U.S. university; majors were CS (2), AI (2) and information security (1). GPAs on a 4.0 scale ranged 3.31–3.9 (4 records), and one was 88 on a 100-point scale; the 3 who reported GRE scores had 320–331, TOEFL was 101–109 (3 records), and one had IELTS 7. Four of the five had research experience.

The only admit was in 2023: their GPA, GRE and TOEFL were the highest among those who reported scores, with overseas summer research and a first-author paper under review, who ended up enrolling. All 4 records from 2025 on are rejects, including someone ranked in the top 10% with several strong letters and someone who reported a first-author paper at a top venue. Rejections came in mid-to-late March; the admit came in early March. The sample is very small, so treat it as a rough picture only.
