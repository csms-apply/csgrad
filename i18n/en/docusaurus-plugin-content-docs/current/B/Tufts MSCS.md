# Tufts MSCS

## Program Overview

The full degree name is M.S. in Computer Science. It is offered by the Tufts Department of Computer Science, which sits in the School of Engineering, and admissions run through the joint graduate admissions office of the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences and the School of Engineering. Classes are on the Medford/Somerville campus in Greater Boston. Tufts also runs a separate fully online MS in Computer Science; this page covers only the on-campus program.

The degree is 30 credits. The official page puts the average time to finish at 12–24 months, full-time or part-time. You can start in fall or spring; there is no summer entry.

The program page does not say whether the degree is STEM and gives no CIP code. The Tufts International Center's STEM OPT page tells students to go by the CIP code on their I-20.

Costs for 2026-27: School of Engineering graduate courses are $1,855 per credit, so tuition for 30 credits is $55,650. The 2026-27 cost of attendance table also lists a Health and Wellness Fee of $1,288, a student activity fee of $62 ($31 per semester), and Tufts health insurance at $4,976 (students with comparable coverage can request a waiver in SIS). The program page says the School of Engineering offers partial-tuition merit scholarships; every applicant is considered automatically, with no separate application.

## Courses

- At least 10 courses, each worth 3 or more credits. At least 24 of the 30 credits must come from approved courses; the remaining 6 can be approved courses, research, independent study, or a mix.
- At least 6 courses must be in computer science, and at least 2 must have a serious programming component. Courses outside CS count only with department approval.
- You have to show core competence in four areas: computer architecture and assembly language, programming languages, data structures and analysis of algorithms, and theory of computation. Which courses cover each area is spelled out in the department's Handbook Supplement.
- Every course needs a B- or better to count, and students who entered from January 2019 on must also keep a B average.
- The thesis is optional. You can do one only after you matriculate and find a faculty advisor willing to supervise, and it takes two semesters.

The department's graduate course list (courses offered in the past five years) includes CS 111 Operating Systems, CS 115 Database Systems, CS 118 Cloud Computing, CS 119 Big Data, CS 121 Software Engineering, CS 135 Introduction to Machine Learning and CS 137 Deep Neural Networks. Check each term's schedule for what is actually offered.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The degree has no required internship.

The School of Engineering runs a Graduate Cooperative Education Program: up to six months of full-time work, at least 35 hours a week, in a single position. The work has to be engineering work; research, grading, teaching and thesis writing don't count. Before you start the job search you need 18 credits (seminar credits excluded), the Career Center's preparatory training, and an Intent to Co-Op Form submitted by the deadline. You become eligible by indicating interest in your admissions application or by applying during your first semester. While working you register for a 0-credit co-op course and stay full-time, and afterwards you return to campus for at least one semester to finish the degree. International students must meet with the International Center to confirm their visa status is current. The International Center's CPT page says students on a school-approved co-op can get full-time CPT and are not enrolled in courses that semester.

Internships outside the co-op: the International Center's CPT page currently says it cannot process CPT for non-required internships, without saying when this started. CPT also normally requires two semesters of full-time study first. This is what the page said in October 2026; check the International Center's latest guidance before you enroll.

## Jobs and Location

Geographically this is Greater Boston; the campus is in Medford/Somerville. The official program page publishes no employment outcomes for this program, and csgrad's DP database has no job-search records for it yet.

## Application Requirements

These are the requirements on the program and admissions pages as of October 2026. The deadlines are listed by month and day only, with no year:

- Deadlines: fall entry January 15 (priority) and April 15 (regular), then rolling through August 1; spring entry October 1 (priority) and December 1 (final). If you need a new student visa, apply by the regular deadline for fall and by the priority deadline for spring. Applications are reviewed in the order they are completed, so apply early
- GRE: not required if you will hold a U.S. or Canadian degree by enrollment; required for everyone else, so applicants from mainland Chinese universities should plan on it
- English: the program page lists "competitive" scores, not hard minimums: TOEFL 100 (R26/L26/W22/S25), IELTS 7.5 with at least 7.0 on each band, Duolingo 120
- Materials: resume, personal statement, transcripts, 3 letters of recommendation, optional portfolio
- Application fee: normally $85. From October 3, 2026, the fee is waived for applications to any 2027 entry term
