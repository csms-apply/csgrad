# UVA MSDS

## Program Overview

This is the in-person data science master's at the University of Virginia (UVA) School of Data Science. The degree is the M.S. in Data Science. The school also runs a part-time online version for working professionals; this page covers only the residential program.

- Length: officially 11 months and 33 credits, full-time, with daytime in-person classes
- Start term: fall only. The whole cohort follows one integrated schedule; the FAQ says the program runs from August to the following July, and applications open once a year
- Campus: Charlottesville, Virginia
- STEM: the FAQ says the program is STEM-designated, so graduates can get 12 months of OPT plus the 24-month STEM extension
- Size: the Class of 2027 has 100 students; 13% are international, the middle 50% undergraduate GPA is 3.3–3.9, and 36% had work experience before enrolling
- Cost (2026-27, counting fall, spring and summer): for non-Virginians, tuition is $55,368 and fees are $5,234; the school estimates living expenses at $36,960, for a total of $97,562. F-1 students are charged out-of-state rates plus a $120 administrative fee each semester. Accepting the offer requires a $500 non-refundable deposit, which is applied to first-semester tuition
- Funding: the FAQ says UVA does not offer financial aid to international students. It also says students may be eligible for a Quantitative Foundation Fellowship (paid across fall and spring, contingent on continuous enrollment and good academic standing), without saying whether international students qualify. The school has on-campus jobs such as Instructional Assistant and Grader, 2–20 hours a week; they are paid hourly, do not come with tuition remission and are not guaranteed

## Courses and Course Selection

The curriculum listed on the school's academics page:

- Fall, 12 credits: DS 6001 Data Engineering I, DS 5030 Understanding Uncertainty, DS 5012 Computation for Data Science, DS 6021 Machine Learning I
- Spring, 12 credits: DS 6002 Ethics of Big Data, DS 6030 Machine Learning II, DS 6050 Machine Learning III: Deep Learning, plus one elective; there is also a non-credit capstone preparation workshop
- Summer, 9 credits: DS 6015 Data Science Capstone, DS 5110 Data Engineering II: Big Data Systems, plus one elective; the degree is conferred in August

Electives total at least 6 credits, generally at the 5000 level or above, and are chosen with the program director. Examples include DS 6040 Bayesian Machine Learning and DS 6425 Time Series Modeling.

Each of the four concentrations consists of two required electives plus a related capstone, with graduation in the summer. The AI concentration takes DS 5001 Text as Data and DS 6051 Decoding Large Language Models. AI and Data Visualization are declared at the end of the first term; Genomics and Sports Data Science have limited seats and must be applied for as part of the admissions application.

The capstone is a team project sponsored by a company or government organization, in which students build a data product from start to finish.

## Internships / CPT

The curriculum has an internship track: you do a pre-approved internship over the summer instead of the capstone, then come back in the fall for DS 5110 and two electives, taught online with the option to attend in person, and receive the degree in December. Students on this track pay the fall semester at that year's tuition rates. Students in a concentration cannot take this track.

The school's pages do not explain how international students get CPT for this internship. If you plan to take the internship track, check with the school and UVA's international student office after you are admitted.

## Jobs and Location

Official employment statistics for the residential Class of 2025 (89% reporting): 74% employed, 6% in further education, 3% in military service, 11% still seeking. The median salary is $87K with a range of $37K–200K; these salary figures include full-time roles, internships, fellowships and part-time jobs. Employers named on the page include Capital One, Deloitte, American Express, PwC, Visa and AT&T, and destination cities include Washington, DC, New York, San Francisco, Seattle and Chicago.

## Application Requirements

Fall 2027 applications open on August 15, 2026. All deadlines are 11:59 p.m. Eastern:

| Round | Application deadline | Recommendations and transcripts | Decision release | Offer response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Priority | 2026-12-15 | 2026-12-22 | 2027-02-01 | 2027-03-01 |
| Regular | 2027-03-01 | 2027-03-08 | 2027-03-15 | 2027-04-15 |
| Rolling | 2027-06-15 | 2027-06-22 | Rolling, May–July | Stated in the offer |

- GRE: not required, and not reviewed if submitted
- English: waived if your undergraduate degree was taught in English; an English-taught graduate degree does not count. TOEFL 100, with Speaking 22, Writing 22, Reading 23, Listening 23; for the new TOEFL taken after January 21, 2026, the minimum is 5 overall, with Speaking 4, Writing 4.5, Reading 4.5, Listening 5. IELTS 7.0, with Reading and Listening 7.0 and Speaking and Writing 6.5. Duolingo 130, with Reading and Writing 130 and Speaking and Listening 125. Scores must be within two years of the application deadline. Students who enroll with test scores must also take UVA's English Language Proficiency Exam (UVELPE) before their first term
- Recommendations: 2. If you are a current or recent student, one must come from a professor, instructor or TA; if you are working, one should come from a current or recent supervisor
- Essays: there is no separate SOP. Instead there are two required short answers (up to 250 words, and 150–250 words) and one optional one, plus a 45-second audio response in place of an interview
- Prerequisites: Calculus II, Linear Algebra, Programming and Statistics. You can apply before finishing them, but they must be completed before you enroll; the school offers online bootcamps for calculus, linear algebra and programming
- Application fee: $85
