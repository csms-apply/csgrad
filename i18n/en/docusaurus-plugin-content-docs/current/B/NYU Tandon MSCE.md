# NYU Tandon MSCE

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Master of Science in Computer Engineering. It sits in the Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) department at NYU Tandon, whose campus is at MetroTech Center in downtown Brooklyn. It is a separate program from Tandon's MSCS, so make sure you pick the right one when applying.

The degree is 30 credits. The official sample plan of study runs 4 semesters: 9 credits in each of the first three, then 3 credits for a project in the last one.

You can start in fall or spring. Computer Science MS is on the Tandon admissions page's list of programs that do not take spring applications; Computer Engineering MS is not.

The degree's CIP code is 14.0901 (Computer Engineering, General), which is on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List.

Tuition is charged per credit. The NYU Bursar lists Tandon graduate tuition for 2026-27 at $2,626 per credit, so 30 credits at that rate comes to about $78,780. That is tuition only, not other fees or living costs.

## Courses and Course Selection

The 30 credits in the 2026-2027 Bulletin break down as:

- 2 core courses (6 credits), chosen from Internet Architecture & Protocols, Advanced Hardware Design, Introduction to VLSI System Design, Real Time Embedded Systems, and Computing Systems Architecture
- 15 credits of ECE-GY electives
- 6 credits of free electives, which can be ECE courses or courses from another NYU science, engineering or management department
- 3 credits of Advanced Project, which can be replaced by an MS thesis

A few limits: at least 24 of the 30 credits must be ECE-GY courses; 5000-level courses from other departments count only with the Program Director's approval, and 5000-level ECE courses plus non-ECE courses together can't exceed 12 credits; you need a graduate GPA of 3.0 or above, and the two core courses must also average 3.0. No transfer credits are accepted.

The program page gives three sample tracks: AI, Software and Robotics. The AI track lists courses like Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Data Center and Cloud Computing. Some ROB-GY robotics courses can count as ECE electives. CS-GY 6843 Computer Networking only counts toward the degree with the Program Director's approval.

## Internships / CPT

The Bulletin says most international students must register for an internship course, CP-GY 9911 or 9921, to do an internship. Each is 1.5 credits, up to 3 credits total, and they can count as ECE electives. The position has to be relevant to computer engineering, an ECE faculty member has to agree to supervise it, and you submit a report at the end. Once you have finished all degree requirements, international students can no longer be approved for an internship course.

Thesis, projects, readings and internships together can't exceed 9 credits, and if you have already taken more than 7.5 credits of these independent studies, an internship won't be approved. If you want both research and an internship, plan your credits early.

## Admission Requirements

The following is from the Tandon graduate admissions pages as of October 2026. The deadlines page doesn't say which admission cycle it covers, so check again before submitting a Fall 2027 application.

- Deadlines: fall priority deadline December 1, fall deadline February 15; spring November 1
- GRE: optional; no Tandon graduate program requires it
- English: TOEFL 90, IELTS 7.0, Duolingo 125
- GPA: undergraduate minimum of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale
- Recommendation letters: optional for master's applicants
- Materials: both personal essay prompts are required, plus a résumé; the application fee is $90
- Background: a bachelor's in CE, EE or CS is required; applicants from other majors need to make up topics such as logic circuits, computer architecture, data structures and algorithms, and C/C++

Backgrounds, roughly: mostly mainland China undergrads (985/211/top-five, non-985/211, Sino-foreign schools and others all appear), plus one US undergrad and one continental European undergrad. Most majored in CS, with others in EE/ECE, AI, software engineering and information security. GPAs on a 4.0 scale ranged from 2.9 to 3.88; percentage-scale GPAs were 86.9 and 91.8. TOEFL 99–109, IELTS 7.0. 9 of the 13 DPs include GRE scores, 318–332, with Quant 168–170. 4 DPs are marked as funded, and the two that give an amount both say $5,000.

Across the 2024 and 2025 cycles, 5 Tandon MSCS applicants in csgrad were advised by the school to switch to CE or were moved to CE: 4 got a CE admit, and 1 chose not to switch. One 2024 CE admit is recorded only in the notes of an MSCS entry, so it isn't counted in the table above; that note mentions a $6,000 scholarship. The switch notices mostly came in February–March, and CE admits mostly came in March, with one arriving in late April.
