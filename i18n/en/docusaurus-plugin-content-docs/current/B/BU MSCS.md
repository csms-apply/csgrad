# BU MSCS

## Program Overview

Statistics electives differ in theoretical depth, assignments and projects; discuss your choices with an adviser. Budget for tuition and living costs in Boston as well.

The full degree name is Master of Science in Computer Science. It is offered by Boston University's Department of Computer Science under the Graduate School of Arts & Sciences (GRS). The department is at 665 Commonwealth Avenue in Boston.

BU has more than one CS master's. Metropolitan College (MET) runs a separate MS in Computer Science: 40 credits (32 for students exempted from the foundation courses), taught in the evenings on campus. The CS department also offers an online MS in Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence. This page covers only the department's on-campus MSCS.

The degree takes 8 courses, or 32 credits. The official FAQ says you can take 4 courses a semester without overloading and finish in one year; the average student takes 3 semesters. Admission is fall only, and the FAQ says spring admission is no longer offered. International students on a visa must be full-time in every semester except the last.

On BU ISSO's STEM majors list (Fall 2026 edition), this degree's CIP code is 11.0701, which is STEM. The CIP code on your I-20 is what counts.

For 2026-27, full-time GRS master's tuition is $36,512 per semester. With the two mandatory fees it comes to $37,036 per semester, or $74,072 for the academic year. The student health insurance plan (SHIP) is a separate $4,054 a year. Admitted students are automatically considered for merit scholarships. The FAQ says awards are generally $5,000–$30,000 per semester of full-time study, and recipients must stay full-time, keep a GPA of 3.0 or higher and earn B- or better in every course.

## Courses and Course Selection

The 8 courses are 5 breadth courses plus 3 electives.

- Breadth must cover Theory, Systems, Software and Applications, with at least one course in each. The recommended courses are GRS CS 611 for Software, GRS CS 630 for Theory, and one of CAS CS 551, 552, 561 or GRS CS 651, 654, 655 for Systems. With advisor approval, a few CAS CS 5xx courses can stand in for Software and Theory.
- For Applications, take one application-oriented graduate course. Courses for the Cybersecurity or Data-Centric Computing specializations or the MS in AI count automatically.
- The 3 electives can be any graduate CS course (CAS CS 500–599 or GRS CS 600–999). Graduate courses in Mathematics & Statistics, the College of Engineering or Questrom School of Business can count with approval.

Grade rules: nothing below a B- counts toward the degree, and among the 5 breadth courses the number of B- grades cannot exceed the number of B+ or higher grades.

A thesis and a project are both optional. A project can be expanded into a thesis, which needs two faculty readers. The department also offers MSCS degrees with a Cyber Security or Data-Centric Computing specialization; this page covers the General Track with no specialization.

## Internships / CPT

The degree has no required internship. The department has an internship course, CAS CS 901 Internship in Computer Science, with variable credits, bi-weekly and final reports, and approval from the Director of the Master's Program. It can be taken only once.

On August 28, 2026, ISSO tightened CPT under new SEVP guidance. BU ISSO's CPT page now says it is pausing CPT processing for internships that are not required of all students in a program; those internships need pre-completion OPT instead. The MSCS has no required internship, so under this rule its students cannot get CPT for now. That is the status as of October 2026, so check ISSO's latest notices before you enroll. CPT also requires one full academic year of full-time study first.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Boston. The official pages publish no employment data for this program.

The CS department's Careers page lists Industry Connections information sessions open only to CS master's students and undergraduate seniors (the most recent was on October 23, 2026). Other job-search resources go through BU's Center for Career Development and Handshake.

## Application Requirements

These are the Fall 2027 requirements as listed on BU's website in October 2026:

- Deadlines: priority January 15, 2027; regular March 15, 2027. The department says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis until the deadline. Applications completed by January 15 go into the first-round review, which gets priority for admission and merit scholarships, and BU strongly recommends that international applicants apply by January 15
- GRE: not required; you may submit scores. Admitted students who submitted the GRE averaged the 80th percentile in Quant and the 60th in Verbal
- English: GRS recommends TOEFL iBT 100 (5.0 on the new TOEFL score reports from January 2026), IELTS 7.0 or Duolingo 130. These are listed as recommended scores, not hard minimums. MyBest scores are not accepted
- Materials: 2 recommendation letters, a personal statement, a resume and unofficial transcripts; the application fee is $95
- Background: the program expects a foundation in algorithms, computer systems, programming languages and theory. Applicants missing one or two of these may be admitted with prerequisite courses. An undergraduate GPA of 3.0 or higher (4.0 scale) is recommended, not required

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [BU MSAI](../B/BU%20MSAI.md): the same department's AI master's. MS in AI courses automatically count toward the MSCS Applications area, and the FAQ says that once enrolled you can switch between the department's four MS degrees if you meet the requirements.
- [NEU MSCS](../B/NEU%20MSCS.md): also in Boston, with a co-op. BU MSCS has no required internship and CPT got tighter in 2026, so compare the two if internship options matter to you.
- [Tufts MSCS](../B/Tufts%20MSCS.md): another MSCS in Greater Boston.
- [NEU CS ALIGN](../B/NEU%20CS%20ALIGN.md): a program for students switching into CS from other majors. BU MSCS expects a foundation in all four core CS areas.
- [UMass MSCS](../B/UMass%20MSCS.md): a public-university MSCS, also in Massachusetts.
