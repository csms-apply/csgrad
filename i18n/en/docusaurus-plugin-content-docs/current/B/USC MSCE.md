# USC MSCE

## Program Overview

The official name is Master of Science in Computer Engineering, administered by the Ming Hsieh Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) in the USC Viterbi School of Engineering. The program page groups the field into three areas: computer architecture and parallel processing, computer networks and protocols, and VLSI design and CAD/verification.

- Units: 28. There is no official fixed length; Viterbi's 28-unit cost example assumes four semesters.
- Start terms: spring and fall.
- Format: on the Los Angeles campus, or online through DEN@Viterbi, with the same degree and the same tuition.
- After the first semester you can request the thesis option or Directed Research; the thesis option is not guaranteed.
- Eligible for STEM OPT.
- Cost: 2026-27 graduate engineering tuition is $2,742 per unit. The official example assumes a Fall 2026 start over four semesters (4/8/8/8 units): tuition plus fees come to $80,519; adding two years of estimated housing ($1,300 a month) brings it to $111,719, and health insurance adds another $7,044 (at 2025-26 rates).

## Courses and Course Selection

The program page lists this coursework requirement: at least 20 of the 28 units must come from the groups below, with at least one course in two of the three areas (Architecture, Networks, VLSI/CAD).

- Architecture: EE 457 Computer Systems Organization, EE 557 Computer Systems Architecture
- Networks: EE 450 Introduction to Computer Networks, EE 555 Broadband Network Architectures
- VLSI/CAD: EE 477L, EE 536a, EE 552, EE 577a
- Foundations (also counts toward the 20 units): CSCI 402 Operating Systems, EE 503 Probability, EE 510 Linear Algebra, EE 660 Machine Learning from Signals

The admissions page does not say how the remaining 8 units work or whether 400-level units are capped; follow the USC Catalogue requirements for your year.

In the ECE department's Fall 2026 first-semester list, where this program is abbreviated CENG, the recommended courses are EE 450, EE 457, EE 477 and EE 503, and the department suggests no more than two courses (8 units) in the first semester. First-semester ECE graduate students cannot register for CSCI courses; from the second semester on, they can request D-clearance from the CS department. If you are missing prerequisites, you can take the department's placement exams (offered for EE 450, EE 457 and EE 477) for free to satisfy them.

## Internships / CPT

CPT rules from USC's Office of International Services (OIS):

- You generally need to have been in F-1 status for one full academic year before applying.
- The practical training has to be a required part of the curriculum; optional internships and elective courses do not qualify for CPT.
- In fall and spring, CPT is part-time only (20 hours a week or less). Full-time CPT is allowed in summer, or in your final semester if your total course load, including the internship course, is half-time or less: for graduate students, 4 units or fewer, or only one remaining course with that course plus the internship course totaling no more than 5 units.
- Viterbi students use a separate CPT I-20 request form and attach the offer letter and the cooperative agreement between the school and the employer.

The program page lists 33 internships in 2025; a footnote says these are CPT internships done by international students. Companies include AMD, ARM, Cadence, Intel, NVIDIA, Samsung, Tenstorrent and Tesla.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Los Angeles. The program page shows results from the 2025 First Destinations Survey: 63 employed, 3 continuing their education, 4 with other intentions, 6 still seeking employment and 18 unreported. Of those who submitted employment data, 92% were employed, continuing their education or pursuing other opportunities. The average reported salary was $134,985. USC notes that this is a voluntary survey and does not represent the whole 2025 graduating class.

The top employers listed are Amazon, Apple, ByteDance, Meta and NVIDIA, with roles spanning chip design, verification, DFT and software development.

## Application Requirements

From the program page (updated September 22, 2026) and the Viterbi and USC graduate admission pages:

- Deadlines: fall, December 15 (scholarship consideration) and January 15 (final deadline); spring, October 15. The program page does not give years; the Viterbi application page says Spring and Fall 2027 applications are open.
- GRE: not required for 2027 applications, and submitted scores are not reviewed.
- Materials: transcripts, resume/CV and personal statement; letters of recommendation are optional.
- English: USC has no university-wide minimum. These are the scores that exempt admitted students from the ISE English exam (for applications to Spring 2027 and later): TOEFL 90 with 20 per section (tests taken before January 21, 2026), or 4.5 with 4 per section on the new TOEFL; IELTS 6.5 with 6 per band; PTE 61 with 53 per band. USC does not accept Duolingo or TOEFL iBT Best scores.
- Application fee: $120, and one fee covers two Viterbi programs; across USC you can apply to at most three graduate programs per cycle.
- Background: most applicants have degrees in engineering or related fields such as electronics, CS, math or physics; applicants from other fields need strong performance in courses like circuits, linear systems, math and physics.
