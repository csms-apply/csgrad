# NWU MSCE

## Program Overview

The full degree name is Master of Science in Computer Engineering, offered by the ECE department in the McCormick School of Engineering. Both the application and the degree go through The Graduate School (TGS). The campus is in Evanston, about 12 miles from downtown Chicago.

Admission is for fall only. Northwestern runs on quarters, and the degree takes 12 courses. The program page says you can finish in 9–12 months; the 2026-2027 manual sets the deadline for the courses-only plan at the end of the 5th quarter (usually fall of the second year).

Tuition follows the TGS 2026-27 rates: full-time master's tuition is $23,662 per quarter, the same whether you take 3 or 4 courses. At that rate, taking 4 courses a quarter for 3 quarters comes to about $70,986; taking 3 a quarter for 4 quarters comes to about $94,648. On top of that there is a student health fee of $298 per quarter (charged in fall, winter and spring) and NU-SHIP student health insurance at $5,996 a year. The department does not fund master's students, and master's students cannot hold TA or RA positions.

In the OISS CIP code list (the version effective May 2022), the MS in Computer Engineering is coded 14.0901 and marked as eligible for the STEM OPT extension. The code on your I-20 after admission is what counts.

## Courses and Course Selection

Requirements from the 2026-2027 ECE master's manual:

- 12 courses = 6 core + 6 electives. Only 300-level and above count, and at least 3 must be 400-level (590 Research does not count)
- Core courses must be ELEC_ENG or COMP_ENG, with at least 3 COMP_ENG; up to 3 CS courses can be petitioned to count as core
- At least 3 electives must be ECE or CS; at most 3 can come from outside ECE/CS, with approval from the Director of the Master's Program
- Everything except 590 Research must be taken for a letter grade, and overall GPA must be at least 3.0

Under these rules, if you want a software focus, all 6 electives can be COMP_SCI courses.

You pick one of three completion plans and submit it in the system by May 1 of your first year:

- Thesis: 1–3 units of 590 research count as electives, you write a thesis, and you must finish by the end of the 7th quarter
- Project: 1–2 units of 590 research count as electives, you submit a project report, and you must finish by the end of the 6th quarter
- Courses Only: all 12 are courses, and 590 does not count

For Thesis and Project, you need to find your own advisor. Once coursework is done and only the thesis or project remains, you can register for TGS 512 to keep student status, at $100 per quarter in 2026-27.

The program page and the manual both list 12 specializations, such as AI/ML, Embedded Systems, High-Performance Computing and Cybersecurity. Completing 6 courses in one (at least 3 from that specialization's core list) earns a department certificate, which does not appear on the transcript. You can also use 3 electives toward a minor such as AI, Cybersecurity or Engineering Management.

Transfers between the EE and CE master's programs are generally supported; transfers into the ECE master's from other Northwestern programs are considered only in exceptional cases.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The 2026-2027 manual does not mention a co-op for master's students. Master's students doing an internship can register for CRDV 411 Engineering Internship Program. It is a 0-credit course that does not count toward the degree, is graded S/U, and keeps you at full-time status when properly registered.

International students need CPT authorization before starting an off-campus internship, and should check with OISS and McCormick Engineering Career Development before they begin.

## Jobs and Location

The school is just north of Chicago. Nearby partners listed on the program page include Argonne, Fermilab, Motorola and Nokia.

The program page gives a median starting salary of $130k for graduates, with 72% going to industry and 28% to PhD study, without saying which years or which students the numbers cover.

For the job search picture in the CS master's at the same school, see [NWU MSCS](../B+/NWU%20MSCS.md).

## Application Requirements

The following is from the ECE admissions page as of October 2026 (labeled for the 2027-28 academic year, i.e. Fall 2027) and from TGS pages:

- Deadline: the admissions page gives no Fall 2027 MS deadline. It only says space is limited, the portal may close early once the cohort is full, and early submission is encouraged. The 2026-2027 manual says the MS deadline is typically the last week of February
- GRE: not required for Fall 2027
- English: TOEFL 90 for tests taken before January 21, 2026, and 4.5 on the new TOEFL for tests taken after that; IELTS 7, the TGS minimum; TGS does not accept Duolingo
- Recommendation letters: at least 3
- Application fee: $95
- Background: the 2026-2027 manual says applicants typically hold a bachelor's in EE, CE, CS or a related field, and recommends a foundation in digital logic and computer organization, operating systems, programming, data structures and algorithms, and embedded systems and computer architecture
- SOP: the admissions page says it carries a lot of weight. Be clear about the area you want to work in and the faculty you want to work with (for MS applicants, this can be a project or thesis advisor)

TGS allows each applicant only one application to one program per year: if you apply for Fall 2027, the earliest you can apply again is Fall 2028. If you also want to apply to another Northwestern program, check first whether it also goes through TGS.

All 4 applicants did their undergrad in mainland China. The 2 admits were in 2023, one from a "top five" Chinese university and one from a 985 school, majoring in CS and software engineering, with GPAs of 3.75/4.3 and 3.85/4.0; both had research and domestic internship experience. The 2 rejects were 2025 and 2026 applicants from non-985/211 schools, majoring in CS and information security, with GPAs of 87 and 91.8 out of 100. English scores: TOEFL 102–103 (3 applicants) and IELTS 7 (1 applicant). The 2 who submitted the GRE scored 326 and 332, with Q 169–170.

With only 4 DPs, there is no trend to read from this.
