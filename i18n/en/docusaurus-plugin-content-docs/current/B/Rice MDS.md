# Rice MDS

## Program Overview

Data management coursework can be useful in practice. Compare the courses and capstone with the kind of work you want to pursue.

The official degree is the Master of Data Science (MDS), a non-thesis professional degree run by the Department of Computer Science. The on-campus program is full-time in Houston, admits once a year for fall, and usually takes three consecutive semesters: fall, spring, and a second fall.

Rice also has an online MDS, and you can apply to only one of the two. On-campus students can take up to 9 credits of online courses with their program advisor's approval.

Tuition: the Rice bursar's 2026-27 engineering table does not list the on-campus MDS on its own. It shows only two graduate tuition rates: Masters & PhD full-time at $33,392 per semester and Professional Masters in Engineering at $30,720 per semester. Which one applies to the on-campus MDS is up to your bill. Each semester also carries a $22.50 GSA fee, a $4 student organization fee and a $360 Health & Wellness fee, with health insurance billed separately. The online MDS has its own row at $1,700 per credit.

Scholarships: the program page says the university awards merit scholarships to top applicants, and submitting an application puts you in consideration automatically. The 2026-27 General Announcements, however, say that neither Rice nor the Computer Science department offers financial aid to MDS students. The two sources disagree, so go by your admission letter.

STEM: the data science page of the General Announcements gives the Data Science degree program the CIP code 30.7001 (Data Science, General), which is on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List (July 2024 version). The CIP code on your I-20 is what ultimately counts.

## Courses and Course Selection

Under the 2026-27 General Announcements, you need 31–35 credits (10–13 courses, depending on what you pick), at least 24 of them taken at Rice, and a GPA of at least 2.67.

- Five core courses: big data (COMP 543 or 643), data visualization (COMP 665), machine learning (COMP 642 or ELEC 578), programming (COMP 614) and statistics (COMP 680).
- One elective, picked from courses such as COMP 510, 536, 539, 582, 644, 682 and ELEC 509.
- At least three specialization courses (9 credits) in one of five areas: Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics, Energy Transition and Sustainability, Image Processing, or Sport Analytics. The program page FAQ says the last three are offered only on campus. The AI area has the most course options, including reinforcement learning, NLP and deep learning. Business Analytics is mostly business school BUSI courses, and pairs such as BUSI 711/712 count only if you take both.
- Professional Development, one of three options: one approved course (ENGI 501, ENGI 510, RCEL 501–505), a relevant internship of 10 weeks to 6 months, or at least 10 weeks of relevant post-bachelor's work experience.
- Capstone: the D2K (Data to Knowledge) project in your final semester, DSCI 535 / COMP 549, 4 credits, working with a partner on a real business problem. Partners listed on the program page include NASA, Texas Children's Hospital, Baylor College of Medicine, AWS, Chevron and the Houston Fire Department.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

An internship is not required to graduate; it is just one way to meet the Professional Development requirement. The program page FAQ says outside internships are usually done in the summer between semesters, that is, the summer between the first spring and the second fall. The program page says nothing about CPT rules or internships during the semester, so ask the university's international student office and the department.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Houston. The program page says most students land paid internships during the program and names recent internship employers including JPMorgan, Memorial Hermann, AWS and Chevron; the capstone partners also lean toward energy, healthcare and the public sector. The program page does not publish graduate employment rates or salaries.

## Application Requirements

Fall 2027 deadlines for the on-campus MDS:

- February 10, 2027: priority deadline for scholarship consideration
- May 1, 2027: international deadline
- July 1, 2027: domestic deadline

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so earlier is better. The department expects MDS decisions from late March to early April 2027.

Materials: undergraduate transcripts (electronic copies when you apply, official copies after admission), CV, statement of purpose, at least three letters of recommendation, and an $85 application fee. The program page lists a GPA of 3.0+, and the GRE is not required. English minimums are TOEFL iBT (Home Edition accepted) 90, IELTS 7 and Duolingo 115; a degree from a U.S. institution or an English-taught degree exempts you.

## Admissions Outcomes (csgrad DP)

csgrad currently has only one Rice MDS data point: Fall 2023, Reject. The applicant came from a mainland China 985 university with an information systems-type major, a GPA of 3.8+/4.0, TOEFL 100+, GRE 320+, plus domestic research and a data analysis internship at a large tech company. One record is not enough to tell where the bar is.

Rice MCS, in the same department, has 64 data points on csgrad, so its page gives a better sense of applicant backgrounds.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [Rice MCS](../B+/Rics%20MCS.md): same Computer Science department and the same Fall 2027 deadlines as MDS. Pick MCS for a general CS curriculum, and MDS if you want statistics, data work and an industry capstone.
- UVA MSDS: another B-tier data science master's, useful for comparing program length and how the capstone works.
- [USC ADS](../B-/USC%20ADS.md), [NYU DS](../A-/NYU%20DS.md), [Columbia DS](../A-/Columbia%20DS.md): also data science master's programs, useful for comparing courses and costs.
