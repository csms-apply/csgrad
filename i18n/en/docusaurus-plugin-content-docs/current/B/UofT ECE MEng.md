# UofT ECE MEng

## Program Overview

The full-time, extended and co-op options have different schedules and costs. Decide whether a work term matters to you and how many courses you want to take per session before comparing the totals.

The full degree name is Master of Engineering (MEng) in Electrical and Computer Engineering. It is offered by the ECE department in U of T Engineering, and classes are in person. It is a course-based master's, and the department says it is not the recommended route to a PhD.

Going by the 2026-27 graduate calendar and the department's own notes, there are several ways to take it:

- Full-time: 3 sessions, designed for 12 months, at most 5 courses per session. Program fees cover the whole degree within 12 months; taking longer costs more, and the department's example is that finishing in 16 months costs 1.5 times the program fee
- Extended full-time (EFT): designed for 20 or 24 months, at most 3 courses per session
- EFT plus co-op: two years, see below
- Part-time: designed for 3 years, at most 2 courses per session

Full-time and EFT have a 3-year time limit; part-time has 6 years. The regular MEng accepts September and January starts; the co-op version is September only.

This is a Canadian program, so US STEM designation does not apply. To stay and work in Canada after graduating, you go through the PGWP. IRCC states that since February 15, 2024, graduates of a master's program at least 8 months long can apply for a 3-year PGWP; master's graduates have no field-of-study requirement; and applications submitted on or after November 1, 2024 need CLB 7 in English (or NCLC 7 in French) in all four skills.

Cost: U of T Engineering lists 2026-27 full-time MEng tuition (including incidental fees) as CAD 76,090.39 for international students and CAD 15,932 for domestic students. That column covers MEng and MEngCEM together and is not broken down by department. Fall 2027 co-op students pay an extra CAD 3,865 co-op ancillary fee.

## Courses and Course Selection

You need nine graduate half courses (4.5 FCE):

- At least 6 technical courses, at least 5 of which are ECE courses. TEP and APS courses count as non-technical, but APS1070H, APS1080H, APS1050H and APS1052H count as technical without prior approval
- You can do ECE2500Y MEng Project, worth 3 courses, and then take 6 more courses; you need to find a professor with ECE graduate faculty membership to supervise it

Emphases are optional. The calendar lists 15; the ones closest to CS are Computer Engineering, Data Analytics and Machine Learning, Identity, Privacy and Security, and Robotics. A completed emphasis is noted on your transcript.

On the 2026-27 timetable, software and ML options include ECE1779H Introduction to Cloud Computing (open to ECE MEng students only), ECE1513H Introduction to Machine Learning, ECE1553H Applied Deep Learning, ECE1762H Algorithms and Data Structures, ECE1784H Trustworthy Machine Learning, and several ECE1724H Software Systems special topics (performant software systems with Rust, program synthesis and verification, distributed systems, and others). On the hardware side there are the ECE1718H sections Embedded Linux and Advanced Computer Architecture, plus ECE1755H Parallel Computer Architecture and Programming. The department notes that not every course is offered every year.

ECE typically does not offer graduate courses in the Summer, and the department strongly recommends that full-time students finish their technical courses in Fall and Winter. In Summer you can take Faculty ELITE or Analytics courses or technical courses from other departments, or work on ECE2500Y.

## Internship / CPT / Co-op

CPT is a US concept and does not apply here. The regular MEng has no work term. If you want a work term inside the degree, apply to the MEng Extended Full-Time with Co-op (Fall 2027 start; ECE is one of six participating departments):

- Two years, limited enrolment, with an 8-month paid work term (May–December 2028); placement is not guaranteed, and students who do not land one finish as regular EFT students
- TEP1701 Navigating Engineering Workplaces is required in Fall 2027; you keep taking courses while looking for a position in Winter 2028, and come back in Winter and Summer 2029 to finish the remaining courses
- You can only get in by applying to it directly; regular MEng students cannot transfer in after admission. Applicants not accepted to co-op are automatically considered for the regular EFT program
- International students are eligible. The program page says that since April 1, 2026, international students who meet study permit and program requirements no longer need a separate co-op work permit for a mandatory work term

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Toronto. The department does not publish employment data for the ECE MEng. The regular full-time MEng takes 12 months with no work term; the co-op version takes two years with an 8-month work term in the middle. To stay in Canada after graduating, you go through the PGWP described above.

## Application Requirements

For the Fall 2027 cycle you apply through the SGS application system, which opens in late October 2026. The application fee is CAD 135.

- Deadlines (September 2027 start): April 15, 2027 for international applicants and May 31, 2027 for domestic applicants; February 8, 2027 for the co-op version, with no late applications. The Faculty page has not yet listed a deadline for a January 2028 start
- Background: the equivalent of a U of T four-year bachelor's in ECE or a related field (computer science, math, physics and similar fields count), with at least a mid-B average in the final year. The department states that meeting the minimum does not guarantee admission
- English: the SGS standard applies. TOEFL iBT 93 with 22 in Writing and Speaking (on the new 1–6 scale: 4.5 overall, 4.5 Writing, 4.0 Speaking); IELTS Academic 7.0 with at least 6.5 in each band. You must meet it at the time of application, with scores no more than two years old. Degrees taught and examined entirely in English in Canada or in the countries/regions the department lists are exempt
- GRE: not mentioned on ECE's minimum admission requirements page or on the Faculty's how-to-apply page
- References: you enter referee contact information in the application system; the official pages do not say how many are required
- The co-op version may include an interview, with decisions by May 2027 at the latest
