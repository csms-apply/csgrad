# USC CS32

## Program Overview

Specializations give the degree a clearer focus but also use some elective space. Compare required courses with the general MSCS and check whether the classes you want fit your plan.

CS32 is this site's name for the USC MSCS degrees with a specialization, offered by the Thomas Lord Department of Computer Science in the USC Viterbi School of Engineering. The general MSCS used to be 28 units; starting Fall 2024 it also became 32 units (students who started in Summer 2024 or earlier stay on 28). So today the main difference between the two is whether the curriculum is fixed.

Three specializations are currently admitting:

- Master of Science in Computer Science - Artificial Intelligence: spring and fall starts, on campus only.
- Master of Science in Computer Science - Data Science: spring and fall starts, also offered online through DEN@Viterbi at the same tuition as on campus.
- Master of Science in Computer Science - Game Development: fall start only, on campus only.

Six specializations stopped admitting students starting Fall 2024: Computer Security, Computer Networks, Software Engineering, Intelligent Robotics, Multimedia and Creative Technologies, and High Performance Computing and Simulation.

What the three share: 32 units, no thesis option, Directed Research can be requested after the first semester, and eligibility for the STEM OPT extension.

Cost: graduate engineering tuition for 2026-27 is $2,742 per unit. The official example assumes a Fall 2026 start and four semesters of 8 units each: tuition plus fees come to $91,487, and with two years of estimated housing ($1,300 per month) the total is $122,687. Health insurance adds about $7,044. Rates for 2027-28 have not been published yet.

## Courses

The department pages (updated August 2026; the USC Catalogue for your entry year is what ultimately counts) lay out the curriculum as follows:

- AI: required CSCI 561 (Foundations of AI), 566 (Deep Learning), 567 (Machine Learning) and 570 (Algorithms), 16 units in total. Then take 4 courses from five groups (ML/DL, NLP and speech, computer vision and robotics, decision making and optimization, AI + X), covering at least two groups.
- Data Science: required CSCI 561, 570 and 585 (Databases), 12 units in total. Take 3 courses across the Data Systems and Data Analysis groups, with at least one from each. The remaining 8–10 units can be 500/600-level CSCI courses or listed DSCI and MATH courses. At most 4 units can be 400-level, and at most 3 DSCI courses count.
- Game Development: required CSCI 570, plus 24 units of emphasis requirements: CSCI 561, CSCI 580 (3D Graphics), two cinema courses (CTIN 532L and CTIN 541), and the two-semester CSCI 529a/b game project sequence. The last 4 units come from a list of CSCI and CTIN electives.

The general MSCS has only one required course, CSCI 570. The prescribed required units are 16 for AI, 12 for Data Science and 28 for Game Development. All three specializations require at least a 3.0 GPA in degree coursework.

To change programs after admission, you must first finish one semester in the program you were admitted to and then apply; the target department reviews the request and approval is not guaranteed. It is best to settle on the specialization when you apply.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

USC does not list a co-op option. Internships go through Viterbi's ENGR 595 internship course to get CPT; the AI and Data Science curriculum pages state that internship units do not count as electives. International students must keep full-time F-1 status for one academic year (fall and spring) before they can apply for CPT; the GPA requirement is 3.0 for fall/spring and 2.75 for summer. In fall and spring you can only work part time unless it is your final semester, in which case you can work full time if you are enrolled in no more than 4 units. Summer internships can be full time.

CPT internships by international students in 2025, as listed on each program page: 48 for AI, 7 for Data Science and 2 for Game Development.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Los Angeles. The official 2025 First Destinations Survey is voluntary: the AI page lists 15 employers, including Amazon and Scale AI, with an average reported salary of $129,667; the Data Science salary figure is based on a single response; Game Development reports an average of $87,100. The samples are small and do not represent the whole class.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines (Fall 2027): December 15, 2026 for scholarship consideration and January 15, 2027 as the final deadline, both at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. The Spring 2027 deadline for AI and Data Science was October 15 and has passed.
- Degree: a completed or in-progress bachelor's degree; most applicants studied CS or a closely related field. Applicants from unrelated majors need at least 3 core CS courses and 1 CS-related math course (algorithms, data structures, discrete math and so on), and review is holistic. For STEM graduates with limited CS background, USC suggests applying to CS37 instead.
- Materials: transcripts, resume and personal statement; recommendation letters are optional. The application fee is $120.
- You can apply to at most 3 graduate programs across USC per term. One application fee covers two Viterbi programs; the second needs a waiver code from the admission office.
- GRE: not required for 2027 applications.
- English: USC has no university-wide minimum score for admission. To be exempt from the International Student English (ISE) exam, master's students need TOEFL 90 with at least 20 in each section (tests taken before January 21, 2026) or 4.5 with at least 4 in each section on the new scale; IELTS 6.5 with at least 6 in each band. Scores must be dated within 24 months of when you submit the application. The tests USC lists are TOEFL, IELTS, PTE and Cambridge C1 Advanced; Duolingo is not among them.

## Admissions Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has 8 self-reported CS32 records under two entries: 5 under AI and 3 under Networks (one of which notes the applicant actually applied to Security). Networks and Security have both stopped admitting, so those 3 records only give a rough picture.

- 2023 Fall: 1 record, rejected
- 2024 Spring: 1 admit, noted as deferred to a 2024 Spring start
- 2024 Fall: 4 records, 2 admits and 2 rejections
- 2025 Fall: 1 admit
- 2026 Fall: 1 admit

In total, 5 admits and 3 rejections; AI alone has 4 admits and 1 rejection. All applicants came from mainland Chinese universities: 4 from 985 schools, 2 from 211 schools, 1 from the "Huawu" top-five group and 1 from a non-985/211 school. Majors include CS, AI, software engineering and EE, plus other science/engineering and other fields. GPA ranges from 3.31 to 3.85 on a 4.0 scale (3 records) and from 84.96 to 91.8 on a 100-point scale (5 records); TOEFL 101–107 (6 records), IELTS 7–7.5 (2 records); GRE 322–332 (5 records). All 3 rejections were from 985 schools, and 2 of them applied to specializations that have since closed. One rejection note says the applicant did not submit recommendation letters and got the rejection the same day they emailed in early May saying they would not wait any longer.

Separately, one 2024 Spring admit filed under the CS28 entry has a note saying CS-Computer Security, so it may also be a specialization; it is not counted above.

The sample is small: only 2 records are from 2025 onward, and there are no records yet for Data Science or Game Development.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [USC CS28](../B/USC%20CS28.md): the general MSCS, now also 32 units, with CSCI 570 as the only required course and a thesis option (36 units in total). Pick the general version for a flexible schedule, or a specialization if you want the area in the degree name.
- [USC CS37](../B/USC%20CS37.md): the 37-unit version for STEM graduates without a CS bachelor's, starting with undergraduate-level preparatory and foundation courses.
- [UW EE PMP](../A/uw%20ee%20pmp.md): this guide compares itself with CS32 and is worth reading alongside.
