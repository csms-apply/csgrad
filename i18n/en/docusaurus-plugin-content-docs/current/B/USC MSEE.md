# USC MSEE

## Program Overview

The Viterbi admission page lists the degree as Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering, offered by the Ming Hsieh Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE). The program page and the department still call it the MSEE when describing degree requirements, and the department's Fall 2026 course list labels it MSECE-General Degree.

The ECE department also has specialized master's degrees such as Computer Architecture, VLSI Design, Machine Learning & Data Science and Electric Power, plus the MS in Computer Engineering. Those are separate programs; this page covers only the general degree with no specialization.

- Units: 28. There is no fixed official length.
- Start terms: spring and fall.
- Delivery: the Los Angeles campus, or online through DEN@Viterbi; tuition is the same and so is the degree.
- After the first semester you can request the thesis option or Directed Research; the thesis is not guaranteed.
- Eligible for the STEM OPT extension.
- Cost: 2026-27 graduate engineering tuition is $2,742 per unit; 2027-28 rates are not out yet. Viterbi's example for 28-unit master's programs assumes a Fall 2026 start over four semesters of 4/8/8/8 units: tuition plus fees come to $80,519; adding two years of estimated housing ($1,300 per month) brings it to $111,719, and health insurance adds another $7,044 (at 2025-26 rates). If you need prerequisite courses, the total units will be higher.

## Courses and Course Selection

Degree requirements listed on the program page:

- At least 20 units must be EE courses. Non-EE courses need written adviser approval and must be technical.
- Take 5 courses from one area: 15–20 units according to the program page, 20 units according to the department page. The areas are Computer Networks, Computer Architecture, Communications, Controls, VLSI/CAD, Electric Power Systems, Mixed-Signal ICs, Signal and Image Processing, and Data Science and Engineering. The school notes that these area lists are not degrees.
- At most 3 courses (12 units) at the 400 level; at least 19 units at the 500/600 level.
- At most 2 EE 599 Special Topics courses (8 units) count.

If you lean toward software, look at the area course lists on the program page: Data Science and Engineering includes two machine learning courses, EE 559 and EE 660, plus CSCI 570 algorithms, CSCI 585 databases and CSCI 551 networking; Computer Networks includes EE 450, EE 457, CSCI 402 operating systems and CSCI 555.

The department's Fall 2026 course-selection notes for new students:

- No more than two courses (8 units) is recommended for the first semester. Full-time enrollment for MS students is a minimum of 8 units (2–3 courses).
- Recommended courses are given by area: for example, EE 450, EE 457 and EE 503 for Computer Networks; EE 483, EE 503 and EE 510 for Data Science; EE 457 and EE 477 for VLSI/CAD.
- You cannot take CSCI courses in the first semester; from the second semester on you can request D-clearance from the CS department. EE 590 Directed Research is also not allowed in the first semester.
- EE 450, EE 457 and EE 477 have placement exams, which satisfy prerequisites for follow-on courses. They are optional and free.
- Undergraduate prerequisites below the 400 level can be waived through a request in myViterbi.

## Internships / CPT

Rules from the USC Office of International Services (OIS): you generally need one full academic year before applying for CPT; the internship must be a required part of the curriculum; in fall and spring CPT is part-time only (20 hours a week or less), while full-time is allowed in summer; for full-time CPT in the final semester, a graduate student's enrollment (including the internship course) must be 4 units or less, or only one course remaining with that course plus the internship course totaling 5 units or less. Viterbi students use a separate CPT I-20 request form.

The program page lists 41 internships in 2025, with a footnote saying the count covers CPT internships by international students. Listed companies include AMD, Apple, Applied Materials, ARM, Intel, NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Rivian, Samsung Austin Research Center and Tenstorrent.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Los Angeles. The program page's 2025 First Destinations Survey reports an average reported salary of $119,378, with top employers AMD, Apple, Huawei, NVIDIA and TSMC. Listed roles include chip design, design verification and DFT, software and firmware, RF and power, and data science. The school notes this is a voluntary survey that does not represent the full 2025 graduating class, and the page gives no response count or employment rate.

## Application Requirements

The following comes from the program page (updated September 16, 2026) and the Viterbi and USC graduate admission pages:

- Deadlines: Spring 2027, October 15; Fall 2027, December 15 (scholarship consideration) and January 15, 2027 (final).
- GRE: not required for 2027 applications.
- Materials: transcripts, résumé, personal statement; recommendation letters are optional.
- English: USC sets no university-wide minimum; scores mainly decide whether admitted students must take the ISE English exam. Exemption scores for master's students (applications for Spring 2027 and later): TOEFL 90 with 20 in each section (tests taken before January 21, 2026), or 4.5 with 4 in each section on the new TOEFL; IELTS 6.5 with 6 in each band; PTE 61 with 53 in each band. Superscores such as TOEFL MyBest are not accepted, and Duolingo is not among the accepted tests.
- Application fee: $120 for most USC graduate programs; you can apply to at most three programs per term.
- Background: most applicants have degrees in engineering or related fields such as electronics, CS, math or physics; applicants from other majors need strong performance in courses like circuits, linear systems, math and physics.
