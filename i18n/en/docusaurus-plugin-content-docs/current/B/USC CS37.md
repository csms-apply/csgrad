# USC CS37

Before choosing this program, consider how much CS foundation you need and whether you can afford the additional credits and living costs. Coursework provides a foundation, but internship and job preparation also takes time.

Foundational courses can be approachable for students with less CS background, while popular courses may require enrollment approval. Workload varies by course and instructor; exams and final projects can make the end of term much busier. Students interested in research should approach research groups proactively.

## Program Overview

The official name is Master of Science in Computer Science - Scientists and Engineers, offered by the Thomas Lord Department of Computer Science in the USC Viterbi School of Engineering. It is meant for people with an engineering or science bachelor's degree but a limited CS background: you first take a few undergraduate-level programming and systems courses, then move on to graduate coursework.

- Units: 37 in total, 33 of which count toward the degree. The department page describes it as a two-year program.
- Start terms: spring and fall.
- Delivery: on campus in Los Angeles, or online through DEN@Viterbi, at the same tuition.
- No thesis option; Directed Research can be requested after the first semester. Eligible for the STEM OPT extension.
- Once admitted, you cannot switch to another USC CS master's program, but electives can come from any CS area.
- Cost: graduate engineering tuition for 2026-27 is $2,742 per unit. The official example assumes a Fall 2026 start and two years / five semesters (including one summer). The 12 units in the first semester are billed at the undergraduate engineering rate, and tuition plus fees come to $100,601. With housing estimated at $1,300 per month, the five-semester total is about $131,801, and health insurance adds about $7,044. Rates for 2027-28 have not been published yet.

## Courses

The department page (updated September 2025; the USC Catalogue takes precedence) lays out the curriculum as follows:

- Preparatory course: CSCI 455x Introduction to Programming Systems Design, 4 units. It must be taken in the first semester and does not count toward the 33 degree units.
- Foundational courses: CSCI 402 Operating Systems, plus either EE 450 Computer Networks or EE 457 Computer Systems Organization, 8 units in total. CSCI 402 is meant for the second or third semester.
- Core course: CSCI 570 Analysis of Algorithms.
- Electives: 21 units of 500- to 600-level CS courses. Recommended options include CSCI 561 (AI), 571 (Web Technologies), 585 (Databases), 576, 577a/b and 580. CSCI 590 and 591 count for at most 2 units each, and only one 600-level course can count.

The preparatory and foundational courses must be taken at USC and cannot be transferred in or waived. You need at least a 3.0 GPA in degree coursework.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

There is no co-op structure. Internships go through Viterbi's ENGR 595 internship course to get CPT, and the department page says internship courses cannot count as electives. International students must keep full-time F-1 status for one academic year (fall and spring) before applying for CPT. The GPA requirement is 3.0 for fall/spring and 2.75 for summer. In fall and spring, unless it is your final semester, you can only work part time, 10–20 hours a week; in the final semester you can work full time if you are enrolled in no more than 4 units, and summer internships can be full time. For an out-of-area full-time internship in fall or spring, the offer letter must say hybrid or remote.

The program page says international students in this program did 57 CPT internships in 2025.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Los Angeles. The 2025 First Destinations Survey cited on the program page lists Amazon, ByteDance, Meta and TikTok as top employers, with an average reported salary of $141,056. The survey is voluntary, and USC itself says it does not represent the whole class.

## Application Requirements

- Deadlines (Fall 2027): December 15, 2026 for scholarship consideration and January 15, 2027 as the final deadline, both at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. The Spring 2027 deadline was October 15, 2026 and has passed.
- Materials: transcripts, resume and personal statement; recommendation letters are optional. The Spring/Fall 2027 application fee is $120.
- GRE: not required for 2027 applications, and submitted scores will not be reviewed.
- English: USC has no university-wide minimum score for admission. To be exempt from the International Student English (ISE) exam, master's students need TOEFL 90 with at least 20 in each section (tests taken before January 21, 2026), or 4.5 with at least 4 in each section on the new scale used after that; IELTS 6.5 with at least 6 in each band. Scores must be dated within 24 months of when you submit the application, and superscoring such as TOEFL MyBestScores is not accepted; the Viterbi application page also says Duolingo is not accepted. The accepted tests are TOEFL, IELTS, Cambridge C1 Advanced and PTE Academic.

## Admissions Results (csgrad DP)

csgrad has 13 self-reported CS37 records:

- 2023 Fall: 9 records, 8 admits and 1 silent rejection
- 2024 Spring: 2 admits, both noting a deferral to 2024 Spring
- 2024 Fall: 1 admit
- 2026 Fall: 1 admit

Background: 11 from mainland Chinese universities (1 Tsinghua/Peking, 6 from the "Huawu" top-five group, 4 from other 985 schools), 1 from Hong Kong and 1 from a US university. Most majors are engineering fields such as EE/ECE, automation, information security and information systems, along with interdisciplinary fields such as BME, other science or engineering majors and non-STEM majors; only 1 record has a CS bachelor's. GPA ranges from 3.36 to 3.98 on a 4.0 scale (8 records) and 87–88 on a 100-point scale (3 records). TOEFL ranges from 98 to 115 (12 records with scores); GRE from 324 to 329 (10 records with scores), with quant 165–170. The only silent rejection was from a 985 school with a GPA above 3.8.

9 of the 13 records are from 2023 Fall and only 1 is from 2025 or later. GRE scores are no longer reviewed, so the GRE numbers above only reflect earlier cycles. Treat all of this as a rough reference.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [USC CS28](../B/USC%20CS28.md): the general MSCS. Since Fall 2024 it requires 32 units (students who started in Summer 2024 or earlier stay on 28), has CSCI 570 as its only required course, counts at most 4 units of 400-level courses, and offers a thesis option (36 units). USC says most applicants have CS or closely related degrees, and applicants from unrelated majors need to have completed and done well in CS and math coursework. CS37 adds CSCI 455x and two 400-level systems foundation courses, for 5 more units in total, has no thesis option, and does not let you switch to the general MSCS after admission.
