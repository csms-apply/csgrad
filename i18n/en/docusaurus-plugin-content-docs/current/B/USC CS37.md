# USC CS28

## Program Overview
A two-year program, famously known for only looking at GPA. The class size is enormous (between CS28 and CS37), approaching nearly a thousand students.

## Admission
Only looks at background and GPA. Recommendation letters are optional. Rolling admissions. All applicants from all schools are pooled together and ranked by GPA, with offers sent from top to bottom. 985/211 university backgrounds are valued.

Internships and research are not considered. Recommended as a safety school for high-GPA candidates.

## RA / PhD Transfer Opportunities

Very difficult. USC CS-related programs have enormous class sizes, making it very hard to find RA opportunities, and transferring to a PhD is also very difficult.

## Courses and workload

CS28 is the old name. The general non-thesis MSCS moved to 32 units in Fall 2024—one more course than before.

570 algorithms is worth considering: theory and proofs, not an interview-prep class. 571 Web is useful for development projects; the 2020–2022 version included web and mobile assignments, with a heavier workload toward the end. 402 OS is a demanding systems option. 585 databases and 572 search are comparatively easier to combine with recruiting, although database material is useful on the job too.

The instructor matters. Even 570 has had different teaching styles and homework-grading arrangements. A systems-heavy schedule and a lighter recruiting-oriented schedule can feel like very different degrees.

## Recruiting

There is a strong recruiting focus, but applications, referrals and coding practice largely remain your own work. Career fairs and Handshake exist, and fairs can produce interviews. Being in LA does not automatically generate more of them. The 2022 new-grad market was very different from 2025; old stories about collecting big-tech offers are a poor expectation for today.

## Life

LA has plenty of food and things to do, with a big difference between having a car and going without one. Living near campus and cooking is workable without a car. Regular trips to Chinese neighborhoods and places farther afield are more convenient with one. The lifestyle can be great, and spending can rise with it.

## CPT — October 11, 2026 update

USC now requires practical training to be mandatory for every student in the program; an elective internship course is insufficient. The general MSCS has no published internship requirement for all students, and 597/598 do not count toward the degree, so older CPT arrangements cannot simply be reused.
