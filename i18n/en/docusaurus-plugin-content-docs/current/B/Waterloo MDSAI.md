# Waterloo MDSAI

## Program Overview

Co-op offers a work term but does not guarantee a placement. Learn about the job search and course schedule early so you can plan your time.

The full degree name is Master of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (MDSAI), offered by the Department of Data Science in the University of Waterloo's Faculty of Mathematics. This page covers the co-op version: full-time only, coursework-based, taught in person on Waterloo's main campus, with Fall admission only. MDSAI also has a non-co-op version (full-time and part-time), which is a separate program with its own application.

According to the Fall 2026 graduate academic calendar, the co-op version runs 4 terms (16 months): 3 study terms and 1 work term. The order is Fall study, Winter study, a Spring (May–August) co-op term, and then one more Fall study term. You can apply to extend the work term to 8 months, which makes the program 5 terms (20 months), but only if you stay with the same employer.

This is a Canadian program, so US STEM designation does not apply. Under current IRCC rules, graduates of a master's program that is at least 8 months long can apply for a 3-year PGWP if they meet the other requirements, even if the program is shorter than two years.

Tuition is charged per course. On the Finance office's Fall 2026 international fee table, MDSAI costs CAD 6,272 per course, so 9 courses come to about CAD 56,000. Students taking 3 or more courses in a term also pay CAD 1,014.11 in incidental fees per term. The co-op fee is CAD 836: it is charged once in your first study term and again in each later work term. Prices for later terms follow whatever the Finance office publishes for that term.

## Courses and Course Selection

The calendar requires at least 9 courses, normally 1 foundation course, 5 core courses and 3 electives.

- Foundation: at most one, depending on your undergraduate major. CS 600 is for students without a CS background and STAT 845 for students without a statistics background; both are offered only in the fall. If you are not assigned a foundation course, you take 4 electives instead.
- Core: CS 738 Data Engineering for Data Science and STAT 847 Exploratory Data Analysis, plus one course from each of three groups: STAT 841/842/844 (statistical learning, visualization), CS 648/680/685 (database implementation, machine learning) and CO 602/673/663 (optimization).
- Electives come from a set list with courses in distributed systems, databases, AI and computational statistics. You can also do DATSC 701/702 Data Science Project, which needs a professor willing to supervise and an approved proposal.

You also have to attend a 3-day workshop on ethics in data science and AI (offered once a year), or take the CS 798 topics course on AI law, ethics and policy instead. Co-op students take WIL 601 Career Foundations in their first term. You need a 75% average and at least 70% in every course; falling below that can mean being required to withdraw.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

Canada has no CPT; the internship is the program's built-in co-op. You must complete 1 work term, in the third term. The job has to be paid, full-time, 12–16 weeks long and related to your program or career goals. Most jobs are found on the university's WaterlooWorks system, which opens to you the term before the work term. A job you arrange yourself (AOJ) is reviewed case by case and must be related to data science. Example roles on the program page include Data Science Intern, Data Analytics Intern and Reporting Analyst. The university also supports co-op placements outside Canada.

If you cannot find a co-op job, you can request a transfer to the non-co-op MDSAI. After the work term you submit a reflective report, and you need at least a satisfactory evaluation from your employer.

On immigration: IRCC announced that, as of April 1, 2026, eligible post-secondary international students no longer need a separate co-op work permit for required co-op placements.

## Jobs and Location

The university is in Waterloo, Ontario. MDSAI students can use the program's own Graduate Data Science Job Board. MDSAI is a Vector Institute recognized AI master's program, so students can apply for Vector scholarships and see its job opportunities. The program does not publish employment rates or salary data.

## Application Requirements

- Deadline: the website lists January 15 each year (for September entry) without a year label. On that schedule, Fall 2027 corresponds to January 15, 2027. The department recommends submitting at least a month early. Review starts only after the deadline, usually takes 8–10 weeks, and decisions appear in the Applicant Portal.
- Background: an honours bachelor's degree in data science, CS, statistics, math or a related field, with an average of at least 78%, and senior-level coursework in at least one of CS or statistics. The Data Science department's admissions page says the actual admission cutoff is much higher than 78%. Applicants missing some background may be admitted on probation and take CS 600 or STAT 845 before the program starts, without degree credit.
- Materials: résumé, a statement of interest of up to 500 words, program-specific questions (PSQ) and transcripts.
- Recommendation letters: 3, at least 2 academic.
- GRE: not required; the department encourages submitting scores as a supplement.
- English: TOEFL 5 (new 1–6 scale; writing 5, speaking 5) or IELTS 7.5 (writing and speaking 7.0 each). Scores must come from a single test sitting; TOEFL MyBest and IELTS One Skill Retake are not accepted, and scores are valid for two years. Applicants who have completed three or more years of post-secondary study in Canada, among other cases, are exempt.

## Admissions Results (csgrad DPs)

csgrad has 5 MDSAI records. They do not distinguish the co-op and non-co-op versions, and all come from Fall 2023 and Fall 2024; there are none for 2025 or later. The sample is very small, so treat it only as a rough reference.

| Entry term | Admit | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 Fall | 2 | 0 |
| 2024 Fall | 1 | 2 |

- The 3 admits all majored in CS. By school category, one studied in Canada and one at a 211 university; the third's category is not standardized in the data. GPAs were 3.74–3.93/4 or around 90.5/100, and those who reported rank were in the top 5%. The two who reported scores had GRE 324–328, with TOEFL 106 or IELTS 7.5.
- The 2 rejects came from mainland Chinese universities at the 985 level or above, majoring in software engineering and data science, with GPAs of 3.6/4 and around 90.5/100, GRE 323–333 and TOEFL 106–108.
- Admit and reject scores overlap, so no clear cutoff shows up. All 5 decisions came out in March. Both 2024 rejects found their decision by logging into Quest themselves, and one received no email (the website now says decisions appear in the Applicant Portal). One 2023 admit got an informal offer first and the formal offer in early April.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [Waterloo ECE MEng](../B/Waterloo%20ECE%20MEng.md): a coursework master's in Waterloo's Faculty of Engineering. Its co-op version also has a separate application and allows 1–2 work terms.
- [UofT MScAC](../A/UofT%20MScAC.md): also in Ontario and also 16 months, but with a fixed 8-month internship; MDSAI's co-op is 4 months by default and can be extended to 8.
- [Waterloo MMath CS](../A-/Waterloo%20MMath%20CS.md): also in the Faculty of Mathematics; a research master's in the CS school that follows the thesis route.
- [SFU Professional MSc CS](../B-/SFU%20Professional%20MSc%20CS.md): another Canadian coursework master's with co-op, including a Big Data track.
