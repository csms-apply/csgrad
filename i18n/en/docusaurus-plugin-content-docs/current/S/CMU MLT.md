# CMU MLT

## Program Overview

MLT stands for Master of Language Technologies, a research master's run by the Language Technologies Institute (LTI) in CMU's School of Computer Science (SCS). The official site frames it as preparation for a PhD: many graduates go on to PhDs at CMU or elsewhere, and others join research-focused companies. The LTI PhD page also says the first two years of the PhD are similar to the MLT.

- 24 months including two summers, which are devoted entirely to research; students usually graduate in August of their second year, and early graduation is allowed only in special circumstances with the Program Director's approval
- Fall entry; classes are in person on the Pittsburgh campus
- Typical research areas listed in the handbook: speech processing, information retrieval, machine translation, NLP, machine learning and computational biology

Tuition: the MLT page says tuition is set by SCS. Student Financial Services (SFS) lists 2026-27 SCS master's tuition at $62,200 a year ($31,100 per semester), with non-tuition costs such as housing, food and insurance estimated at $37,374 a year; SFS also notes that rates may vary by program.

Funding: the program page says research assistantships are only occasionally offered by advisors to current students, are not guaranteed and can change from semester to semester. The AY26-27 handbook, however, says in Section 9.1 that all MLT students are guaranteed full funding (tuition plus stipend) for the first year, while Section 5.1 mentions self-supporting students. The two don't match, so go by what your admission letter says.

## Courses and Course Selection

A research master’s involves sustained research alongside coursework. Students with a weaker CS background may need more time for courses, so consider the questions you want to explore as well.

Graduation requirements in the AY26-27 handbook:

- At least 120 units of senior- or graduate-level courses, including at least 72 units of LTI courses (11-xxx) and at least 24 units of SCS courses; a 12-unit course counts as one course and a 6-unit course as half
- 11-711 Advanced NLP is required
- 11-791 or one substitute. 11-791 is not currently offered, so courses such as 11-731 Machine Translation, 11-751 Speech Recognition, 11-777 Multimodal Machine Learning, 11-785 Deep Learning or 11-797 Question Answering count instead
- One Applications Engineering course (added in the Fall 2025 revision, replacing the old Task Orientation requirement)
- 12 units of 11-910 Directed Research count toward the total, plus 12 units of open elective
- The LTI Colloquium (11-700) every semester; 11-705 can replace it once
- Two summers of full-time directed research

Any one MLD graduate course (10-xxx) can count as an LTI course. Electives outside SCS can be taken at CMU or cross-registered at Pitt, for example ECE, statistics or Pitt linguistics; discuss them with your advisor in advance. Full-time status requires at least 36 units per semester. There is no minimum GPA, but a course must be a C or higher to count; at the end of each fall and spring semester the LTI faculty review every student's progress.

Advisor matching happens in the first month: you attend faculty research talks, meet potential advisors on your own, list your top three choices, and the full faculty then meets to decide the matches. A master's thesis is optional; if you do one, it is two courses, 11-928 and 11-929. MLT students do not normally TA, and need their advisor's approval to do so.

## Internships / CPT / Co-op

The handbook says that because the program is 24 months with research scheduled through both summers, students may intern only if their advisor recommends it. International students must check work authorization with OIE before looking for an internship. Students funded by an LTI research project are normally expected to work only on that project; outside consulting is capped at one day (8 hours) per week, needs advisor consent and, for international students, advance approval from OIE.

## Jobs and Location

The school is in Pittsburgh. Neither the website nor the handbook gives employment rates or salary data; they only say many graduates go on to PhDs and others go into industry, many at major corporate research labs.

If you want to stay at CMU for a PhD, you apply through the regular SCS PhD process; if admitted, courses you took at CMU count as if taken during the PhD.

## Application Requirements

Fall 2027 cycle (centralized SCS application, opens September 9, 2026):

- Deadlines: early November 18, 2026, final December 9, 2026, both 3 p.m. EST. The fee is $80 before the early deadline and $100 after, per program
- GRE: optional; if submitted, scores must be less than five years old
- English: TOEFL is required for non-native speakers with no exceptions; IELTS is also accepted. No minimum is published; the SCS admissions page lists typical ranges for admitted applicants of TOEFL 105–114 and IELTS 7–8. Without a U.S. degree, scores must be within two years, and tests taken before September 1, 2024 are not accepted
- Three letters of recommendation (up to five accepted), at least two from faculty or recent employers; SOP, resume and transcripts
- Video essay: optional but strongly recommended; a 1–3 minute answer to a given question, with three recording attempts
- SCS lets you apply to up to three doctoral programs and any number of master's programs, so the LTI PhD and the MLT can be applied to together

- TOEFL 101–104 (3 applicants), the fourth submitted IELTS 7.5; only two reported GRE, in the 321–329 range
- All four had research experience, three had papers under submission or published, some in NLP; each had at least one research letter
- Two noted in their comments that they also applied to the PhD. The Fall 2023 rejections all came in mid-February and the Fall 2024 one in early February, noted as rejected without an interview

With only 4 data points and no admits, treat this as a rough reference only.

## Comparison with Similar Programs

- [CMU MIIS](../S/CMU%20MIIS.md): also an LTI master's, but career-oriented. It comes in 16-month and 21-month versions, requires a summer internship (waivable with enough prior work experience) and includes a semester-long group capstone. The MLT is 24 months with both summers spent on research, and internships need your advisor's go-ahead
- [CMU MCDS](../S/cmu%20mcds.md): another LTI master's, focused on engineering and deploying large-scale information systems
- [CMU MSAII](../S/CMU%20MSAII.md): LTI's AI/ML curriculum combined with team projects in innovation and entrepreneurship
- The first two years of the LTI PhD are similar to the MLT; 5LT is a research-oriented fifth-year version for CMU SCS undergraduates
